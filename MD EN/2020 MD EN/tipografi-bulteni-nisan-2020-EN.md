### A monthly Newsletter featuring noteworthy news in typography.

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Community’s April 2020 bulletin:

## A useful app from Gábor Kerekes: GlyphCollector

*Image/Video: [© [GlyphCollector](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eG9wsK8WNs4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eG9wsK8WNs4)*

[GlyphCollector](https://glyphcollector.app), developed by [Gábor Kerekes](https://twitter.com/krks_gbr), has been announced. It allows you to collect all instances of a glyph found in a document and generate their averages. Usable for both research and production, the desktop application GlyphCollector is ideal for revival projects.

---

## Personal typefaces can be added to Creative Cloud.

*Image/Video: [© [Adobe.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NOgkkFZNo0&)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NOgkkFZNo0&)*

Adobe [announced](https://theblog.adobe.com/keep-brand-consistency-with-add-fonts-to-creative-cloud/) that it is allowing Creative Cloud users to upload typefaces from their own archives so they can sync them across multiple devices. Whether the licenses of the typefaces in their archives permit such use is left to the users.

---

## Browser-based font matching program: FontMatch

*Image: © [FontMatch.](https://font-match.netlify.com)*

Developed by [Markos Konstantopoulos](https://markoskon.com), the browser-based [Font Match](https://font-match.netlify.com) allows you to compare fonts. Using [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com)’ database, FontMatch can present anatomical differences by overlaying two selected fonts or placing them on the same line.

## Klint Finley: “Open-licensed fonts are love letters written to the design community.”

*Image: © [Elena Lacey.](https://www.wired.com/story/open-source-fonts-love-letters-design-community/)*

An [article](https://www.wired.com/story/open-source-fonts-love-letters-design-community/) by [Klint Finley](https://twitter.com/klintron) from [WIRED](https://www.wired.com) on the rise of the open-licensed font movement. Finley notes that freely usable and modifiable fonts give others the chance to improve their craft and share feedback. 🇬🇧

## A new type family from Ceyhun Birinci: Habanera

*Image: © [MyFonts.](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/artegra/habanera)*

[Ceyhun Birinci](https://www.behance.net/ceyhunbirinci)’s latest type family [Habanera](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/artegra/habanera) has gone on sale on MyFonts. Consisting of seven weights in regular and rounded versions, totaling twenty-eight fonts, the geometric sans-serif family Habanera is available with a 70% introductory discount.

## A variable font for Türkiye İş Bankası museums: Müze Mono

*Image/Video: [© [Müze Mono.](https://vimeo.com/404005602)](https://vimeo.com/404005602)*

A variable font named [Müze Mono](https://bit.ly/2xZXaWt) was designed to be used in signage and exhibitions at Türkiye İş Bankası museums. Designed by [Emre Senan](http://www.emresenan.com) and developed by [Onur Yazıcıgil](http://www.onuryazicigil.com), [Müze Mono](https://bit.ly/2xZXaWt)’s variability feature not only allows the designer to access a rich list of options for each letter, but also enables most glyphs to be altered in four cardinal directions.

Don’t miss the [article](https://medium.com/tipografi/müze-mono-değişken-font-projesi-6a8929772e07) where Emre Senan and Onur Yazıcıgil share the details of this variable font project.

[Müze Mono: Variable Font Project — *Emre Senan and Onur Yazıcıgil*](https://medium.com/tipografi/m%C3%BCze-mono-de%C4%9Fi%C5%9Fken-font-projesi-6a8929772e07)

## Glyph search tool: Glyphfinder

*Image: © [Glyphfinder](https://www.glyphfinder.com)*

Developed by [Philipp Kühn](http://www.philipp-kuehn.com) for designers, developers, and writers, [Glyphfinder](https://www.glyphfinder.com), which makes searching for glyphs easy, has been released. Glyphfinder, which allows you to search among 34,306 glyphs in its database and can also work without an internet connection, is priced at $6.99.

---

## Underware shared the PDF version of the publication titled 26 due to quarantine.

*Image: © [26](https://underware.nl/publications/26/)*

Dutch type foundry [Underware](https://underware.nl/site2/index.php) [shared](https://underware.nl/publications/26/) the PDF version of their publication titled *26*, prepared for [Gerard Unger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Unger), due to quarantine. An elaborated version of Underware’s presentation given during the book launch of Gerard Unger’s [Theory of Type Design](https://www.amazon.com/Theory-Type-Design-Gerard-Unger/dp/9462084408) at Het Nieuwe Instituut in Rotterdam on September 6, 2018, the publication was released on the occasion of a one-day symposium organized by Mathieu Lommen at the University of Amsterdam’s Allard Pierson Institute on October 3, 2019. 🇬🇧

## Letterform Archive opened its collection to online access.

*Image: © [Letterform Archive](http://oa.letterformarchive.org)*

[Letterform Archive](https://letterformarchive.org) has opened its collection to [online access](http://oa.letterformarchive.org). Allowing searches by categories such as person, year, and format, the archive contains approximately 9,000 high-resolution images and 1,500 objects.

*Image: © [The Type Foundry Directory](https://typefoundry.directory)*
