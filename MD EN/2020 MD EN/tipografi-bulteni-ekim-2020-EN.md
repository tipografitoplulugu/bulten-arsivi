## Futura has been updated.

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmOGDAFcEqc)*

Monotype released [Futura Now](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/futura-now), the most comprehensive update to date of Futura, which was designed by Paul Renner in 1927. [Steve Matteson](https://twitter.com/SteveMatteson1), who leads Monotype’s design team, states that Futura, which has become a typographic icon, brings back much-needed humanism to the world of geometric sans-serifs. With Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic scripts, Futura Now offers designers limitless variety with OpenType features and variable fonts.

An important [exhibition](http://www.zeytinburnuhaber.org/kultur-sanat/18438-kazlicesmede-sanatta-ebuzziya-ailesi.html) has opened at the Kazlıçeşme Sanat Center by the Zeytinburnu Municipality. ## Ed Benguiat passed away.

*Image: Ed Benguiat (1927–2020)*

American type designer [Ed Benguiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Benguiat), who created more than 600 typefaces including Tiffany, Bookman, Panache, Souvenir, as well as Benguiat and Benguiat Gothic bearing his own surname, has passed away.

Following Benguiat’s passing, Steven Heller penned an article titled [The Benguiat Period](https://www.printmag.com/post/the-daily-heller-the-benguiat-period), expressing his admiration for Benguiat. The Type Directors Club re-shared the video recordings of their [interview](https://youtu.be/KHjdoSP-jVc) with Benguiat conducted in 2016 as part of the *Legends of Typography* series and the [interview](https://vimeo.com/225122829) conducted by Debbie Millman in 2017. Neil Genzlinger paid tribute to Benguiat, who redesigned The New York Times logo, with an [article](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/10/16/business/media/ed-benguiat-dead.html) published by the paper.

## Custom typeface for UK stations from Margaret Calvert

*Image: © [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2020/10/22/rail-alphabet-2-margaret-calvert-typeface-uk-stations/)*

British graphic designer [Margaret Calvert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Calvert) [designed](https://www.dezeen.com/2020/10/22/rail-alphabet-2-margaret-calvert-typeface-uk-stations/) a typeface called *Rail Alphabet 2* for use on station signage across the United Kingdom. Rail Alphabet 2, an updated version of the Rail Alphabet designed by Calvert with Jock Kinneir in the 1960s, was designed in collaboration with Henrik Kubel from [A2-TYPE](https://www.a2-type.co.uk/about). 🇬🇧
