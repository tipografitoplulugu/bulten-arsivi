# Typography Newsletter: September 2022

### A monthly digest of notable developments in the world of typography

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Community’s September 2022 bulletin:

---

## The fourth edition of Spiekermann’s book is on sale!

*Image: © [p98a](https://shop.p98a.com/products/p98a-find-out-how-type-works-by-erik-spiekermann-4th-edition)*

The fourth edition of Erik Spiekermann’s book titled “[Find Out How Type Works](https://shop.p98a.com/products/p98a-find-out-how-type-works-by-erik-spiekermann-4th-edition)” has been published. In the fourth edition published by [TOC](https://www.toc.berlin/), Spiekermann updates his book with a new chapter on variable fonts, numerous visual examples of how to communicate effectively with type, and new typefaces. Spiekermann’s engaging style helps you look at type, work with type, select the best typeface for your message, and express yourself more effectively through design. 🇬🇧

## Efforts to modernize the Ukrainian alphabet

*Image: © [alphabettes.org](https://www.alphabettes.org/abetki/)*

Ukrainian type designers [Victoria and Vitalina Lopukhina](http://vikavita.com) [shared](https://www.alphabettes.org/abetki/) details from their long-standing work focused on history to distinguish the alphabet used by Ukrainians, known as *Abetka*, from the Russian alphabet. 🇬🇧

## Eight TV shows and movies with a spectacular use of typography

## The first conference on Native North American typography: Ezhishin

*Image: © [TDC](https://www.tdc.org/news/tdc-presents-ezhishin-the-first-ever-conference-on-native-north-american-typography/)*

The Type Directors Club (TDC) is organizing a conference on North American typography called [Ezhishin](https://www.tdc.org/news/tdc-presents-ezhishin-the-first-ever-conference-on-native-north-american-typography/). Featuring presentations, discussions, and workshops to foster dialogue between Native and non-Native communities, the online [conference](https://www.tdc.org/news/tdc-presents-ezhishin-the-first-ever-conference-on-native-north-american-typography/) includes more than a dozen panels and presentations on November 11–12, 2022, and workshops to be held on November 13. 🇬🇧

## ISType is being held on November 5–7.

*Image: © [ISType](https://istype.com/2022/)*

The ISType Arabic Typography Conference will be held on November 5–7, 2022. Focusing on the presentation of the book “[Arabic Typography: History and Practice](https://www.amazon.com/Arabic-Typography-Practice-Titus-Nemeth/dp/3721210174)” published this past July, [ISType Mukaddeme](https://istype.com/2022/) is hosted by the Sakıp Sabancı Museum. Featuring speakers who bring together design, research, and interdisciplinary practices such as Gerry Leonidas, Titus Nemeth, Orlin Sabev, and Borna Izadpanah, the conference brings together historical research on Arabic typography and contemporary design. Following the one-day conference, an Arabic type design workshop led by Borna Izadpanah and Rainer Erich Scheichelbauer will take place on November 6–7, 2022.

## Inscript Experimental Type Festival

*Image: © [Inscript Experimental Type Festival](https://inscript.tf/)*

## The Museum of Words opened in Ankara.

*Image: © [Kelime Müzesi](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/kultur/turkiyenin-ilk-kelime-muzesi-ziyaretcilerini-bekliyor/2695301)*

Founded in Ankara by author Şermin Yaşar to teach children and young people the meanings of Turkish words, proverbs, and idioms, Turkey’s first “Museum of Words” has [opened](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/kultur/turkiyenin-ilk-kelime-muzesi-ziyaretcilerini-bekliyor/2695301) its doors.

## History of Orthography and Punctuation in Turkey Turkish

*Image: © [Nobel Publishing](https://www.nobelyayin.com/turk-dil-kurumu-ve-dil-dernegi-yazim-kilavuzlari-cercevesinde-turkiye-turkcesi-yazim-ve-noktalama-tarihi-18686.html)*

The new book “[History of Orthography and Punctuation in Turkey Turkish](https://www.nobelyayin.com/turk-dil-kurumu-ve-dil-dernegi-yazim-kilavuzlari-cercevesinde-turkiye-turkcesi-yazim-ve-noktalama-tarihi-18686.html)” by Assoc. Prof. Dr. [Sedat Balyemez](https://twitter.com/SedatBalyemez1) from Bartın University has been published. In his book, Balyemez addresses what has changed in spelling rules and punctuation marks since the earliest style guides of the TDK and Dil Derneği, as well as the differences between the current rules of the TDK and Dil Derneği. [To examine the book’s table of contents](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iO0-nPnmqGmiLe5nRCJri-L1t0q_7_kv/view) ➚
