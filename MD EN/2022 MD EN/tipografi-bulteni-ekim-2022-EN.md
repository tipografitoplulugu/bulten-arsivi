## New typeface for Braun

*Image: © [iconwerk](https://www.iconwerk.com/braun-font.html)*

[Iconwerk](https://www.iconwerk.com/) designed a new typeface called [Braun Linear](https://www.iconwerk.com/braun-font.html) for Braun. Designed by Stefan Dziallas with Ludwig Übele and Nina Sagra in collaboration with Oliver Grabes and Renke Thye from the Braun design office, the typeface aims to expand Braun’s communication as an elegant, practical typeface.

## Interview with font licensing expert Greg Mitchell

*Image: © [Dinamo](https://abcdinamo.com/newsletter/the-dinamo-update-18)*

Dinamo Type Foundry published an [interview](https://abcdinamo.com/newsletter/the-dinamo-update-18) with Greg Mitchell of [Font Shield](https://www.fontshield.com/), an expert on font licensing. Mitchell, who has worked in the type industry for 40 years and notes that font licenses have become increasingly complex over time with more people confused by the legalities of licensing, shares fascinating stories from his career and his views on today’s type industry in the interview. 🇬🇧

## New typeface for the London Fire Brigade

*Image: © [creativeboom.com](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/london-fire-brigade/)*

A new typeface inspired by the lettering on vintage fire engines was designed for the London Fire Brigade. Designed in collaboration between [Studio Sutherl](http://studio-sutherland.co.uk) and [The Foundry Types](https://www.thefoundrytypes.com), [Fire Brigade](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/london-fire-brigade/) is a geometric, sans-serif typeface that pays homage to the bold lettering of Victorian-era fire engines. 🇬🇧

## The 14th International Anatolian Calligraphy and Typography Event takes place November 7–11.

*Image: © [International Anatolian Calligraphy and Typography Event](https://kaligrafitipografi.anadolu.edu.tr/)*

Organized since 2009 by the Graphic Arts Department of Anadolu University's Faculty of Fine Arts, the 14th edition of the [International Anatolian Calligraphy and Typography Event](https://kaligrafitipografi.anadolu.edu.tr/) will take place between November 7–11, 2022. This year’s guest speakers and participants include Mustafa Eren, Odile Khoury, Pelin Öztürk Göçmen, Umut Altıntaş, Yunus Ak, Zeynep Özüm Ak, along with our member Murat Ertürk. The event will feature lectures on Monday, November 7, 2022, and workshops between November 8–10, 2022. In addition, the *Yunus Emre Exhibition*, held as part of the event, will be open to visitors at the Museum of Contemporary Arts between November 7–18, 2022.

## New book on Arabic typography

*Image: © [Arabic Typography History and Practice](https://www.niggli.ch/en/produkt/arabic-typography/)*

An outcome of the *TypoArabic* Research Project announced last year and concluded on June 3, 2022, the book titled “[Arabic Typography History and Practice](https://www.niggli.ch/en/produkt/arabic-typography/)” has been published by [Niggli Verlag](https://www.niggli.ch/en/). Led by Titus Nemeth with a foreword by Robert Bringhurst, and featuring contributions from figures such as Emanuela Conidi, Borna Izadpanah, Gerry Leonidas, and Onur Yazıcıgil from Turkey, this 416-page book offers comprehensive content on Arabic typography. The first section brings together a group of young scholars researching the subject, providing an in-depth synthesis of the typographic history and development of Arabic script. In the second section, these historical foundations are complemented by a concise guide to Arabic typography through the lens of a designer. Bonus: [Titus Nemeth’s article on the book](https://research.reading.ac.uk/typoarabic/arabic-typography-history-and-practice-is-out-now/) ➚

## On Arabic typography with Nadine Chahine

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://youtu.be/rwIbkOs2LkI)*

> “There were only three good Arabic typefaces”

Oliver Schöndorfer published his conversation with Nadine Chahine on Arabic typography and type design in two parts on his [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/PimpmyType). In the [first part](https://youtu.be/rwIbkOs2LkI) of the conversation, Chahine explores why the Arabic script was neglected for so long and shares the state of Arabic type design in the late 1990s. In the [second part](https://youtu.be/23nZ7Ml9ks8), she compares the state of font licensing fifteen years ago with today, explaining what makes [I Love Typography](https://fonts.ilovetypography.com/), where she serves as director, distinct. 🇬🇧
