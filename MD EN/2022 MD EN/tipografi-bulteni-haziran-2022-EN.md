## A visual story about Polish trains: Radical Passion

*Image: © [Three Dots Type](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/three-dots-type-graphic-design-310522)*

> “This book reveals the untold history of wild design, visual culture, and the community of ‘radical train lovers.’” Marian Misiak

[An article worth reading](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/three-dots-type-graphic-design-310522) about *Radical Passion*, a project examining the lettering on twentieth-century Polish trains, by Wrocław-based [Three Dots Type](https://threedotstype.com/about/), founded by Marian Misiak in 2017. 🇬🇧 The book about the EN57—designed in 1962 and the longest electric train ever produced in the world—is just one of the fascinating projects spanning a five-year process and born from the studio's interest in exploring the cultural-technical possibilities of typography… The studio also designed a typeface called [Tor Grotesk](https://threedotstype.com/#things) for the book, reviving the lettering used on the trains and pre-World War II signage.

## An interesting study on optimal line length

*Image: © [baymard.com](https://baymard.com/blog/line-length-readability)*

[Research conducted](https://baymard.com/blog/line-length-readability) on the interface designs of large-scale e-commerce websites reveals that the optimal line length for body text is between 50–75 characters, and readers faced with excessively long lines are more likely to avoid reading the text. 🇬🇧

## Can a typeface be both striking and legible?

*Image: © [medium.muz.li/](https://medium.muz.li/reciprocity-what-actually-shapes-the-spirit-of-a-typeface-1f8a69a51add)*

Type designer [Maria Doreuli](https://mariadoreuli.wordpress.com) concludes, through *Chimera* and *Beeline Sans*—two of [Contrast Type Foundry](https://contrastfoundry.com/en)’s most significant works—that the reader and the context also help shape the ultimate voice of a typeface. 🇬🇧

## Berlin’s typographic heritage is being digitized.

*Image: © [Fontstand](https://fontstand.com/news/design-news/berlins-typographic-legacy/)*

Four institutions from Berlin [have collaborated](https://fontstand.com/news/design-news/berlins-typographic-legacy/) to digitize a large portion of their collections of printed type specimens and make them accessible online for free. *Stiftung Deutsches Technikmuseum Berlin*, *Erik Spiekermann Foundation gGmbH*, *Kunstbibliothek der Staatlichen Museen zu Berlin*, and *Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin* applied for funding for a joint project. The funding was granted by the Funding Program for the Digitization of Cultural Assets of the State of Berlin (digiS). The digitization project was supported by an academic advisory committee consisting of Jürgen Franssen, Dan Reynolds, and Katharina Walter. 🇬🇧

## A new typeface from Studio Hanzer Liccini: Timezone

*Image: © [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/timezone-is-a-workhorse-of-a-font-inspired-by-the-heyday-of-digital-type-design/)*

[An article](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/timezone-is-a-workhorse-of-a-font-inspired-by-the-heyday-of-digital-type-design/) about [Timezone](https://hanli.eu/timezone/), the new typeface by [Studio Hanzer Liccini](https://hanli.eu/home/), inspired by type designs from the phototypesetting era of the 1970s and 1980s as well as early examples of digital fonts. 🇬🇧 With one of its primary sources of inspiration being [Demos](https://www.linotype.com/96889/demos-family.html), one of the first digital fonts designed by Gerard Unger in 1976, the low-contrast serif typeface Timezone offers a sturdy structure for long texts.

## The surprising psychology of fonts

*Image: © [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90757581/the-surprising-psychology-of-fonts)*

How typefaces affect emotions has not yet been extensively studied, but [a new report](https://www.fastcompany.com/90757581/the-surprising-psychology-of-fonts) confirms what was already suspected. In a study conducted with 400 participants in collaboration between Monotype and the neuroscience company [Neurons](https://www.neuronsinc.com), it was concluded that typefaces are subjective and mean different things to different people. 🇬🇧

## A new book on Eugen Gomringer’s work: “Words Form Language”

*Image: © [Slanted](https://www.slanted.de/words-form-language/)*

Edited by Simon Mager and compiling concrete poetry, typography, and the work of [Eugen Gomringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugen_Gomringer), “[Words Form Language](https://www.slanted.de/words-form-language/)” was published in English and German by Triest Verlag. Realized in collaboration between the *University of Art and Design Lausanne* ([ECAL](https://www.ecal.ch/fr/100/homepage)) and the *University of Applied Sciences and Arts Western Switzerland* ([HES-SO](https://www.hes-so.ch/en/homepage)), the book presents original visuals and selected works produced as an art director and copywriter for various companies by Bolivian-born Swiss poet, writer, and publisher Eugen Gomringer, described as the father of concrete poetry.

## A new type family from Klim Type Foundry: The Future

*Image: © [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz/blog/the-future-design-information/)*

[An article worth reading](https://klim.co.nz/blog/the-future-design-information/) about the design process of [The Future](https://klim.co.nz/collections/the-future/), the new type family by [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz), paying homage to Paul Renner’s progressive classic Futura. 🇬🇧 The Future is a careful observation of Futura’s geometric architecture and pioneering alternates, echoing Renner’s profound reflections on modernity, abstraction, and purity.

## 2nd International Lettering Culture Festival

*Image: © Kaligrafist*

First held in 2017, [Kaligrafist](http://www.kaligrafist.com.tr/index.html) is taking place on June 23–July 3, 2022, as the [2nd International Lettering Culture Festival](https://kultur.istanbul/etkinlik/kaligrafist/), hosted by the IMM Department of Cultural Affairs. Encompassing events such as conferences, exhibitions, and performances, as well as workshops for adults and children, field trips, and talks, the festival is being held in venues including IMM Taş Oda-Sultanahmet, Barın Han, IMM Neighborhood Houses (Mahalle Evleri), the Historic Peninsula Lettering Route, Karaköy Coastal Park, and Karaköy Istanbul Kitapçısı. At the festival, our founding member Ömer Durmaz gave a talk on Sait Yada, a lettering master whose works had not been brought together until now.

## A series uniting architecture and typography from Federico Babina: Archifont

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://youtu.be/PUmpWt-4_U8)*

With his [*Archifont*](https://www.designboom.com/art/federico-babinas-archifont-letters-architecture-06-22-2022/?mc_cid=6ff873765a&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) series, Federico Babina created alphabets representing twenty-eight prominent architects and designers of the twentieth century.
