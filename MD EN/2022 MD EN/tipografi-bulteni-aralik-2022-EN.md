## A new book from MuirMcNeil

*Image: © [System Process Form](https://vol.co/product/system-process-form/)*

Designed by [MuirMcNeil](https://muirmcneil.com/), the book [System Process Form](https://vol.co/product/system-process-form/), showcasing hundreds of visual examples drawn from the studio’s two type systems and printed throughout in three neon inks and metallic black, was published by Unit Editions. As the studio’s first publication, the book offers a detailed examination of two type systems—an extensive collection of geometric alphabets where every line, shape, letterform, and word is designed to collaborate. In [their interview with Steven Heller](https://www.printmag.com/daily-heller/the-daily-heller-a-newer-new-typography/), McNeil says that this book is "a potent testament to the beauty of analytical approaches to form-making for visual communication, a book that embraces both micro and macro viewpoints and can be as magnificent as its results." 🇬🇧

## A new book from the French design studio M/M (Paris): “Letters from M/M (Paris)”

*Image: © [Thames & Hudson](http://thamesandhudson.com/letters-from-m-m-paris-9780500025277)*

The new book “[Letters from M/M (Paris)](http://thamesandhudson.com/letters-from-m-m-paris-9780500025277)”, detailing the design process of ninety typefaces created since the founding of the French design studio M/M (Paris)—established by Michaël Amzalag and Mathias Augustyniak—has been published. Featuring a foreword by Björk, designed by Paul McNeil, and published by Thames & Hudson, the book invites readers into the diverse, prolific, and exuberant landscape of the studio’s typographic world, starting from a typeface they created for a zine in 1992.

**Bonus: **Sophie Tolhurst from Design Week’s [interview](https://www.designweek.co.uk/issues/5-9-december-2022/mm-paris-typographic-book/) with the book's designer, Paul McNeil** ➚**

## Key considerations in print with Diana Varma

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://youtu.be/37F15cF0bMQ)*

Oliver Schöndorfer’s [interview](https://youtu.be/37F15cF0bMQ) with Canadian print specialist and designer Diana Varma on typographical considerations in print. 🇬🇧 In the interview, Varma provides useful insights on topics such as the advantages of print over digital, the differences between legibility and readability, proper type sizes, and the smallest font size you can print. ## The archive of the Klingspor Museum is now accessible online.

*Image: © [Klingspor](https://klingspor-type-archive.de/)*

The archive of the Klingspor Museum, which houses historical materials preserved from the Klingspor Type Foundry founded by Carl Klingspor in 1892, [is now accessible](https://klingspor-type-archive.de/) online. The digitization project was carried out in collaboration between the museum and the University of Art and Design (HfG) in Offenbach.

## A fine revival example from Didem Öğmen: Wolss

*Image: © [Didem Öğmen](https://didemogmen.notion.site/About-Wolss-An-18th-century-revival-016bfc733de34c75b658dd4d1aa37543)*

Type designer [Didem Öğmen](https://didemogmen.com/) announced [Wolss](https://didemogmen.notion.site/About-Wolss-An-18th-century-revival-016bfc733de34c75b658dd4d1aa37543), revived from a type cut by 18th-century Flemish typographer *Jan Baptist van Wolsschaten* (1714–1776). Wolss reflects the findings of extensive research conducted by Öğmen on original specimens in the collection of the Plantin-Moretus Museum in Antwerp, Belgium. 🇬🇧

## On the typography of the 2022 World Cup

*Image: © [Print](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/monotypes-charles-nix-on-how-typography-is-the-underdog-of-the-2022-world-cup/)*

> “Typography is a key player in the 2022 World Cup identity. As a typographer, I think it's the captain.”

An [interview](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/monotypes-charles-nix-on-how-typography-is-the-underdog-of-the-2022-world-cup/) by Chloe Gordon from Print magazine with Monotype Creative Director Charles Nix about the typography and identity of the 2022 FIFA World Cup. Nix explains that the visual identity is much more than meets the eye, from its devotion to Arabic calligraphy to how it balances the culture surrounding a sport. 🇬🇧
