## Cyrus Highsmith updates two of his classics.

*Image: © [Type Network](https://typenetwork.com/articles/cyrus-highsmith-upgrades-two-of-his-classics)*

Cyrus Highsmith of Occupant Fonts is overhauling his popular typefaces [Dispatch and Stainless](https://occupantfonts.com/articles/design/dispatch-and-stainless-2/) after 25 years of periodic updates. Reconstructed to be technically flawless, with Cem Eskinazi also adding new weights and obliques, Dispatch 2 and Stainless 2 are ready for the next 25 years. [In the article published on Type Network](https://typenetwork.com/articles/cyrus-highsmith-upgrades-two-of-his-classics), Highsmith discusses this revision with Lucas Czarnecki and Font Engineer Guido Ferreyra. 🇬🇧

## A new microeconomy model within the type industry

*Image: © [NFTy.pe](https://www.nfty.pe/)*

Renowned design director and typographer [Craig Ward](http://wordsarepictures.co.uk/) introduces a new model with [NFTy.pe](https://www.nfty.pe/), a revolutionary platform that represents a *rethinking of the type industry from top to bottom*. Under the new model, each unique edition of each NFTypeface can legally have only one owner at a time, and this ownership is stored and verifiable on a digital ledger via blockchain. ➚ [Reading Recommendation](https://www.creativeboom.com/news/nftype/)
