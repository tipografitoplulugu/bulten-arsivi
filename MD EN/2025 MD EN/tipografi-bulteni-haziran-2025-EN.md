# Typography Bulletin: June 2025

### In this month’s bulletin, there are significant developments in typography from around the world and in Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Community’s June 2025 bulletin:

---

## Call for a “Font Revolution” to the Football Federation

*Image: © [Arialfontlaneti](https://x.com/arialfontlaneti/status/1934580786545517047)*

A campaign has been launched on social media against the Turkish Football Federation’s persistent use of the Arial typeface for kit names and numbers. Started by the account named [Arialfontlaneti](https://x.com/arialfontlaneti/status/1934580786545517047), the campaign draws attention to Arial’s aesthetic inadequacy by comparing the current kits of football clubs. Using the hashtag *#ArialOut*, the campaign demands that the federation and clubs use typefaces that are more original and reflect club identity.

## The new era of typography: Human Types

*Image: © [Human Types](https://www.monotype.com/type-trends/human-types)*

Monotype’s initiative named [Human Types](https://www.monotype.com/type-trends/human-types) aims to explore the new relationship between artificial intelligence and typography and to re-emphasize the place of human creativity in typography. Highlighting the insights, expertise, and emotions of leading creators in the type industry such as Matthieu Salvaggio of Blaze Type, Matteo Bologna of MuccaTypo, and Sina Otto of Monotype, Human Types was one of the six themes of Monotype’s report titled “[Re:Vision](https://www.monotype.com/resources/ebook/unlock-report-revision-2025)”—which we also featured in our [March bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-mart-2025-09e6ff1f9a12). *☞ *If you haven’t subscribed to the bulletin yet, you can do so [here](/tipografi/newsletters/tipografi-bülteni).

## Improve web typography with new CSS units!

*Image: © [Webkit](https://webkit.org/blog/16831/line-height-units/)*

Jen Simmons paves the way for bringing vertical rhythm and consistency to web typography by introducing the `lh` and `rlh` units added to CSS in 2023. These new units [provide](https://webkit.org/blog/16831/line-height-units/) an effective guide, especially for aligning paragraph spacing and for line-height-based typesetting.

## From paper to pixels: The evolution of reading technology

*Image: © [Microsoft Design](https://microsoft.design/articles/from-paper-to-pixels-the-evolution-of-cleartype-and-onscreen-reading/)*

Tracy Jones [recounts](https://microsoft.design/articles/from-paper-to-pixels-the-evolution-of-cleartype-and-onscreen-reading/) the history of the transition from paper to screen, from Apple’s TrueType to the vital role of the method introduced by ClearType, and the technological and cultural shift that made digital reading equivalent to paper.

> “Handwriting is redefined any time that definitions of what it is to be human change. There is a huge value on the human gesture, no matter what new technologies come through.” Sabrina Lopez

---

## New typeface with tools for handwriting instruction: Didactica

*Image: © [Print Magazine](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/didactica-is-a-typeface-with-tools-for-handwriting-instruction/)*

Buenos Aires-based designer Sabrina López [spoke to](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/didactica-is-a-typeface-with-tools-for-handwriting-instruction/) Print magazine about [Didactica](https://typesenses.com/family/didactica/), a new typeface featuring specialized tools for handwriting instruction developed in collaboration with [The Type Founders](https://thetypefounders.com/). Didactica’s design process is based on extensive research into handwriting instruction methods worldwide and draws inspiration from the pedagogical approaches of handwriting educator Tom Gourdie.

## “Cine-lettering” exhibition

*Image: © [İrem Bilgi](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKZ9WnLoKzl/)*

İrem Bilgi’s solo exhibition titled “[Cine-lettering](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKZ9WnLoKzl/)” opened at Kült Kavaklıdere in Ankara between June 13–20, 2025. The exhibition featured Bilgi’s works reinterpreting popular quotes from cinema history with hand-drawn letters and illustrative expressions.

## Gülizar Çepoğlu on the “Critical Shifts” exhibition

*Image: © [Eye](https://www.eyemagazine.com/blog/post/shifts-and-glyphs-in-istanbul) Magazine*

Gülizar Çepoğlu, [in her article published in Eye magazine](https://www.eyemagazine.com/blog/post/shifts-and-glyphs-in-istanbul), focuses on exploring the *material state of *writing in Istanbul within the context of the “Critical Shifts” exhibition, held at Barın Han in Istanbul between October 3 and November 30, 2024.

## New book about Gutenberg

*Image: © “[Johannes Gutenberg: A Biography in Books](https://reaktionbooks.co.uk/work/johannes-gutenberg)”*

Eric Marshall White’s new book “[Johannes Gutenberg: A Biography in Books](https://reaktionbooks.co.uk/work/johannes-gutenberg)” has been published by *Reaktion Books*. The book reconstructs Gutenberg’s life—shrouded in legends due to the limited and ambiguous documents surviving from the fifteenth century—through his works such as books, pamphlets, broadsides, indulgence documents, and especially the first printed Bible.

## “Sampling Type”

*Image: © [Sampling Type](https://samplingtype.xyz/)*

Consisting of a web page and risograph-printed posters, [Franzi Häußner](https://franzihaeussner.de/)’s project titled “[Sampling Type](https://samplingtype.xyz/)” questions how typefaces bearing traces of the past are reproduced while calling for a critical approach to the use of sources, presenting interviews conducted with designers in both digital and physical formats. Inspired by the concept of “sampling” in music, the project investigates creative ways to reuse typefaces. Developed as part of a master’s thesis at [Konstanz University of Applied Sciences](https://www.htwg-konstanz.de/) (HTWG), the project was exhibited at the [Werkschau](https://werkschau-konstanz.de/) in Konstanz this past February.

## Typeface inspired by Turkic inscriptions: Turk Runic

*Image: © [Turk Runic](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/turk-runic-font-turktype-foundry)*

Fuat Akdenizli announced [Turk Runic](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/turk-runic-font-turktype-foundry), a new typeface rooted in the aesthetic power of Turkic inscriptions in Central Asia. Containing 234 glyphs and catering to the regional character sets of 86 different countries alongside Turkish, the typeface was prepared with contributions from Tuğrul Mustafa Günaydın.

---

## New typeface from Erkin Karamemet: EK Ultimo

*Image: © [EK Ultimo](https://erkinkaramemet.com/ultimo/)*

Erkin Karamemet has released [EK Ultimo](https://erkinkaramemet.com/ultimo/), a new experimental typeface designed for screens. Inspired by the appearance of droplets, the typeface features strong contrast, rounded curves, and soft transitions, and is offered in three weights: Regular, Bold, and Black.
