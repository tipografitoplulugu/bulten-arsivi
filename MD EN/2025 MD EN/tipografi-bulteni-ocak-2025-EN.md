*Image: Rumeysa Zeynep Kurtuluş © [TRT World](https://www.trtworld.com/turkiye/words-fly-away-writing-remains-turkish-womans-embrace-of-calligraphy-18025840)*

## A new type family from Ahmet Altun: Ebru Sans

*Image: © [Ahmet Altun](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFaxtX7IKIL/)*

Type Designer Ahmet Altun released his new type family called [Ebru Sans](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFaxtX7IKIL/). Featuring four typefaces—Extra Condensed, Condensed, Regular, and Wide—the type family has nine weights in each typeface.

## A new typeface from Oğuzhan Cengiz: Scrifha

*Image: [Scrifha](https://www.instagram.com/p/DEF9BM5ONl8/?img_index=1) © Oğuzhan Cengiz*

Type Designer [Oğuzhan Cengiz](http://www.hurufatfont.com/) announced his new typeface [Scrifha](https://www.instagram.com/p/DEF9BM5ONl8/?img_index=1), featuring a high-contrast structure and rich OpenType features.

## Cheetos’ typeface designed with the other hand

*Image: © [Cheetos](https://www.cheetos.com/otherhand)*

Cheetos released a [commercial](https://www.cheetos.com/otherhand) based on how Cheetos lovers eat with their dominant hand and therefore have to do the rest of their tasks using their other hand. The commercial tells the design process of the typeface named “[Other Hand](https://www.cheetos.com/otherhand),” which Cheetos designers created with their other hand because one hand was covered in Cheetos.

## A new type specimen from Emigre

*Image: © [Letterform Archive](https://letterformarchive.org/shop/emigre-fonts/)*

Founded in 1984 by Rudy VanderLans and Zuzana Licko, San Francisco–based type foundry [Emigre](https://www.emigre.com/) compiled specimens of the typefaces they released between 1986 and 2024 into a 1,264-page [book](https://letterformarchive.org/shop/emigre-fonts/). They collaborated with Letterform Archive for the reprint of the book, which was first published in 2016 and had been out of print since.

## A new book from Slanted

*Image: © [Slanted](https://www.slanted.de/molecular-typography-laboratory-3/)*

The book titled “[Molecular Typography Laboratory](https://www.slanted.de/molecular-typography-laboratory-3/)” has been published, presenting Kobi Franco’s research project examining experimental typography and investigating the intersection of function and aesthetics, content and form. Based on the premise that Latin and Hebrew letters possess a molecular structure, the book—an interdisciplinary endeavor bridging the gap between design, science, and language—explores how this concept can be applied to alphabets and languages.

*Image: © [Tom Mullaney](https://x.com/tsmullaney/status/1881939272846197105/photo/1)*

The [SILICON Type Design Incubator](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc5zbtdcJDde7NrVemsEA_9TsUd9nfAUd5PST2si-KmSXyjSg/viewform) invites students, especially from digitally disadvantaged language communities, to explore typography in a supportive online program. ## Salt’s screening program

*Image: © [Salt](https://saltonline.org/en/2822)*

The screening of the 2009 film “[Typeface](https://saltonline.org/en/2822),” directed by Justine Nagan—which tells the history of movable wood type that held an important place in design and printing until the widespread use of the Macintosh—took place on Thursday, January 30, 2025, at Açık Sinema in Salt Beyoğlu. The events organized under Salt’s “[Point, Paste Up, Print!](https://saltonline.org/en/2819/point-paste-up-print)” program will conclude on February 2, 2025.
