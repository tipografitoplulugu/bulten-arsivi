## Anti-piracy campaign created with a pirated typeface!

*Image: © [Sky News](https://news.sky.com/story/you-wouldnt-steal-a-font-famous-anti-piracy-campaign-may-have-used-pirated-typeface-13357462)*

## The punctuation mark equivalent to a jazz solo: Em dash

*Image: © [microsoft.design](https://microsoft.design/articles/the-em-dash-conspiracy-how-pop-culture-declared-war-on-literatures-favorite-punctuation/?mc_cid=eb384d5312&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1)*

The em dash ([em dash](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c)) has recently been in the spotlight with two articles. In his article, Adam Cecil questions whether the use of the em dash is an indicator of AI-written text, [examining](https://www.nightwater.email/em-dash-ai/) how this perception has spread on social media. Celeste Moure, focusing on the literary history of the em dash, emphasizes its ability to reflect the flow of thought and add emotional tone, [stating](https://microsoft.design/articles/the-em-dash-conspiracy-how-pop-culture-declared-war-on-literatures-favorite-punctuation/?mc_cid=eb384d5312&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) that she finds associating this mark with artificial intelligence ironic.

## Lost Chinese typewriter entrusted to Stanford Libraries

*Image: © [news.stanford.edu](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/05/mingkwai-chinese-typewriter-prototype-stanford-libraries)*

Stanford University Libraries has added the *MingKwai* typewriter—developed in the 1940s by Chinese-born author, translator, and cultural commentator Lin Yutang, and its only known prototype—to its collection. The typewriter, whose name means *clear and fast* and is noted as the origin of Chinese computing, was the first Chinese typewriter to feature a keyboard designed to resolve the complexity of the Chinese writing system.

## New typeface from Grilli Type: GT Standard

*Image: © [Grilli Type](https://gt-standard.com/)*

Standing out with the microsites they design to promote their typefaces, Grilli Type has announced their new typeface [GT Standard](https://gt-standard.com/). ## New typeface from Element Type: Crit

*Image: © [Element Type](https://elementtype.co/prototype/)*

Founded by İbrahim Kaçtıoğlu and Doğukan Karapınar, Element Type announced [Crit](https://elementtype.co/prototype/), a new sans-serif typeface presented in the *Prototype* category, where type designs that have not yet reached their final form are temporarily published.

## New typeface from Fatih Hardal

*Image: © [Fatih Hardal](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJ043vfsPbI/)*

[Fatih Hardal](https://www.hardalstudio.com/) designed a custom typeface for [Migros Yemek](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJ043vfsPbI/) in collaboration with *Tribal Worldwide Istanbul*. Featuring six weights including italics, the typeface—which provides clarity and consistency across both digital and print applications—was designed to reflect Migros Yemek’s friendly and accessible identity.

## The typeface designed for the Finnish football league is based on supporter scarves.

*Image: © [Its Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/bond-veikkausliiga-graphic-design-project-220525)*

Undertaking the rebranding of Finland’s football league Veikkausliiga, Helsinki-based design studio [Bond](https://www.thisisbond.com/) designed a custom typeface called [Scarf Gothic](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/bond-veikkausliiga-graphic-design-project-220525), inspired by the scarves supporters carry to matches. The typeface aims to create a shared symbol uniting fans of all teams by reflecting the folds and movement of the scarves.

## Offenbach revival from Poem Editions

*Image: © [Poem Editions](https://www.poem-editions.com/products/offenbach)*

Poem Editions is offering [Offenbach Mager](https://www.poem-editions.com/products/offenbach)—a digital revival of Offenbach, which Rudolf Koch began designing in 1928, created during a one-week workshop led by Jérôme Knebusch in 2022—free of charge.

## Google’s new design language: Material 3 Expressive

*Image: © [Material 3 Expressive](https://m3.material.io/) (M3 Expressive)*

Introduced alongside Android 16, Google’s design language [Material 3 Expressive](https://m3.material.io/) (M3 Expressive) offers a simplified structure, new typefaces, scaling, and customization options under its [Typography](https://m3.material.io/styles/typography/overview) section. Also noteworthy is the [Editorial Treatments](https://m3.material.io/styles/typography/editorial-treatments) section, which adapts design principles used in traditional publishing to digital interfaces.

## “In the Footsteps of Type: Mehmet Emin Efendi and 8-Point Naskh Type”

*Image: © [FSMVÜ Faculty of Art, Design and Architecture](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJ8-m29IMVk/)*

[Onur Yazıcıgil](http://www.onuryazicigil.com/) gave a [presentation](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKPPK4_MJbU/) as part of the *TypeOn* events held at the FSMVÜ Topkapı Campus on May 26, 2025. In the presentation, he shared information on Mehmet Emin Efendi, known as the first Turkish type maker in Ottoman printing, and the 8-point Naskh type cast in Istanbul between 1870 and 1875 and used in various typographic layouts across Ottoman printing houses. The topic Yazıcıgil addressed in his presentation is examined in detail in his [article](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/2226824) published in 2022 in the journal *Sanat Tarihi Yıllığı*.
