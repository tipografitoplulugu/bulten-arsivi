## How to draw the capital Eszett (ß)?

*Image: © [Christian Thalmann](http://cinga.ch/eszett/)*

[A detailed guide](http://cinga.ch/eszett/) by Christian Thalmann on how the capital Eszett (ß) character should be drawn. 🇬🇧 A character with a long history in the German alphabet, the Eszett generally lacked an uppercase form because it never appears at the beginning of words. With the uppercase ẞ character being added to Unicode in 2008 and officially accepted into the German alphabet in 2017, resources on how to draw it began to emerge.

## Kyiv’s metro fonts

*Image: © [kyivtypefoundry.com](https://www.kyivtypefoundry.com/projects/kyiv-metro-fonts?id=0)*

In order to preserve Kyiv’s typographic heritage, the lettering found in the Kyiv Metro was transformed into new type designs through workshops held in the summer of 2023; five of the resulting works were taken up, developed, and brought to life as a type family called [KTF Metro](https://www.kyivtypefoundry.com/projects). 🇬🇧

## Amira 2, updated by Cem Eskinazi

*Image: © [Amira 2](https://occupantfonts.com/articles/don-t-forget-to-floss/)*

From [Occupant Fonts](https://occupantfonts.com/), [Cem Eskinazi](https://www.instagram.com/cemeskinazi/)—referencing calligraphic, high-contrast sans-serifs such as Hermann Zapf’s [Optima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optima) and Warren Chappell’s [Lydian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydian_(typeface)) that inspired Cyrus Highsmith to design [Amira](https://store.typenetwork.com/foundry/occupant/fonts/amira-2) nearly twenty years ago—[announced](https://occupantfonts.com/articles/don-t-forget-to-floss/) his involvement in its update. Stating that his first task was to add Cyrillic and Greek versions to the typeface, Eskinazi will enrich the character set of the typeface with various typographic features. 🇬🇧 ➚ **Reading Recommendation:** [For our interview with Cem Eskinazi](https://medium.com/tipografi/s%C3%B6yle%C5%9Fi-cem-eskinazi-a8794f1afde4)

## Type design for individuals with dyslexia from Leo Burnett Dubai

*Image: © [Its Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/maqroo-leo-burnett-omantel-dubai-graphic-design-110424)*

Leo Burnett Dubai [designed](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/maqroo-leo-burnett-omantel-dubai-graphic-design-110424) an Arabic typeface called *Maqroo* for individuals with dyslexia. Despite more than 8.4 million people in the MENA region experiencing learning difficulties, there was a lack of widely used dyslexia-friendly Arabic typefaces, prompting the agency to partner with telecommunications company Omantel in Oman to design a typeface catering to the needs of dyslexic readers. 🇬🇧

## A typeface embodying Manchester’s charm and uniqueness

*Image: © [creativeboom.com](https://www.creativeboom.com/news/f37-northern-is-launched/)*

Manchester-based [F37 Type Foundry](https://f37foundry.com/) revived the lettering on the facade of the Great Northern Warehouse and expanded the 19 letters on the building, [transforming](https://www.creativeboom.com/news/f37-northern-is-launched/) them into a typeface. 🇬🇧 Although many of Manchester’s famous red-brick buildings are no longer used for industry, they are preserved as iconic pieces of history to pass the city’s industrial typographic landscape on to the future. F37 founder Rick Banks describes the building facades as “a great time capsule and source for typographic inspiration.”

## Hanging punctuation in CSS

*Image: © [adactio.com](https://adactio.com/journal/21027)*

An [article](https://adactio.com/journal/21027) by Jeremy Keith on how to hang punctuation marks like hyphens and quotation marks outside the text boundary in CSS—known in English as *hanging punctuation*. 🇬🇧 According to Keith, “There’s no reason not to use it! A single line of CSS will clean things up for browsers that support it, and leave everything as is for browsers that don’t.” ➚ **Viewing Recommendation:** Hanging punctuation in [Adobe InDesign](https://youtu.be/wIhEKO5MFPE?si=2yJEwajCy8K1h_bB&t=558) and [Adobe Illustrator](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MIz2sKkUdpw&t=2s).
