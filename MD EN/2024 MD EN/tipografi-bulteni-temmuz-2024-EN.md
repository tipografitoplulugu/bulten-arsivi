## Custom jersey design for Turkey at the Paris Olympics

*Image: © [Adidas](https://www.adidas.com.tr/tr/team-turkiye--100.-yila-ozel-hatira-tisort/JG3507.html)*

Adidas, the official apparel supplier of Turkey for the 2024 Paris Olympic and Paralympic Games, prepared a special collection to be worn by Turkish athletes. [According to information provided by Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/spor/adidastan-paris-2024-olimpiyatlari-icin-turkiyeye-ozel-koleksiyon/3196525), the word “Türkiye” on the collection was set in a new typeface. When the jersey is examined, in addition to an overall disharmony between the letters, anatomical issues are evident in the letters Y and E. Additionally, positioning diacritics below the cap line—a practice we discussed extensively and emphasized as incorrect under the subheading “Lowercase Fetishism” in [the first part of our “Typographic Errors with Examples” series](https://medium.com/tipografi/%C3%B6rneklerle-tipografik-hatalar-b%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-1-9e4977b0188f)—is present in this example as well.

## A new typeface from Jeremy Tankard: Ravenscar

*Image: Sketches of Ravenscar © [Jeremy Tankard](https://studiotype.com/originals/ravenscar)*

We value articles that convey the processes behind type designs and frequently feature them in our bulletins. Type designer Jeremy Tankard’s [article](https://studiotype.com/originals/ravenscar) recounting the design process of his new typeface [Ravenscar](https://typography.net/fonts/ravenscar) is one of them... Designed to create detail in publishing, the slab serif Ravenscar consists of a total of thirty-four fonts across four optical sizes: *Text, Large Text, Display*, and *Fine*.

## A typeface in Caslon’s footsteps: MD Lórien

*Image: [MD Lórien](https://mass-driver.com/typefaces/md-lorien/) © Rutherford Craze*

Another design process article came from Rutherford Craze with the typeface [MD Lórien](https://mass-driver.com/typefaces/md-lorien/). ## The latest output of the Primarium project: Playwrite

*Image: © [Playwrite](https://fonts.google.com/?query=playwrite)*

In our [August](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-a%C4%9Fustos-2023-5ee8b04702ec) and [November](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-kas%C4%B1m-2023-2ba98f905d1f) 2023 bulletins, we mentioned the project [Primarium](https://primarium.info/), launched by TypeTogether to research Latin-script handwriting education in primary schools across more than forty countries and document different handwriting models. — You can subscribe [here](https://medium.com/tipografi/newsletter) so you don't miss developments in the typography world. — One of the latest outputs of this project, [Playwrite](https://fonts.google.com/?query=playwrite), has now been released for free on Google Fonts. Supporting more than one hundred and fifty Latin-based languages, including Vietnamese, each variable font in the Playwrite typeface reflects handwriting specific to a country.

## A new type family from Esintype: Anatolithic

*Image: [Anatolithic](https://esintype.com/fonts/releases/anatolithic/) © [Esintype](https://esintype.com/)*

Ali Rıza Esin, founder of [Esintype](https://esintype.com/), has announced the new type family [Anatolithic](https://esintype.com/fonts/releases/anatolithic/), inspired by letterforms from Anatolia’s multilingual past representing the roots of Indo-European languages. The type family features two typefaces: the Latin-based *Anatolithic* and *Anatolithic Base*, which contains 583 fundamental glyphs recognized as the complete repertoire of Anatolian hieroglyphs.

## A new exhibition from the Klingspor Museum

*Image: © [Klingspor Museum](https://www.offenbach.de/microsite/klingspor_museum/ausstellungen/content-ii.14-same-bold-stories.php)*

The [Klingspor Museum](https://klingspor-museum.de/) in Offenbach, Germany, is organizing an exhibition titled “[Same Bold Stories?](https://www.offenbach.de/microsite/klingspor_museum/ausstellungen/content-ii.14-same-bold-stories.php)”, which uncovers hidden narratives of women and queer individuals in type design. Open until November 24, 2024, the exhibition reveals the contributions of those long overshadowed in the traditionally male-dominated field of type design. In addition, a book featuring articles and interviews with contemporary figures in type design, set to be published on August 23, 2024, accompanies the exhibition. The book offers a deeper perspective on the theoretical aspects of the exhibited works by highlighting the ongoing dialogue in the history of type design.

## A new project from Typotheque for North American languages

*Image: © [Typotheque](https://www.typotheque.com/research/north-american-research)*

In our [January 2022 bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-ocak-2022-c3389a6d20f7), we featured Typotheque Type Foundry’s work on indigenous North American languages. The type foundry [announced](https://www.typotheque.com/research/north-american-research) that it has launched a project to provide digital language support for indigenous languages spoken in North America. By collaborating with the *Nattilik*, *Dakelh*, and *Haíɫzaqv* communities, the project aims to overcome technical issues these communities face at the Unicode Standards level, offer digital language tools and solutions free of charge, and thus ensure the correct typographic representation of these languages across all digital devices.

## A new typeface from Element Type: Tuaf

*Image: © [Element Type](https://www.futurefonts.xyz/element-type/tuaf)*

Element Type has published its new typeface [Tuaf](https://www.futurefonts.xyz/element-type/tuaf) on Future Fonts. Tuaf is the final version of Wierd, an experimental Latin and Arabic type family designed for small sizes, which started as the graduation project of foundry co-founder İbrahim Kaçtıoğlu at EsadType. In [our interview with Kaçtıoğlu in May](https://medium.com/tipografi/s%C3%B6yle%C5%9Fi-i%CC%87brahim-ka%C3%A7t%C4%B1o%C4%9Flu-181110d5158a), we discussed the design process of this type family. If you'd like to learn more about the development process of Tuaf, you can check out the [process book](https://esadtype.esad-amiens.fr/wp-content/uploads/dissertation-ibrahim-kactioglu.pdf).
