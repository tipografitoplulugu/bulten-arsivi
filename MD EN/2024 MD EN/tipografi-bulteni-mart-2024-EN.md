# Typography Bulletin: March 2024

### In this month’s bulletin, there are important developments in typography from around the world and Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~6 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights from the Turkish Typography Community’s March 2024 bulletin, featuring new typefaces, news, and developments from the world of typography:

---

## Typefaces of the Oscar nominees

*Image: © [Print](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/best-picture-nominees-as-typefaces/)*

Charlotte Beach from Print magazine [compiled](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/best-picture-nominees-as-typefaces/) the typefaces of the ten films nominated in the Best Picture category at this year’s 96th Academy Awards. 🇬🇧

## New book from Counter-Print: “Three Dimensional Type”

*Image: © [Counter-Print](https://www.counter-print.co.uk/)*

Publishing house [Counter-Print](https://www.counter-print.co.uk/) has released its new book titled “[Three Dimensional Type](https://www.counter-print.co.uk/collections/counterprinttitles/products/three-dimensional-type)”, which showcases the boundless possibilities of transforming letterforms into three-dimensional artworks. The book serves as a practical source of inspiration with striking works by designers and insightful project descriptions. 🇬🇧

---

## Müteferrika series from Ketebe Publications

*Image: © [Müteferrika Series](https://www.ketebe.com/mutefferika-kitapligi)*

As part of its [Müteferrika series](https://www.ketebe.com/mutefferika-kitapligi), Ketebe Publications has published Hans Peter Kraus’s “A Rare Book Saga: The Autobiography of Hans Peter Kraus” and Giambattista Toderini’s “The Printing Press of İbrahim Müteferrika and Turkish Printing”, both edited by Oğuz S. Başar. According to [Rıfat Bali](https://twitter.com/brbali/status/1766398673791930473), the first Turkish edition of “A Rare Book Saga”, translated from German, was published in 2005 by Müteferrika Sahaf. The original first edition was in English and published in 1978.

## Now24 starts on June 1

*Image: © [TypeParis](https://typeparis.com/now24)*

Organized for the second time this year by the TypeParis team—and shared with our readers in the [November 2023 bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-kas%C4%B1m-2023-2ba98f905d1f)—the [Now24](https://typeparis.com/now24) conference will take place on June 1. Featuring speakers this year such as Carolina Laudon, Paul Shaw, Rainer Erich Scheichelbauer, and Violaine & Jérémy, the conference brings together typography enthusiasts, graphic designers, art directors, and type designers to explore how typography makes a visible impact. 🇬🇧

## Typographics to take place on June 14–15

*Image: © [Typographics 2024](https://2024.typographics.com/)*

The website for [Typographics 2024](https://2024.typographics.com/), another major typography event taking place this year, is now live. Focusing on contemporary typography and where its future may lie, the conference—celebrating its tenth edition this year—will be held online from Cooper Union in New York on June 14–15, 2024. 🇬🇧

## The design process of OH no Type’s new typeface Forevs

*Image: © [OH no Type](https://ohnotype.co/blog/the-process-of-forevs)*

## New typeface from Zetafonts: Maligna

*Image: © [Maligna](https://zetafonts.com/maligna)*

Another new typeface is [Maligna](https://zetafonts.com/maligna) from Zetafonts. In an interview, Italian type designer Valerio Monopoli discusses the design process of Maligna, a contemporary serif. Valerio is particularly known for typefaces such as *Gatwick, Migra*, and *Räder* for Pangram Pangram, *Sapfir* and *Sagittaire* for Blaze Type, *Absans* for Collletttivo, and *Gil Modern* for Cast. 🇬🇧

## An app that lets you design type in the browser: Typlr

*Image: © [Typlr](https://typlr.app/)*

Evgeny Agasyants has developed an app called [**Typlr**](https://typlr.app/) that allows you to design type directly in the browser. Still in its testing phase, Typlr is not the first in this field. Previously, Just van Rossum and Gaëtan Baehr developed [Fontra](https://fontra.xyz/), an application that enables viewing and editing typefaces in the browser.

## An innovative way to make a typeface interactive with your hands

*Image: © [Handy Type](https://www.commarts.com/project/36290/handy-type)*

Developed by Rozi Zhu and Haocheng Zhang, [Handy Type](https://www.commarts.com/project/36290/handy-type) integrates interactive hand-tracking data into a dynamic typeface generation process. Originating from traditional type design, this interactive typeface leverages AI and machine learning techniques to allow users to generate personalized typefaces by manipulating four distinct variables—stem, weight, radius, and slant—using their hands. Handy Type is also one of the winners in the interactive category of the competition organized by Communication Arts magazine. 🇬🇧

## Monotype’s “Content Credentials” initiative

*Image: © [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/resources/expertise/generating-trust-safeguarding-authenticity-typography)*

Bryan Comeau from Monotype [announced](https://www.monotype.com/resources/expertise/generating-trust-safeguarding-authenticity-typography) that the company will adopt the “[Content Credentials](https://contentcredentials.org/)” initiative in the coming years, which is based on the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) standard that documents content provenance and editing history to help reduce the spread of misleading information online. In doing so, the company aims to strengthen the credibility of digital assets and contribute to a more trustworthy and transparent online environment by addressing the challenges of creating and trusting digital media in the era of generative AI. Reactions to Monotype’s initiative were not long in coming. In a [post](https://twitter.com/arabictype/status/1772713336183889998), Kris Sowersby of Klim Type Foundry wondered how they would realistically handle this, noting that Monotype cannot yet reliably detect pirated typefaces sold on MyFonts. Nadine Chahine [stated](https://x.com/arabictype/status/1772713336183889998?s=20) that Monotype had sent out a newsletter about Futura Now where the entire text was about Avenir, remarking that they should get their own house in order first. 🇬🇧
