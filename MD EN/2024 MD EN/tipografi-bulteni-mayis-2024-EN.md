## The rise of the typographer after World War II

*Image: © “[Mid-Century Type: Typography, Graphics, Designers](https://bookshop.org/p/books/mid-century-type-typography-graphics-designers/)”*

Charlotte Beach's [article](https://www.printmag.com/typography/mid-century-type-david-jury/) on David Jury's book titled “[Mid-Century Type: Typography, Graphics, Designers](https://bookshop.org/p/books/mid-century-type-typography-graphics-designers/)”, published in late 2023. 🇬🇧 The book presents a visual survey of how the typographer became an independent and influential participant in the rapidly evolving world of technological communication after World War II, between 1945 and 1965.

## What do font licenses mean?

*Image: © [typeheist.co](https://typeheist.co/blog/what-is-font-licensing-basics/)*

When browsing fonts, you encounter various license types such as an open font license or a desktop license. Laura Eddy [explained](https://typeheist.co/blog/what-is-font-licensing-basics/) what all the different license types mean. Another worth-reading [article](https://medium.com/@axelCorjon/when-you-like-fonts-but-forget-to-purchase-them-5c195b57d21f) on font licenses came from Axel Corjon, founder of Fonts Ninja. 🇬🇧

## InfoRiver from Taner Ardalı

*Image: © [taf-studio.com](https://www.taf-studio.com/custom-fonts/inforiver/)*

Taner Ardalı designed a typeface of the same name for [InfoRiver](https://www.taf-studio.com/custom-fonts/inforiver/), a business management and analytics product developed by Lumel Technologies. Designed for displaying numerical data, InfoRiver consists of eight styles across four weights along with obliques.

---

## Letter design with GPT-4o

*Image: © [Min Choi](https://twitter.com/minchoi/status/1790107352605053353/photo/1)*

One of the most important developments in May was the introduction of [GPT-4o](https://openai.com/index/hello-gpt-4o/). Introduced at an event organized by OpenAI and raising the bar in human-computer interaction, GPT-4o can accept any combination of text, audio, and image as input and generate a combination of text, audio, and image outputs. GPT-4o also seems poised to open a new window in type design. A user named [Min Choi](https://twitter.com/minchoi/status/1790107352605053353/photo/1) designing letters by entering prompts is intriguing…

## History of ligatures

*Image: © [Blaze Type](https://blazetype.eu/blog/history-of-ligatures-part-1-2)*

A worthwhile two-part [article](https://blazetype.eu/blog/history-of-ligatures-part-1-2) from [Sebastien Hayez](https://x.com/HayezSebastien) on the history of ligatures ([ligature](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c)). 🇬🇧

> Ligature (Bağ): The term for characters designed and tied together as a single character when certain characters appear side by side, in order to reduce distracting structural interactions between them or to add visual richness. Ligature.

## Spotify’s new typeface: Spotify Mix

*Image: © [Spotify](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-05-22/introducing-spotify-mix-our-new-and-exclusive-font/)*

Spotify refreshed its corporate typeface. Reflecting the dynamic and evolving nature of audio culture over the years in the form of a variable font ([variable font](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c)), [Spotify Mix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pU9tTd_D_eQ) was designed by the Berlin-based [Dinamo](https://abcdinamo.com/) Type Foundry. 🇬🇧

## North Yorkshire removes the apostrophe from street signs.

*Image: © [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-york-north-yorkshire-68942321)*

According to a report by Naj Modak from BBC News, North Yorkshire Council in England has [announced](https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-york-north-yorkshire-68942321) that it will remove apostrophes from street signs to avoid issues arising from their impact on geographic databases. 🇬🇧

## New book project from Elliot Jay Stocks: “Fine Specimens”

*Image: © [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/elliotjaystocks/8-faces-presents-fine-specimens)*

[In the April bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-nisan-2024-df8f79c906db), we announced Elliot Jay Stocks' book “[Universal Principles of Typography](https://elliotjaystocks.com/book)”. Announcing his new project, Stocks is raising funds on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/elliotjaystocks/8-faces-presents-fine-specimens) to create a type specimen ([type specimen](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c)) featuring contemporary typefaces from independent type foundries around the world. If you are interested in type specimens, you might also like the limited-edition [specimen](https://www.neue.shop/articles/neue-serie57-type-specimen) of 500 copies prepared by Erik Spiekermann and Alexander Roth, showcasing Neue Serie57 based on Akzidenz-Grotesk.

## New type family from Grilli Type: GT American Intl

*Image: © [GT American Intl](https://gt-america.com/)*

[Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/) announced a new type family called [GT American Intl](https://gt-america.com/), which combines the design characteristics of the 19th-century American Gothic and 20th-century European Grotesque traditions, featuring extensive language support, six widths, seven weights, and a variable font. The microsite created for the new design is also quite impressive.

## New typeface for Lamborghini from Character Type

*Image: © [Character Type](https://charactertype.com/typefaces/lamborghini/)*

Character Type [designed](https://charactertype.com/typefaces/lamborghini/) a custom typeface for Lamborghini. Designed as part of Lamborghini's rebranding led by [Strichpunkt](https://www.strichpunkt-design.de/en), the typeface features three widths, four weights, and twenty-four styles along with broad language support.

## New type family from Klim Type Foundry: American Grotesk®

*Image: © [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/american-grotesk/)*

Klim Type Foundry announced its new type family called [American Grotesk®](https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/american-grotesk/). A reinterpretation of Franklin Gothic, an important part of American type design and graphic history, the type family features extensive language support and three typefaces: *American Grotesk, American Grotesk Condensed*, and *American Grotesk Compressed*. ➚ **Reading Recommendation: [https://klim.co.nz/blog/american-grotesk-design-information/](https://klim.co.nz/blog/american-grotesk-design-information/)** The design process of American Grotesk.
