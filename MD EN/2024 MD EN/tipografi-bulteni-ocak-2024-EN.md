## Maarif: History of Turkish Education Exhibition

*Image: © Presidential National Library*

As part of the 100th Anniversary Events of the Founding of the Republic of Turkey, the “[Maarif: History of Turkish Education Exhibition](https://mk.gov.tr/icerik/detay/maarif-turk-egitim-tarihi-sergisi)” opened at the Presidential National Library Seljuk Museum and Exhibition Hall. The [exhibition](https://youtu.be/EZf9uKTCwtc?si=GhvQYu7HmdBCs2Og) brings together hundreds of works gathered from thirty different institutions and the most important documents of our *maarif* history, starting with the earliest Turkic alphabets and spanning the Seljuks, the Ottomans, and the Republican Era. The exhibition can be visited on weekdays and weekends between 9:00–18:00.

## Pioneers of book cover designers

*Image: © [CoBAC Workspace](https://youtu.be/J6pg9Y4DZdk)*

In a talk organized as part of [CoBAC Workspace](https://cobac.work/)’s exhibition “First Sight: Reading a Book by Its Cover”—which presented a compilation of book covers published in Turkey from the founding of the Republic to the present and was open between May 3–31, 2023—our founding member Ömer Durmaz and İshak Reyna [discussed](https://youtu.be/J6pg9Y4DZdk) the pioneers of book cover designers, moderated by Yalçın Armağan. Additionally, Merve Akbaş’s interview with Ömer Durmaz and Davut Yücel regarding the reflection of the growth of publishing on cover designs was published in [Litros Sanat](https://www.litrossanat.com/yayinciligin-buyumesi-kapak-tasarimlarina-yansidi/).

## A new typeface from Oğuzhan Cengiz: Ragik

*Image: © [Hurufatfont](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/hurufatfont-type-foundry)*

Type designer Oğuzhan Cengiz announced his new typeface, [Ragik](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/ragik-sans-font-hurufatfont-type-foundry). Providing high legibility with an anatomy featuring low contrast, wide apertures, and [counters](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c), the sans-serif typeface is designed with a simple and unadorned aesthetic. With rich [OpenType](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c) features, it boasts over eight hundred [glyphs](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-s%C3%B6zl%C3%BC%C4%9F%C3%BC-temel-terimler-50fb6ffeef7c), eight weights from thin to heavy, and a total of sixteen fonts including obliques…

## Visualizing the Genealogy of Ottoman Naskh Types

*Image: © [Genealogy of Ottoman Naskh Types (1729–1928)](https://manifold.press/osmanli-nesih-hurufatinin-soyagacini-gorsellestirmek)*

In their [article](https://manifold.press/osmanli-nesih-hurufatinin-soyagacini-gorsellestirmek) published in [Manifold](https://manifold.press/), Onur Yazıcıgil and Deniz Cem Önduygu discuss the development process of the data chart that visualizes the visual and production relationships—both existing and non-existing—among fifteen Naskh typefaces identified between 1729, when Naskh metal type was used in the first book printed by İbrahim Müteferrika, and the Alphabet Reform of 1928.

## Symposium on Armenians in the Early Modern Ottoman Empire

*Image: © [ISType](https://istype.com/armenian_conf)*

The [symposium](https://istype.com/armenian_conf) to be organized by ISType at Ibn Haldun University on Saturday, February 3, 2024, between 13:00–19:00, aims to bring together early-career scholars working on Armenian history in the Ottoman Empire between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries. With opening remarks by Henry Shapiro, the symposium's speakers include Fr. Levon Zekiyan, Polina Ivanova, Anna Ohanjaniyan, Cesare Santus, Erin Pinon, Onur Yazıcıgil, and Murat Cankara. The closing address will be delivered by Prof. Dr. Suraiya Faroqhi. Admission to the symposium is free of charge, and presentations will be held in English without simultaneous interpretation. 🇬🇧

## A new book from students of the Plantin Institute of Typography

*Image: © [addition-projects.com](https://addition-projects.com/)*

The book titled “[Melting Metal: Developing Typecraft](https://addition-projects.com/)”, prepared by the 2022–2023 graduates of the [Plantin Institute of Typography](https://www.plantininstitute.be/) in Antwerp, has been published. Commencing with a foreword by Dr. Frank Blokland, the book provides an overview of digital revivals produced during the academic year based on the work of four different figures involved in type production during the Renaissance and Baroque eras. 🇬🇧

## Phil Baines has passed away.

*Image: [Phil Baines](https://www.arts.ac.uk/colleges/central-saint-martins/stories/professor-phil-baines-1958-2024) (1958–2024)*

Prof. Phil Baines, a faculty member at Central Saint Martins (University of the Arts London) who made a name for himself with his work as a graphic designer, author, and educator, [has passed away](https://www.arts.ac.uk/colleges/central-saint-martins/stories/professor-phil-baines-1958-2024). After graduating from St Martin’s School of Art, Baines taught as a visiting lecturer in the 1980s and began working full-time in 1991 at what was newly renamed Central Saint Martins; he was made a professor in 2006 for his contributions to the discipline. Turning the vernacular almost into a vocation and frequently leading walking tours in London to explore local lettering, Baines co-curated the [Central Lettering Record](https://collections.arts.ac.uk/collections/clr;jsessionid=6E09E68F344CA5896CF5CC1AA078A40E) at Central Saint Martins alongside Catherine Dixon. He had also been invited to conduct a seminar and workshop at the 13th edition of the [Grafist](http://grafist.org/project/grafist-13) event, held between October 2–17, 2009.

*Image: © [ARETE](https://uclab.fh-potsdam.de/arete/en)*
