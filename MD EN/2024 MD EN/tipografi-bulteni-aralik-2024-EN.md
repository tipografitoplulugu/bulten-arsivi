## Custom type design for TikTok from Grilli Type

*Image: © [TikTok Sans](https://www.grillitype.com/commissions/tiktok-custom-font)*

There is another piece of news from Grilli Type. The type foundry [announced](https://www.grillitype.com/commissions/tiktok-custom-font) that it has completed its work on custom type design for TikTok, which began in 2022 to adapt across all media. The type family called *TikTok Sans* includes two members: *Text* and a variable font with four axes, designed to serve brand and marketing needs as well as interface design.

## Lettercarving for type designers

*Image: © “[Lettercarving for Type Designers](https://www.beeldhouwwinkel.nl/en/lettercarving-for-type-designers-francoise-berserik)”*

The new book “[Lettercarving for Type Designers](https://www.beeldhouwwinkel.nl/en/lettercarving-for-type-designers-francoise-berserik)” by Françoise Berserik, an instructor in the Type & Media master’s program in type design at the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague, has been published. It covers essentials for type designers such as carving exercises, stone selection and preparation, learning about tools, and sketching and drawing for carveable lettering.

## A new book from Alphabettes

*Image: © [Alphabettes](https://www.alphabettes.org/)*

Alphabettes announced its upcoming book titled “[Alphabettes Soup: Ten Years of Feminist Approaches](https://bikini-books.com/products/alphabettes-soup-2015-2025-ten-years-of-feminist-approaches-to-type)”, to be published in September 2025. ## A new book from Richard Ardagh: “Type Archived”

*Image: © [The Daily Heller](https://www.printmag.com/daily-heller/the-daily-heller-saving-the-printing-historys-precious-metals-and-wood/)*

Founded in London in 1992, the *Type Archive* amassed nearly eight million items over the years, but closed in 2023. Highlights of the archive included antique materials from Stephenson Blake, the type foundry active in Britain from the early nineteenth century until the 1990s; hot-metal technology from The Monotype Corporation; and innovative wood type produced at Robert DeLittle’s factory. Type Archive volunteer Richard Ardagh’s book “[Type Archived: A visual journey through typographic history](https://vol.co/collections/type-archived)” sheds light on materials significant to both the history of art and engineering. **☞ Recommended Reading: **Steven Heller’s [interview](https://www.printmag.com/daily-heller/the-daily-heller-saving-the-printing-historys-precious-metals-and-wood/) with Ardagh about the fate of the archive and the new book.

## Atlas of endangered alphabets

*Image: © “[An Atlas of Endangered Alphabets](https://www.amazon.com/Atlas-Endangered-Alphabets-Tim-Brookes/dp/1529408245)”*

Tim Brookes’s book “[An Atlas of Endangered Alphabets](https://www.amazon.com/Atlas-Endangered-Alphabets-Tim-Brookes/dp/1529408245)”, offering a global exploration of endangered writing systems and the stories and cultures they carry, has been published by Mobius. The book is an output of the *Endangered Alphabets* project initiated by Tim Brookes to help preserve and revitalize traditional writing systems—which we also featured in our [November 2023 bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-kas%C4%B1m-2023-2ba98f905d1f). **☞ Recommended Watching: **The [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LEvAiiGJqNU) with Tim Brookes about the book.

## Monotype acquired Extensis.

*Image: © [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/monotype-announces-acquisition-extensis)*

Monotype [announced](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/monotype-announces-acquisition-extensis) that it has acquired [Extensis](https://www.extensis.com/), which has been providing solutions in font management for over thirty years. This acquisition brings together Monotype typefaces and the Extensis Connect font management software to create an all-in-one inventory and font software solution for Monotype’s library of over 150,000 fonts, offering a holistic solution. **☞ Recommended Reading: **Our article titled “[Monopolization in the Type Industry](https://medium.com/tipografi/yaz%C4%B1-end%C3%BCstrisinde-tekelle%C5%9Fme-f100fe18760b)”, discussing Monotype’s acquisitions.
