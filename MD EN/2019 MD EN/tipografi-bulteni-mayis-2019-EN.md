## Are the inconsistent terminal angles of Helvetica Now a design flaw?

An [interesting discussion](http://bit.ly/2H6RFrh) took place on TypeDrawers following Mehmet Gözetlik’s question about whether the inconsistent terminal angles in Helvetica Now, the revised version of Helvetica, are a design flaw. Gözetlik had previously shared his views on the renewed Helvetica on his Medium page with an article titled [“‘Helvetica Now’ is better than ‘Helvetica Never.’”](https://medium.com/antrepo/helvetica-şimdi-helvetica-asla-dan-iyidir-bcd2185149a?fbclid=IwAR2qul0sKsw8UOB-hLpvWvV9RP8-5xVFZ4u8YEU6IL7M88fYVB5lsSvbH_g).

## A tribute to Cooper Black and ITC American Typewriter: Doyle

*Image: [Sharp Type](https://www.facebook.com/sharptypeco/?__tn__=K-R&eid=ARAdv8T3VptOOAmDC8hZLxYIlko2uZshC5TDX7irBfeX76mApcB86Bu9RDW3681C9AuLPhSJS-6GGYsT&fref=mentions&__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARAypl9Yi9L3EYi6Xg4ryHL_cMM9QsqlJfDsZYN_hxLduBNSiqgvO5KUNcfYbkUNDJly9sw-XamZOt1EwuY3l7-e-1HaQsy4bM2l2xVnzcAN_iNAPTKl3Xq4L7NYlTjhdtbVCkxX7n0cFm7JqY36eo5L6RZd8vlJCHvabnOCTy8Qtfq29nHexMk41rFP8qqvJZLI9ud66hoQnzymZ9yQN90RzdxCsXUnJkc5kW9RCPVq3bpcZVCxWsZrOrWbxYLao_DYz-ISbWjdX7-KeTyKgIgaqPZ_Iquy3PCx_AdwHmuFUdFo1yddw9wIQJ-rChPSq5Le1oUHmfHbrKYpgbiVPUzOGw)’s new typeface Doyle*

New York-based type foundry [Sharp Type](https://www.facebook.com/sharptypeco/?__tn__=K-R&eid=ARAdv8T3VptOOAmDC8hZLxYIlko2uZshC5TDX7irBfeX76mApcB86Bu9RDW3681C9AuLPhSJS-6GGYsT&fref=mentions&__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARAypl9Yi9L3EYi6Xg4ryHL_cMM9QsqlJfDsZYN_hxLduBNSiqgvO5KUNcfYbkUNDJly9sw-XamZOt1EwuY3l7-e-1HaQsy4bM2l2xVnzcAN_iNAPTKl3Xq4L7NYlTjhdtbVCkxX7n0cFm7JqY36eo5L6RZd8vlJCHvabnOCTy8Qtfq29nHexMk41rFP8qqvJZLI9ud66hoQnzymZ9yQN90RzdxCsXUnJkc5kW9RCPVq3bpcZVCxWsZrOrWbxYLao_DYz-ISbWjdX7-KeTyKgIgaqPZ_Iquy3PCx_AdwHmuFUdFo1yddw9wIQJ-rChPSq5Le1oUHmfHbrKYpgbiVPUzOGw) has shown its appreciation for Cooper Black and ITC American Typewriter with its new typeface design, [Doyle](https://sharptype.co/typefaces/Doyle/). Designed in 2019 by Lucas Sharp and Marc Rouault, Doyle features a total of ten fonts across five weights.

## A typeface designed in collaboration with Monotype: Alibaba Sans

*Image: [Alibaba Sans](http://bit.ly/2H8hy8D), designed by Monotype*

Chinese e-commerce giant Alibaba has designed a custom typeface in collaboration with [Monotype](https://www.facebook.com/ByMonotype/?__tn__=K-R&eid=ARDScwxfdxoh003ReF1MB6M6EjGMrlOr01DJA4wstFioSMFjrjWr_JBFyyvo800kQq4zNAE4qvjEsapQ&fref=mentions&__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARDVmCZkvhKslWLfmeLEAUHtWgXZ0Bu7-cepsLkOFfZ5VKo_Y8KkbSPYUSpzlKB675XXd6e9nmA4jQfEMoZ58Qx7sI8Q91YEkQpvcd5jGC4J-Is8W6iUAUBI64WZww2Bxf6XJCFtSTHCji7J7tgJcLSFYDfKskifr8ZFTEszqAdFeNSSdZtWWNioVZPjZ1N3jZ-A3y5Q_llTRHGxpHgwGIB6cGcGLF2MTSpGAIysKKNcz2ofnnD_VC5087iAwTzaD2FPgekDJJxfApm7J27qzecXJILAw6DFvu2n6MIW7pBl7h3byjDh3iLmHHUCv85bEQpWKnf1O2wPkW8tT1TpGcgqtw). Announced under the name Alibaba Sans, the typeface was first used at the company’s annual UCAN Design Conference held on April 27 in Hangzhou, China. Supporting 172 languages, [Alibaba Sans](http://bit.ly/2H8hy8D) features 11 weights.
