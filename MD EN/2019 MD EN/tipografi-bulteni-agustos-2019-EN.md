## A new typography book from John Boardley

*Image: © [bodleianshop.co.uk](https://bodleianshop.co.uk)*

[Typographic Firsts: Adventures in Early Printing](http://bit.ly/2YsQjRZ), the new book by John Boardley, editor of the website [I Love Typography](https://ilovetypography.com), has taken its place among the typography books published in 2019. Published by [Oxford University Bodleian Library Publishing](https://bodleianshop.co.uk), the book explores the influences and innovations that shaped the printed book, from how the first fonts were designed to the invention of italics.

## A custom typeface for programming: Victor Mono

*Image: © [rubjo.github.io/victor-mono/](https://rubjo.github.io/victor-mono/)*

Designed for use in programming, the typeface [Victor Mono](http://bit.ly/31mq2Bw) offers seven weights, including italic styles, with an anatomy optimized for coding.

*Image: © [ft.com](https://www.ft.com)*

In [her article](https://on.ft.com/2YLBkOh) published in the [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com), [Laura Pitel](https://twitter.com/laurapitel) leads a valuable discussion on gender distinction in language through Turkish, noting that Turkish having gender-neutral pronouns encourages more inclusive thinking.

## What role could a typeface play in the launch of Apollo 11?

*Image: © TEDx*

Douglas Thomas, author of the book [Never Use Futura](https://amzn.to/2TilIR8), shares in his [TEDx talk](http://bit.ly/2YNuFmE) Futura’s role in the launch of the Apollo 11 spacecraft and how it became one of the most widely used fonts in the world. (Eng.)

## 10 promising names in type design from the Type Directors Club

*Image: © [tdc.org](https://www.tdc.org)*

The [Type Directors Club](http://tdc.org) has [announced](http://bit.ly/2ZRfU3t) 10 designers under the age of 35 who have demonstrated remarkable achievement in the field of type design. Selected from 130 candidates across 33 countries, the designers will receive their awards designed by Paul Carlos at a ceremony in New York on August 13; their work will be exhibited from August 13 to September 13, 2019, and published in the Type Directors Club 2020 Annual.

## A new open font licensed typeface: Lexend

*Image: © [thomasjockin.github.io/lexend/](https://thomasjockin.github.io/lexend/)*

Designed by [Thomas Jockin](https://twitter.com/ThomasJockin) in collaboration with Dr. Bonnie Shaver-Troup to improve reading proficiency, the Open Font License (OFL) [Lexend](https://thomasjockin.github.io/lexend/). The version of Lexend offered in seven weights can be explored [on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/?query=lexend), and its variable font version [via its GitHub page](https://thomasjockin.github.io/lexend/).

## 5th International Poster Contest themed “Typography: A Window to the World”

*Image: © [Museum of Typography](http://www.typography-museum.gr/)*

Organized by the [Museum of Typography](http://www.typography-museum.gr/) located on the island of Crete, Greece, the [5th International Poster Contest](http://bit.ly/2PfnunS) themed *Typography: A Window to the World* accepts posters inspired by designers’ cultures, the characteristic monuments, symbols, natural environment, history, and people of their countries. The deadline for online submissions to the contest is September 30, 2019.

## IKEA switches to a new typeface.

*Image: © [Details of Ikea’s 2020 (left) and 2019 (right) catalog. [Photos: Ikea]](https://www.fastcompany.com/90393259/ikea-is-quietly-changing-its-brand-again-for-a-very-good-reason)*

IKEA, which made headlines in 2009 by changing the brand’s typeface from Futura to Verdana, [is switching to a new typeface](http://bit.ly/30GqCue). Announcing that it will use [Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Noto+Sans), designed by Google in 2016, starting in 2020, the brand’s choice is driven by universal language support, which is crucial for a global company like IKEA. Currently supporting more than 800 languages, Noto Sans contains 300,000 glyphs.

## 270 new typefaces added to the Adobe Fonts library

*Image: © [Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com)*

[Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com) has [announced](https://adobe.ly/2ZtSVPa) that it added 270 new typefaces to its library for Creative Cloud users. The vast majority of the typefaces were selected from [Canada Type](https://canadatype.com) and [Device Fonts](https://www.devicefonts.co.uk). In addition, typefaces from type foundries such as [Fontpartners](https://fontpartners.com), [JTD Type](https://jtdtype.com), [Mark Simonson](https://www.marksimonson.com), [Mostardesign](http://www.mostardesign.com), [Northern Block](https://thenorthernblock.co.uk), [OH no Type](https://ohnotype.co), [psType](https://cargocollective.com/pstype), [Sudtipos](https://www.sudtipos.com), and [XYZ Type](https://xyztype.com) were made available to users.

## A new book from Toshi Omagari: “Arcade Game Typography”

*Image: © [Arcade Game Typography](https://www.amazon.com/Arcade-Game-Typography-Pixel-Type/dp/0500021740/ref=sr_1_1?keywords=9780500021743&qid=1566989143&s=gateway&sr=8-1)*

The book titled [Arcade Game Typography](https://www.amazon.com/Arcade-Game-Typography-Pixel-Type/dp/0500021740/ref=sr_1_1?keywords=9780500021743&qid=1566989143&s=gateway&sr=8-1) by [Toshi Omagari](https://twitter.com/tosche_e), who works as a type designer at [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com), will take its place among the typography books of 2019. To be published in October by [Thames & Hudson](https://thamesandhudson.com), the book presents readers with the fascinating world of pixel typefaces in the video game world of the 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s. Gathering an eclectic range of typography from games such as Super Sprint, Marble Madness, and Space Harrier to lesser-known titles, *Arcade Game Typography* promises a nostalgia trip for gamers, designers, and illustrators.
