## Custom font for individuals with autism from Efecan Serin

Inspired by the drawings of an autistic child he met on a bus, [Efecan Serin](https://www.behance.net/efecanseri7343), who is pursuing a master's degree in graphic design and illustration at *Escola Superior de Artes e Design* (ESAD) in Portugal, developed a [custom font](http://bit.ly/2Un3VeH) for individuals with autism.

## New typeface from Bülent Yüksel: Bodrum Sans

The typeface [Bodrum Sans](http://bit.ly/2Vz0X3O), designed by [Bülent Yüksel](https://www.myfonts.com/person/Bülent_Yüksel/), is now available on MyFonts. Offering multilingual support with over 650 glyphs, Bodrum Sans has twenty weights.

## New book from TwoPoints: “On the Road to Variable”

*Image: © creativeboom.com*

Prepared by the design studio [TwoPoints.Net](http://twopoints.net/?fbclid=IwAR35JeNzQqEX1OvxejolukGq2RsaAZa6HUicTUD2Yna5i-HoBNx15HoH0YM), the book titled *On the Road to Variable — The Flexible Future of Typography* has taken its place among the typography books published in 2019. Offering a perspective on the future of typography through variable fonts, the book can be [purchased](http://bit.ly/2OXX4mL) via Victionary. For an article about the book, [see here.](http://bit.ly/2FWfXCc) (Eng.)

## Why would a government have a font designed?

*Image: © [public-sans.digital.gov](https://public-sans.digital.gov)*

An open font licensed (OFL) typeface developed on GitHub by the US government to create accessible and mobile-friendly government web pages for the American public as part of the federal government's design system: [Public Sans](http://bit.ly/2XiFP2e).

---

## Is there a tax on serif fonts?

*Image: © [CSS-Tricks](http://css-tricks.com/)*

[Chris Coyier](https://twitter.com/chriscoyier) from [CSS-Tricks](http://css-tricks.com/) wonders if there is a [serif tax](http://bit.ly/2VaHtG5) when it comes to font file sizes. Considering that fonts are also vectors, serif fonts created with more points have larger sizes. (Eng).

---

## Two new type design tools from Dinamo

Swiss type foundry [Dinamo](https://www.abcdinamo.com/front), which recently unveiled their new typeface [Whyte](https://www.abcdinamo.com/typefaces/whyte), announced the release of two new type design tools. The tools, named [Dinamo Dark Room](https://dinamodarkroom.com) and [Dinamo Pipeline](https://dinamopipeline.com) and accessible via web pages, [offer](http://bit.ly/2VYRwLx) various ways to test variable fonts.

---

## New typeface from Etcerera: Trispace

*Image: © Etcerera*

Founded in 2018 by Tyler Finck, [Etcerera Type Foundry](https://www.etc.supply/) (ETC) has released its new variable font, [Trispace](http://bit.ly/2vqVJMp). Featuring nine weights and five widths, Trispace can be obtained for free from the type foundry's website.
