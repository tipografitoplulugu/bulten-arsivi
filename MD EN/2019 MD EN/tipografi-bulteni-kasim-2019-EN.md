## New typeface from Production Type: Tesseract

*Image: © [Production Type](https://www.productiontype.com)*

Paris-based [Production Type](https://www.productiontype.com), founded by [Jean-Baptiste Levée](https://twitter.com/opto), announced [Tesseract](https://www.productiontype.com/news/new_fonts_from_production_type_tesseract), its new typeface intended for screen and text use. Tesseract consists of five weights, each also featuring an oblique version.

## New typeface from Sharp Type: Simula

*Image: © [Sharp Type](https://cdn.storefront.production.sharptype.co/documents/2019_SIMULA_NrpUvWv.pdf)*

New York-based type foundry [Sharp Type](https://sharptype.co), founded by Lucas Sharp and Chantra Malee, announced its new typeface [Simula](https://sharptype.co/typefaces/simula/). The culmination of four years of work by *Justin Sloane*, Simula draws attention with its anatomical structure in which calligraphic forms are mechanically reinterpreted.

## A review of the Decimal typeface by John Boardley

*Image: © [I Love Typography](http://bit.ly/31WroTG)*

[John Boardley](https://twitter.com/ilovetypography) has [penned a review](http://bit.ly/31WroTG) of [Decimal](https://www.typography.com/fonts/decimal/overview), the latest typeface from the renowned type foundry [Hoefler&Co](https://typography.com). ## GMK 38th Graphic Design Awards find their winners.

*Image: © [GMK](http://gmk.org.tr/news/gmkdan/38-grafik-tasarim-odulleri-sahiplerini-buldu)*

The 38th Graphic Design Exhibition, organized since 1981 by the [Turkish Society of Graphic Designers](http://gmk.org.tr) (GMK), [opened](http://gmk.org.tr/news/gmkdan/38-grafik-tasarim-odulleri-sahiplerini-buldu) on Friday, November 1, 2019, at the Tophane-i Amire Culture and Arts Center. In the competition, where 236 graphic designers submitted 676 works, Best and Achievement Awards were presented across 26 categories, along with 7 special awards.

In the Typeface Design Category, the Best Award was presented to [Erman Yılmaz](http://ermanyilmaz.com) for his typeface [Gaslamp](http://ermanyilmaz.com/portfolio/gaslmap-typeface/); and Achievement Awards were given to [Fatih Hardal](http://fatihhardal.com) for his designs [Ampersand](https://www.behance.net/gallery/79726267/Ampersand-Typeface) and [Quad](https://www.behance.net/gallery/72967269/Quad-Typeface-(2018)), to Onur Aşkın for his design Le Corbusier, and to [Oğuzhan Cengiz](https://www.behance.net/oguzhancengiz) for his designs [Apron](https://www.behance.net/gallery/77601947/Apron-Font-Family) and [Grosen](https://www.behance.net/gallery/82795717/Grosen-Type-Family).

We congratulate our award-winning designers and wish them continued success.

## New type family from Ahmet Altun: Revello

*Image: © [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/AhmetAltun/4264988-Revello-SVG-Hand-Painted-Font-Family)*

Type designer [Ahmet Altun](https://www.ahmetaltuntype.com)’s new type family [Revello](https://creativemarket.com/AhmetAltun/4264988-Revello-SVG-Hand-Painted-Font-Family) has been released on Creative Market. Consisting of two weights—decorative and bold—with compatible yet distinct forms, where all characters were hand-drawn and colored with watercolors, Revello stands out by being offered in SVG format and with OpenType features.

## Creative Cloud fonts will be available on iPad and iPhone.

*Image: © TTT*

We previously [shared](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-ekim-2019-9d95ca9dc851) that Apple introduced custom font installation features to users with iOS 13 and iPadOS. Recently, Adobe also [brought support](https://www.theverge.com/2019/11/1/20943561/apple-iphone-ios-ipados-adobe-fonts-creative-cloud-typekit) for this feature offered by Apple. Updating the Creative Cloud app for iPhone and iPad, Adobe added a total of 1,300 Open Font License (OFL) fonts to the app with this update. Fonts offered under the Open Font License are permitted for personal or commercial use. The free fonts can be used in apps that support this operating system feature.

The book titled [“Type for Type: Custom Type Solutions for Identity Design”](http://www.victionary.com/product/type-for-type/) (Type for Type: Custom type solutions for identity design) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2019. ## Variable font from Google Fonts: Recursive

*Image: © [Recursive Design](https://www.recursive.design)*

Designed by [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com) to maximize variability, control, and performance, [Recursive](https://www.recursive.design) has been announced. The five-axis variable font Recursive allows you to choose from various predefined styles or control each axis. Leveraging all the advantages of variable font technology, Recursive is also open to development via [GitHub](https://github.com/arrowtype/recursive).

## A variable font that allows a wide range of customization: Universal Sans

*Image: © [Universal Sans](https://universalsans.com/)*

[Universal Sans](http://geniş bir kişiselleştirme yelpazesine izin veren değişken yazıtipi Universal Sans), a variable font that allows a wide range of customization by reducing long lead times and high costs, has been announced. Developed by *Briton Smith* for [Family Type](https://familytype.co) as a proposed solution to the challenges designers face when searching for the right typeface and issues such as abandoning designing an ideal custom typeface for a project due to time and budget constraints, Universal Sans allows users to make modifications across four primary axes and instantly download customized modifications.

## Adobe InDesign now supports variable fonts.

*Image: © [Adobe.](https://adobe.ly/36Un6Qq)*

Recently, we announced that Adobe was working on enabling the use of variable fonts in InDesign. Finally, variable font support [has arrived](https://adobe.ly/36Un6Qq) in InDesign with the current release. In order to use variable fonts—which allow you to make adjustments to attributes such as weight, width, slant, and optical size—a convenient icon has been placed within the Control, Character, Character Style, and Paragraph Style panels in InDesign.

## New book from Peter Dawson: “Type Directory”

*Image: © Thames & Hudson*

Peter Dawson’s book titled [Type Directory](http://bit.ly/2NPloIh) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2019. Published by [Thames & Hudson](https://thamesandhudson.com) with a foreword written by [Tobias Frere-Jones](https://frerejones.com), the book categorizes over 1,800 typefaces by their classification, making it easier for readers to directly compare typefaces with a similar appearance and better understand the typeface selection process. The typefaces it covers range from classic typefaces such as Garamond, Bodoni, and Times to contemporary Bliss, Gotham, and Meta. As the ultimate visual encyclopedia of typography, [Type Directory](http://bit.ly/2NPloIh) serves as a reference book for both typography enthusiasts and experts alike.

## An interesting interview on designing the Joker movie logo with letterpress

## Letters from Sweden’s new solar eclipse-inspired typeface: Eklips

*Image: © [Letters from Sweden](https://lettersfromsweden.se/font/eklips/)*

Sweden-based type foundry [Letters from Sweden](https://lettersfromsweden.se)’s solar eclipse-inspired typeface [Eklips](https://lettersfromsweden.se/font/eklips/) has been released. Designed by [Göran Söderström](https://lettersfromsweden.se/designer/goran-soderstrom/), Eklips explores how the appearances formed by the moon just before eclipsing the sun can create letterforms. Featuring intersecting lines with an almost stencil-like appearance, Eklips comes in eight weights.

## The Atlantic renewed its visual identity using a custom typeface.

*Image: © [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/news/archive/2019/11/introducing-new-look-atlantic/601762/)*

[The Atlantic](https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&rls=en&q=atlantic&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8), which began publishing in 1857, [redesigned its look](http://bit.ly/2CYO5MN). In accordance with its new visual identity coordinated by the magazine’s creative director [Peter Mendelsund](https://www.petermendelsund.com), a custom typeface called *Atlantic Condensed* was used. Inspired by the letterforms used in the magazine’s first issue, Atlantic Condensed has a classic appearance where legibility is prioritized.

## Eco-friendly typeface from Collletttivo: Apfel Grotezk

*Image: © [Collletttivo](http://collletttivo.it)*

Type foundry [Collletttivo](http://collletttivo.it), founded by a group of Italian design students whom we introduced last February, announced their new open font licensed typeface [Apfel Grotezk](http://collletttivo.it).
