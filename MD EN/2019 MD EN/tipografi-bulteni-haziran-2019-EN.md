# Typography Newsletter: June 2019

### A monthly Newsletter featuring noteworthy news about typography.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~2 minutes

Hello everyone!

Featured news, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Community’s June 2019 bulletin:

---

## New typeface from Apple: New York

*Image: Typeface created by Apple: “New York”*

Apple [released](https://developer.apple.com/fonts/) a free and revamped version of the typeface “New York”. Featuring enhancements for ever-increasing screen resolutions, the serif typeface New York was designed 34 years ago by [Susan Kare](https://kare.com). Although New York can be downloaded for free, the license only permits its use in Apple applications.

---

## Educational License for students from Indian Type Foundry

*Image: © [indiantypefoundry.com](https://www.indiantypefoundry.com)*

Founded in 2009 by Satya Rajpurohit and Peter Bil’ak, [Indian Type Foundry](https://www.indiantypefoundry.com/) announced an [Educational License](http://bit.ly/2WzOeT7) that allows use exclusively by design students to support design education worldwide. Design students can access the fonts in the type foundry’s catalog by filling out the form in the *Trial Fonts* section.

---

## New typeface from Horizon Type: Crossten

*Image: © Myfonts*

Founded by Ufuk Aracıoğlu and Emre Güven, [Horizon Type](https://horizontype.com)’s twenty-weight typeface based on geometric forms, [Crossten](http://bit.ly/2MAZYAa), went on sale on Myfonts.

## “The world’s first variable font logo design”

*Image: “The world’s first variable font logo design” created by the [Fontsmith](https://www.facebook.com/Fontsmith-61447361061/?__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARA-0RIakF8shaRMK2XpbwLgU6ylxBh6lJntWrPP5L1VLMrnMUw0eRVYybPWwjXsITbcVtJ2W9_QdQ8x49ilB_dZAl5oet_TdVG9ylGA_hJ1KKkZD0m1tL4xKJI9gHivK_vY1Ff7_TzJBbnV-7yCp7lUd49yCUB3JAlESMgjZPQSMGYuhX54DwiCWbBvKibH7KhmD4Fqe9kbo1rgLk715yhW_d48h347DM0Ioyv5Z6qmZHhF5ypydn0peKCCfqmHrD8q_01Nwy4Ee_p2XPozT3N4B128LGot1pmzC9BH9m5GmbPH_YwpZWjG4y-wh8x128PMEcSizhL2R_VAM5cJcXCnfw&__tn__=%2CdK%2AF-R&eid=ARA9U-AK-rTb9l6z3pVsl7SHzbGgsNLcW-EIB574e3isedQyhw2q5gnHzoEpFAMDEPF7xY6vktca7vxz) Type Foundry*

The Fontsmith Type Foundry announced what it claims to be [“the world’s first variable font logo design”](http://bit.ly/2Fix16a). Created for the WPP campus in Amsterdam in collaboration with the VBAT advertising agency, the logo demonstrates the possibilities of variable font technology with an anatomy that morphs according to human behavior.

Meanwhile, speaking of which, the use of variable fonts drew attention at a concert by the *Spice Girls* as part of their world tour. In the [image](https://twitter.com/carlosdetoro/status/1140183823159832576) shared on Twitter by [Carlos de Toro](https://twitter.com/carlosdetoro), a piece created with a variable font is featured on the giant screen on stage.

## The world’s first interactive electronic Quran from DecoType

*Image/Video: [Presentation video of the Mushaf Muscat project by Thomas Milo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UpxsWGxgJIo&t=145s)*

The Sultanate of Oman [introduced](http://bit.ly/2KVDV4E) the world’s first interactive electronic Quran, developed by the Dutch company [DecoType](https://www.decotype.com). The [“Mushaf Muscat Project”](https://mushafmuscat.om/) was developed by experts from Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, and Lebanon.
