# Typography Newsletter: September 2024

### This month's bulletin features important typography developments around the world and in Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~6 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the typography world in the Turkish Typography Society’s September 2024 bulletin:

## ISType at Salt Galata on November 2–3!

Held for the ninth time this year, the [ISType conference](https://istype.com/2024/) will take place at Salt Galata between November 2–3, 2024, with the theme “Text-ure”.

As the conference's first speaker, [Tobias Frere-Jones](https://frerejones.com/) will trace his journey from high school student to professional designer and the insights gained along the way through his projects. Following Frere-Jones, [Ulrike Rausch](https://liebefonts.com/), known for her script typefaces, will demonstrate how she combines countless ligatures and alternate letterforms with modern font technologies and how she creates script typefaces. Marc Schütz, creative director of [Studio Schultzschultz](https://schultzschultz.com/), which specializes in corporate design and branding, will explore the design processes of custom type design projects combining typography and creative coding; Astrid Stavro will discuss her experience of growing up surrounded by ink and paper in a print shop and how this led to her fascination with letters and language; and [Eben Sorkin](https://www.sorkintype.com/) will share in-depth insights into the issue of optical size in typefaces. In the afternoon program, [Bartu Başaran](https://atelierbartubasaran.com/) will present on a Greek typeface revived from the monumental inscriptions of Perge, while [Albert-Jan Pool](https://www.instagram.com/albertjanpool/) will give a presentation on serendipity in type design, drawing from his encounter with the DIN 1451 typeface. [Kostas Bartsokas](https://kostasbartsokas.com/) will examine how typographic texture changes in an era where legibility rules give way to creativity in the shift from text to screen; and [Liza Enebeis](https://www.loveliza.nl/) will touch upon the boundaries of experimentation, behavior, tools, materials, and collaboration to define the essence of an identity.

On the second day of the event, [Georg Seifert](https://www.instagram.com/schriftgestalt/) will host a workshop aimed at developing participants' Glyphs skills.

## Letterpress Summit 2024

On October 6, 2024, Ladies of Letterpress and Central Print are organizing a free, one-day online event titled “[The State of the Art and Craft Letterpress Summit](https://ladiesofletterpress.com/conference/)” to discuss as a community where letterpress stands, how it has changed, and where they want it to be.

## TipoItalia 2024

[TipoItalia](https://www.tipoitalia.net/) guides the typographic research and digital revival of brush-painted signs from the streets of Venice, Renaissance inscriptions, and Tipoteca’s collection of Art Nouveau and Art Deco wood and metal type.

## Presentation on Ebüzziya Tevfik by Özgür Türesay

On September 19, 2024, Özgür Türesay gave an online presentation at the [French Institute of Anatolian Studies](https://www.ifea-istanbul.net/index.php/fr/) (IFEA) about his book on Ebüzziya Tevfik. Türesay’s book, titled “[Being an Intellectual in the Ottoman Empire: Ebüzziya Tevfik (1849–1913) and His Time](https://www.peeters-leuven.be/detail.php?search_key=9789042945128&series_number_str=28&lang=en)”, adopts a socio-historical approach to trace the biography of Tevfik, a self-taught intellectual who embraced both Ottoman and Western culture. Delving into the intellectual roots of Tevfik’s ideas and stances while analyzing the public debates this multifaceted author engaged in, the book presents a panorama of intellectual and political life in the Ottoman Empire from the 1860s to the 1910s.

## “Zevk-i Selîm Hatt-ı Halîm” Exhibition

The “Zevk-i Selîm Hatt-ı Halîm” Exhibition opened at the Rami Library on September 30, 2024. Presenting a selection of cover lettering created by Calligrapher Halim (1898–1964) for printed works, the exhibition features rare pieces born from the meeting of calligraphy and the printing press. The exhibition can be visited until November 1, 2024.

## Klingspor Museum Exhibition: “Same Bold Stories?”

In the [July bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-temmuz-2024-1e68e9d4a48c), we mentioned that the Klingspor Museum was organizing an exhibition titled “Same Bold Stories?”, uncovering the hidden narratives of women and *queer* individuals in type design. — To not miss events in the typography world, you can [subscribe](https://medium.com/tipografi/newsletter) to the bulletin for free. — [Fontstand](https://fontstand.com/news/design-news/same-bold-stories/) offers a great look for those curious about the exhibition with dozens of photographs. Highlighting the contributions of individuals long overshadowed in the traditionally male-dominated type industry, the exhibition can be visited at the museum in Offenbach, Germany, until November 24, 2024.

## “Characters: Type in Action” Exhibition

“[Characters: Type in Action](https://www.museumofdesign.org/current)”, a new exhibition by [Vocal Type](https://www.vocaltype.co/) founder Tré Seals examining the impact of typography on social justice, its history, and its role in contemporary movements, opened on September 19 at the Museum of Design Atlanta.

## Typotheque Celebrates Its 25th Anniversary.

Typotheque Type Foundry [opened](https://www.typotheque.com/blog/25th-anniversary) an exhibition in The Hague on September 21, 2024, documenting its work in the fields of type design and research to celebrate its 25th anniversary.

---

## Whitman is Updated.

Kent Lew’s popular typeface Whitman, celebrating its twenty-first anniversary and recognized with an award by the Type Directors Club in 2002, is updated under the name [Whitman Text](https://typenetwork.com/articles/A-New-Chapter-for-Whitman-Text), bringing greater versatility, refined aesthetics, and expanded language support to the typeface.

---

## 1-Pixel-Thin Font

Based on the fact that every pixel actually consists of three subpixels, Matt Sarnoff [developed](https://www.msarnoff.org/millitext/) a 1-pixel-thin font that can be viewed with a magnifying glass or a digital camera.

---

## The Typography of Books

Addressing topics such as book page layout, legibility, and the choice of typefaces used, John D. Berry [discusses](https://typofonderie.com/gazette/the-typography-of-books) in his article how modern book designs are combined with traditional arrangements and its impact on readers.

---

## New Project from Joyce Shi: “Type Something For Me”

New York-based designer [Joyce Shi](https://joyceshidesign.com/) presents her research on typography to readers through three distinct perspectives in her project “[Type Something For Me](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/with-type-something-for-me-joyce-shi-lets-us-into-her-years-long-meditation-on-typography)”: *type as an enigma, type as a way of seeing*, and *type as a reflection*.

---

## Forty Postcards from Letterform Archive’s Collection

“[Letter Love](https://draw-down.com/products/letter-love-40-postcards-from-the-collection-of-letterform-archive)”, a book featuring forty postcards from Letterform Archive’s own collection showcasing letterforms by renowned figures in the design world such as Saul Bass, Seymour Chwast, and Hermann Zapf, has been published. When the postcards printed with metallic ink are torn out, an illustrated catalog sharing reproductions and art-related details is revealed.

---

## “Dossier Jannon” on Pre-order!

The compilation book “[Dossier Jannon](https://draw-down.com/products/dossier-jannon)”, comprising documents related to the work of French printer Jean Jannon—considered one of the primary representatives of French typography in the first half of the 17th century and the first of the major typographers of the European Baroque—has been published by Draw Down Books in partnership with Klim Type Foundry and Production Type.

---

## “The Book as Object, Content, Idea, and Interface”

Amaranth Borsuk’s new book “[The Book as Object, Content, Idea, and Interface](https://www.amazon.com.tr/Kitap-Amaranth-Borsuk/dp/6259867778)”, published by *Mindset Institute*, examines the relationship between form and content in the development of the book by exploring what the book meant in the past, what it represents today, and what it might transform into in the future.

## A Magazine on Type Design: Footnotes

Published by *La Police*, a periodical featuring content on applied research in type design since 2016: [Footnotes](https://lapolice.ch/publications/).

Grilli Type designed a custom type family called *Figma Sans* for Figma, featuring a clean, utilitarian design that can adapt to the brand’s evolution. ➚ [Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/commissions/figma-custom-font) ➚ [Figma](https://www.figma.com/blog/the-story-of-creating-figma-sans/)

## Custom Typeface for FC St Pauli

German football club [FC St Pauli](https://www.fcstpauli.com/) [unveiled](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fc-st-pauli_fcsp-typografie-activity-7237798402682441729-wJnm/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios) its new typeface. Developed specifically for the club in collaboration with Christoph Koeberlin to replace Futura, *FC Sans Pauli* was designed in a backslant style as a visual symbol of the club’s ambitions.

## FontCreator Is Now on macOS!

First released in 1997, [FontCreator](https://www.high-logic.com/news/fontcreator-for-macos-released) is now available on the macOS operating system with its fifteenth version. FontCreator offers professionals and users of all levels the ability to easily design and edit type, including variable fonts.

## “Common Turkic Alphabet” Proposal

At the Common Alphabet Commission of the Turkic World meeting, organized by the Turkic Academy and the Turkish Language Association in Baku between September 9–11, 2024, and attended by officially appointed commission members from Turkic states, the Latin-based [common alphabet](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortak_T%C3%BCrk_alfabesi) project first proposed in 1991 was comprehensively reviewed, and a consensus was [reached](https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/duyurular/ortak-turk-alfabesine-iliskin-duyuru/) on a 34-letter “Common Turkic Alphabet”. Prof. Dr. Osman Mert, President of the Turkish Language Association, answered questions and shared details regarding the matter on the [Ekonomim](https://www.ekonomim.com/gundem/turkiyede-alfabe-degisecek-mi-turk-dil-kurumu-baskani-acikladi-haberi-767855) website.

## “Don’t Rely on Google Fonts for Hosting”

If you opted for Google Fonts for the typefaces used on your web page, some problems may arise. Using the recently updated Inter as an example, Oliver Schöndorfer [states](https://pimpmytype.com/google-fonts-hosting/) that caution is needed when using Google Fonts typefaces on web pages.

## New Visual Identity for PayPal from Pentagram

Pentagram [unveiled](https://www.pentagram.com/work/paypal) the new visual identity it designed for PayPal. For the renewed visual identity aimed at making financial transactions simple, seamless, and secure for everyone, a typeface named *PayPal Pro* was designed by [Lineto Type Foundry](https://lineto.com/). PayPal Pro is a customized version of LL Supreme, a modern interpretation of Futura.
