## In-house norms used by Aldus Manutius

*Image: Type Spaces*

[Hyphen Press](https://hyphenpress.co.uk/) is offering the book [Type Spaces](http://bit.ly/2JmJXfx) by Peter Burnhill, who passed away in 2007, for free to its readers.

In his book, Peter Burnhill closely examines certain books from the early sixteenth century, gathering evidence of a rational measurement system based on features of these editions such as character count, line length, and type area. According to him, [Aldus Manutius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldus_Manutius) and [Francesco Griffo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Griffo) used a series of in-house norms that could still be applied today.

## New book from Sean Adams: “The Designer’s Dictionary of Type”

*Image: © [fastcompany.com](https://www.fastcompany.com/)*

Sean Adams’s new book, [The Designer’s Dictionary of Type](https://amzn.to/2OnMNzL), has taken its place among the typography books published in 2019. Published by Abrams, the book [examines](https://www.fastcompany.com/90322896/who-was-garamond-anyway-the-people-behind-typographys-famous-names?fbclid=IwAR1YZbDXHaprBuPlq-DGc15RHHkbEYOy6LKtReHZp8RZssU-VsGAZHYsrHA) forty-eight selected typefaces, ranging from classics like Garamond and Helvetica to contemporary digital typefaces such as OCR-A and Keedy Sans.

— Sean Adams is the co-founder of the award-winning design firm AdamsMorioka and is cited as one of the forty most important people shaping international design. In addition to being a national president of AIGA, he directs the graphic design program at ArtCenter College of Design.

## New typeface from Typotheque: Ping

*Image: © typotheque.com*

Peter Bil’ak announced that they have released [Ping](http://bit.ly/2UTgNG9), a sans-serif typeface they began working on eleven years ago. Constructed with the minimum number of pen strokes and supporting 65% of the world’s languages, Ping comes in nine weights with OpenType features.
