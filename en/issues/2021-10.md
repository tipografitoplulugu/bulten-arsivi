## How does the color font LiebeHeide work?

Type designer [Ulrike Rausch](https://liebefonts.com/about) [explains](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WCUsvt4bUto) how the effect of a ballpoint pen on paper can be authentically reproduced using color font ([color font](https://www.colorfonts.wtf)) technology with the popular font [LiebeHeide](https://liebefonts.com/fonts/liebeheide). 🇬🇧 LiebeHeide was also [selected](https://www.tdc.org/news/tdc67-and-td24-judges-choice-and-student-awards-released/) in the Type Directors Club’s 24th annual type design competition this year.

## Is stretching type always wrong?

Everyone who studies typography knows that stretching type horizontally or vertically is wrong, because stretching type distorts the anatomy of glyphs meticulously defined by type designers and disrupts the carefully arranged harmony of typesetting. However, type designer [Roman Gornitsky](https://www.myfonts.com/person/Roman_Gornitsky/) [says](https://letters.temporarystate.net/entry/6/) that in some cases, breaking the delicately tuned harmony of proportional typesetting is precisely what one should look for. 🇬🇧 Gornitsky’s proposition recalls Robin Rendle’s sentence: “[challenging what we know about typography is the first step to contributing to its future.](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografinin-gelece%C4%9Fi-8c4b01c088b3)”

[READING RECOMMENDATION: “The Future of Typography” — *Robin Rendle, January 6, 2017*](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografinin-gelece%C4%9Fi-8c4b01c088b3)

## An interesting typeface from Étienne Aubert Bonn: Nurraq

Montreal-based type designer Étienne Aubert Bonn designed a typeface called [Nurraq](http://typemedia2013.com/nurraq.html), which pairs Eastern Canadian Inuktitut with the Latin alphabet. He aimed to create an intriguing context for two alphabets with vastly different natures and origins to share influences stemming from each other’s traditions. 🇬🇧

## New book from Radek Sidun: “Manual of Diacritics”

Written by Radek Sidun of [Briefcase Type Foundry](https://www.briefcasetype.com), “[Manual of Diacritics](http://manualofdiacritics.eu),” which focuses on diacritics in contemporary type design, will meet readers in November 2021. Prepared as a graduation thesis at [The Academy of Arts, Architecture & Design in Prague](https://www.umprum.cz/web/en/) and currently available for pre-order, the book serves as a comprehensive guide to diacritical marks in languages using the Latin script.

## A representative of a generation that cared for and embraced type design: Mustafa Eren

> “If the histories of designers are written correctly, design history is also written correctly.” Ömer Durmaz

Although Turkey possesses a rich cultural heritage in terms of design history, accurate examples that have materialized into biographies are rare. The [article](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/1879236) by [Dicle Yıldırım](http://dicleyildirim.fsm.edu.tr/Biyografi) from the Graphic Design Department of the Faculty of Fine Arts at Fatih Sultan Mehmet Vakıf University, which comprehensively covers Mustafa Eren’s type design and calligraphy works, focuses on going beyond Eren’s existing recognition to better introduce, commemorate, and explain him.

## Digital library from Typographica

Founded in 2002 by Stephen Coles and Caren Litherland, Typographica announced its [digital library](https://library.typographica.org) comprising resources on type and lettering. The [metal and wood type specimen books page](https://library.typographica.org/specimen-books-of-metal-wood-type), where specimens from over 200 type foundries, particularly from the 18th to the 20th centuries, have been scanned and made available online, is well worth exploring! Those interested in historical type specimens can also check out the [digital library](http://jacques-andre.fr/faqtypo/BiViTy/) prepared by Jacques André. 🇬🇧

## A guide to diacritics for Central European languages from Insects Project

Following Radek Sidun’s book “[Manual of Diacritics](http://manualofdiacritics.eu),” another book on diacritics came from the [Insects Project](http://theinsectsproject.eu) as a product of research aimed at sharing knowledge about Central European typography. Aiming to meet the needs of designers designing type for Czech, Hungarian, Polish, and Slovak, containing four essays written by invited experts, and available for free download from the website, the book provides a non-historical overview and guide to good diacritical practice in specific languages. Written by Palo Bálik, Filip Blažek, and Robert Kravjanszki, the book was edited by Agnieszka Małecka and Zofia Oslislo.

## A variable font drawing attention to the climate crisis: “Climate Crisis”

The variable font [Climate Crisis](https://kampanjat.hs.fi/climatefont/) was commissioned by the Nordic region’s largest newspaper, [Helsingin Sanomat](https://www.hs.fi), to help people see the urgency of climate change more concretely. Available as a free download, the font is based on Arctic sea ice data from the [National Snow and Ice Data Center](https://nsidc.org) between 1979 and 2019, as well as forecasts up to 2050 from the [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/srocc/). Featuring OpenType capabilities, this variable font offers a collection of weights ranging from bold to light representing the years 1979, 1990, 2000, 2010, 2019, 2030, 2040, and 2050. In this way, as the years go by, the impact of climate change can be observed through the variable font.

## PampaType joins Type Network.

Founded in 2001 by Alejandro Lo Celso as South America’s first type foundry, [PampaType](https://pampatype.com) [joined](https://pampatype.typenetwork.com/news/article/pampatype-joins-tn) Type Network, which brings together independent type designers and type foundries from all over the world. Type Network represents a new model for type design, development, and licensing, created in response to the increasingly complex needs of type designers and type users. PampaType will be featured on Type Network with four typefaces: [Amster](https://store.typenetwork.com/foundry/PampaType/fonts/amster), [Atahualpa](https://store.typenetwork.com/foundry/PampaType/fonts/atahualpa), [Margarita](https://store.typenetwork.com/foundry/PampaType/fonts/margarita), and [Amster Versal Iluminada](https://store.typenetwork.com/foundry/PampaType/fonts/amster-versal-iluminada).
