# Typography Newsletter: December 2023

### In this month's bulletin, there are significant developments in typography from around the world and Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s December 2023 bulletin:

---

## Jan Middendorp has passed away.

*Image: Jan Middendorp (1956–2023)*

Dutch author, typographer, and designer Jan Middendorp (1956–2023) has passed away. Middendorp initially began writing on theater and the performing arts, and later specialized in type design. Known for milestone publications such as *Dutch Type* and *Shaping Text*, Middendorp had been undergoing medical treatment for some time.

---

## Typography: History and technology

*Image: © [Erik Spiekermann](https://medium.com/@espiekermann/typography-history-technology-40856c9b4bc8)*

> “Before the invention of writing, humans lived in an acoustic world, left to their dark thoughts. Humanity was only freed from its ignorance with the invention of writing.”

Erik Spiekermann’s comprehensive and masterfully updated [article](https://medium.com/@espiekermann/typography-history-technology-40856c9b4bc8) on the Eurocentric history and technology of typography. 🇬🇧 Additionally, Spiekermann announced the launch of his website [spiekileaks.com](https://www.spiekileaks.com/) to provide a platform for all his writings, including notes, articles, prefaces, and opinions.

---

## The printed book in the 19th-century Ottoman world

*Image: © [The Printed Book in the 19th-Century Ottoman World Conference](https://tarihvakfi.org.tr/etkinlikler/etkinlik/19-yuzyil-osmanli-dunyasinda-matbu-kitap-konferansi/)*

Jointly organized by Tarih Vakfı (The History Foundation), Toplumsal Tarih Akademi, and ANAMED, [The Printed Book in the 19th-Century Ottoman World Conference](https://tarihvakfi.org.tr/etkinlikler/etkinlik/19-yuzyil-osmanli-dunyasinda-matbu-kitap-konferansi/) was held between December 14–15, 2023, at the ANAMED Library. During the two-day conference, Onur Yazıcıgil also delivered a presentation titled “Beyond Istanbul: The Use of Ottoman Printing Types Outside Istanbul”.

---

## On Indigenous North American typography

*Image: © [Typotheque](https://www.typotheque.com/books/indigenous-north-american-type)*

Typotheque has published a [book](https://www.typotheque.com/books/indigenous-north-american-type) presenting the typography and typographic preferences of Indigenous North American languages, resulting from ongoing research and collaboration with local language communities. 🇬🇧

## 2024 International Typography Awards

*Image: © [ISTD](https://istdawards.com/)*

Submissions have opened for the [2024 International Typography Awards](https://istdawards.com/), organized by the [International Society of Typographic Designers](https://www.istd.org.uk/) (ISTD).

## GMK Graphic Design Awards

*Image: © [Yaprak Buse Çağlar](https://www.instagram.com/typolea/)*

Organized for the forty-second time this year by the Turkish Society of Graphic Designers (GMK) as part of the *Graphic Design Exhibition*, the [Graphic Design Awards](https://gmk.org.tr/news/gmkdan/yilin-grafik-tasarim-isleri-bu-sergide-42-grafik-tasarim-sergisi-5-ocaka-dek-ziyaretcilerini-bekliyor) have found their recipients. Selected from 450 submitted designs, awards were presented across 25 design categories alongside 6 special awards. In the type design category, the award was presented to [Yaprak Buse Çağlar](https://www.instagram.com/typolea/)’s typeface *Stralic*. The Graphic Design Exhibition can be visited until January 5, 2024, at the Marmara University Faculty of Fine Arts Main Gallery.

## Five tips for optimizing the typeface design workflow

*Image: © [TYPE01](https://type-01.com/5-top-tips-for-optimising-the-typeface-design-workflow/)*

An [article](https://type-01.com/5-top-tips-for-optimising-the-typeface-design-workflow/) by type designer [Diana Ovezea](https://www.instagram.com/dianaovezea/) focusing on the process of designing a typeface from scratch, setting up your project, and identifying issues to help sustain your workflow so you can work faster and with fewer errors. 🇬🇧

## Colophon Type Foundry joins the Monotype family.

*Image: © [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/acclaimed-colophon-foundry-joins-monotype-family)*

Founded in 2009 by Anthony Sheret and Edd Harrington, London-based [Colophon Type Foundry](https://www.colophon-foundry.org/) [joins](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/acclaimed-colophon-foundry-joins-monotype-family) the Monotype family with its library consisting of eighty-five type families. 🇬🇧

Communication Arts announced the [14th Typography Annual](https://www.commarts.com/gallery?d=typography&y=2024), featuring highlights in typography. 🇬🇧

## Exhibition of the Üsküdar Printing House on Its 220th Anniversary

*Image: © [Haber1](https://www.haber1.com/guncel/220-yilinda-uskudar-matbaasi-sergisi-sanatseverlerle-bulustu/)*

Established in Üsküdar in 1803 and holding a special place in our publishing history—from the first atlas to medical books, religious publications to specialized printing techniques—the [exhibition](https://twitter.com/CarsUskudar/status/1739739667912618014) organized for the 220th anniversary of the Üsküdar Printing House opened on Friday, December 29, 2023. The exhibition can be viewed until February 20, 2024, at the Mimar Sinan Gallery adjacent to the Üsküdar Second-hand Book Bazaar.
