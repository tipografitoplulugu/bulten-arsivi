## About the paragraph mark

*Image: © [typography.com](https://www.typography.com/blog/pilcrow-capitulum)*

Commonly thought of as a reversed letter P, the paragraph mark (pilcrow) actually evolved from the letter C. Type Designer Jonathan Hoefler [wrote](https://www.typography.com/blog/pilcrow-capitulum) an article worth reading about the paragraph mark, which, like most punctuation marks, has an intriguing history. 🇬🇧

## AI handwriting tool: Calligrapher

*Image: © [calligrapher.ai](https://www.calligrapher.ai/)*

A web tool called [Calligrapher](https://www.calligrapher.ai/) that allows you to generate handwriting using an AI infrastructure. Although it still has issues with Turkish characters, it also has impressive aspects, such as the AI generating different results when two of the same character are side by side. The generated handwriting can be downloaded in SVG format.

## What will happen to the typefaces we own after we die?

*Image: © [Pixabay](https://www.pexels.com/tr-tr/fotograf/scrabble-harf-parcalari-yigini-278888/)*

On [TypeDrawers](https://typedrawers.com/), Miles Newlyn started an [interesting discussion](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/4590/what-to-do-about-our-fonts-after-we-die) about what will happen to the typefaces we own after we die. Noting that a collection of good typefaces remains a valuable part of an estate after its owner passes away, Newlyn says that [The Type Founders](https://thetypefounders.com/#about) is eager to make deals on this matter. 🇬🇧

## Proposal to include an ancient Anatolian script in Unicode

*Image: © [Anshuman Pandey](https://twitter.com/anshumanpandey_/status/1583667570493640704)*

A proposal to include [Sidetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidetic_language)—an extinct member of the Indo-European language family found on coins and inscriptions in Side dating roughly between the fifth and third centuries BCE—in Unicode has been [submitted](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2022/22235-sidetic-rev-2.pdf). For the proposal authored by [Anshuman Pandey](https://twitter.com/anshumanpandey_), revisions based on scholarly serial numbers instead of phonetic values were made by consulting Michaela Zinko and Alfredo Rizza. 🇬🇧
