## The results of the 2019 Font Purchasing Habits Survey have been announced.

[Monotype](https://www.monotype.com) [announced](http://bit.ly/31vwuY8) the results of its survey on 2019 font purchasing habits. According to the results of the survey, conducted in six languages with more than 21,000 participants, the features customers wanted above all else were typefaces with well-crafted spacing and kerning, ligature and alternate options, and numerous weights. It turned out that less than 3% of participants cared whether the typeface had won any awards. You can access [Mary Catherine Pflug](https://www.marycatherine.design)’s article detailing the survey results via the link below. (English)

## Aldus Manutius, inventor of the modern book, who published Ancient Greek works through the Aldine Press revolution

[Typeroom](https://www.typeroom.eu) published [an article](http://bit.ly/3bhGclD) on the anniversary of the death of [Aldus Pius Manutius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldus_Manutius), the Italian humanist, scholar, and printer who founded the [Aldine Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldine_Press). Manutius, a pioneer of the modern book format, was also instrumental in bringing the Greek canon to the present day by publishing surviving Greek classics. (English)

[Typefaces as History: Aldus Manutius and the Noble Bembo — *Joel Friedlander*](https://medium.com/tipografi/tarih-olarak-yaz%C4%B1y%C3%BCzleri-aldus-manutius-ve-asil-bembo-a03ef4e89c0c)

## Typographics will be held in New York on June 19–20.

Celebrating its sixth year, the [Typographics Typography Conference](https://2020.typographics.com), which will focus on contemporary typography and its future, will be held at [The Cooper Union](http://cooper.edu/welcome) in New York between June 19–20, 2020. The conference is being organized as part of the Typographics Festival, taking place between June 15–25, 2020.

## The past, present, and future of variable fonts according to Dalton Maag

[Bianca Berning](http://bberning.com), creative director at [Dalton Maag](https://www.daltonmaag.com), [explains](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/dalton-maag-variable-fonts-opinion-graphic-design-100220) where variable fonts—which she considers the most exciting innovation in type design—came from, how they are made, and how far they can go. ## Mustafa Eren’s new book “Kaligrafik” has been published.

Mustafa Eren, described by Süleyman Saim Tekcan as “the last great master after Emin Barın to bring calligraphy with the Latin alphabet to its peak,” has released his book titled *Kaligrafik*. Featuring Eren’s work, the book was [published](http://www.grafikkitaplari.com/) bilingually in Turkish and English by [Alternatif Yayıncılık](http://www.grafikkitaplari.com/).

## New book from House Industries: “Lettering Manual”

The book [Lettering Manual](https://houseind.com/hi/house-industries-lettering-manual), prepared by [Ken Barber](https://twitter.com/TypeLettering) from [House Industries](https://houseind.com), has taken its place among the typography books to be published in 2020. Pre-orders are available for the book, which serves as a guide for those interested in lettering and is set to be released this coming April.

## A new playground for those interested in variable fonts: Font Playground

Created by [Wenting Zhang](https://twitter.com/DesignJokes) and Hua Shu, [Font Playground](http://play.typedetail.com) offers a playground for typographers and designers interested in variable font technologies to explore what this new technology can offer and how it can benefit their creative workflow. Content created in Font Playground can be exported as CSS as well as tested with [CodePen](https://codepen.io).
