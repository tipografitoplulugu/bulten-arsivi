# Typography Newsletter: February 2021

### A monthly Newsletter featuring noteworthy news on typography

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s February 2021 bulletin:

---

## Wakamai Fondue for all the features of a font

[Roel Nieskens](https://twitter.com/PixelAmbacht) has developed a tool called [Wakamai Fondue](https://wakamaifondue.com/beta/) that answers the question “What can my font do?”. You can view and test all the features of any font you upload to the Wakamai Fondue tool, from its metadata to the languages it supports. Nieskens shared the new features introduced with the update on his [Twitter account](https://twitter.com/PixelAmbacht/status/1356253502809513987).

---

## Monotype released the updated Avenir.

[Monotype](https://www.monotype.com) has [updated](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/monotype-avenir-next-world-typography-graphic-design-280121) [Avenir](https://catalog.monotype.com/family/linotype/avenir), one of the most widely used typefaces in the world, for the first time in twenty years. Originally designed by [Adrian Frutiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Frutiger) in 1988, Avenir was updated in 2002 by Frutiger and Monotype’s creative director [Akira Kobayashi](https://www.myfonts.com/person/Akira_Kobayashi/). The type family named *Avenir Next World* has been expanded to support more than 150 scripts and languages.

---

## Special typeface for people with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder

> “I consulted psychology and neurology researchers in Budapest. (…) One of the conclusions I reached was that different people have different needs even just while reading.”

Hungarian designer [Vatány Szabolcs](https://www.behance.net/szabicsoki) [created](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/02/03/focus-ex-vatany-szabolcs-font-extension-graphic-design/) the typeface *Focus Sans* and a [reading tool for browsers](https://focusex-extension.webflow.io) to make it easier for people with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder to concentrate while reading online content. 🇬🇧

---

## Color font: LetterBoard

[Kyle Jones](http://justkyle.com) designed a color font that takes full advantage of OpenType capabilities. Inspired by sign lettering and featuring only uppercase letters, [LetterBoard](http://www.fonts.lol/letterboard) is free for personal use.

---

## Tips for those seeking feedback on type designs

James Edmondson, founder of [OH no Type](https://ohnotype.co), has written an [article](https://ohnotype.co/blog/proof-it) on how to prepare a good file for those who want to get feedback on their type designs. 🇬🇧

[Gary Hustwit](https://twitter.com/gary_hustwit/), director of the documentaries *Helvetica, Objectified*, and *Urbanized*, has turned over one hundred hours of interviews with more than seventy-five designers into a 736-page [book](https://uncrate.com/helvetica-objectified-urbanized-the-complete-interviews/). 🇬🇧

---

## MATD19/2020 Projects are live!

The typefaces prepared by the master's students of the [University of Reading Department of Typography & Graphic Communication](http://typefacedesign.net/courses/matd/) during the 2019–2020 academic year have been [presented](http://2020.typefacedesign.net/).

## The Readability Group needs your help!

How legible and accessible is one font compared to another? The Readability Group needs your help to examine this complex question in detail.

The [survey](https://survey.thereadability.group/survey/readability-study/) conducted by [The Readability Group](https://www.thereadability.group), founded by Bruno Maag, David Bailey, Michael Mathews, and Gareth Ford Williams, was prepared as part of a two-year study to provide a data and research framework that allows readers to make informed choices. 🇬🇧

---

## New typography book from Poem: Gotico-Antiqua

[Gotico-Antiqua, proto-roman, hybrid](https://www.poem-editions.com/products/gotico-antiqua-book) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2021. Featuring conference proceedings and an exhibition catalog held in Nancy, France in 2019, the book brings together researchers from the fields of typography, paleography, and incunabula, focusing on 15th-century scripts and letterforms that fall between Gothic and Roman.

## The world's most sustainable typefaces

Amsterdam-based design studio [Formafantasma](https://formafantasma.com) [uses](https://www.fastcompany.com/90605005/these-are-the-worlds-most-sustainable-fonts) Arial and Times New Roman to be more sustainable. Stating that sustainability can be measured not only by emissions but also in *bytes*, the studio made its visual choices in favor of fonts they believe to be more sustainable.

---

## New typeface from Dalton Maag: Widescreen

Renowned type foundry [Dalton Maag](https://www.daltonmaag.com) has introduced its new typeface, [Widescreen](https://www.daltonmaag.com/library/widescreen). The font family also includes variable fonts with OpenType features that incorporate different algorithms.

---

## A reading recommendation on the history of type foundries

Melissa Baron has written an informative [article](https://bookriot.com/type-foundries/) on the history of type foundries, which she describes as fascinating. 🇬🇧

---

## Support cancer research by buying fonts!

[Buy Fonts Save Lives](https://buyfontssavelives.com) aims to support cancer research in the United Kingdom through font sales. All proceeds from the website, conceived by Paul Harpin and run by Joe Graham, will be donated to [Cancer Research UK](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org) and [Macmillan Cancer Support](https://www.macmillan.org.uk).

---

## Audi renewed its visual identity.

Audi is updating its visual identity with [a new type family](https://www.audi.com/ci/en/intro/basics/typography.html) that includes a variable font. In the project coordinated by [Jürgen Siebert](https://twitter.com/jr_siebert), it was envisioned that being able to adjust the thickness of the rings in the Audi logo would strikingly reflect the change, its message, and its content.
