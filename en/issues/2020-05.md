## What would happen if Times New Roman and Arial were merged?

Hamburg-based design studio [Liebermann Kiepe Reddemann](https://liebermannkiepereddemann.de) has created an interesting [experiment](https://timesnewarial.liebermannkiepereddemann.de) that merges the *Times New Roman* and *Arial* typefaces using variable font technology. Project details are featured in [It’s Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/liebermann-kiepe-reddemann-times-new-arial-digital-220420)’s [interview](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/liebermann-kiepe-reddemann-times-new-arial-digital-220420) with Liebermann Kiepe Reddemann.

## The world’s first listenable and playable alphabet

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://vimeo.com/413530694)*

The world’s first listenable and playable alphabet was [created](http://elmaaltshift.com/dunyanin-ilk-calinabilir-ve-dinlenebilir-fontu-amadeus/) to promote the theater play *Amadeus*, directed by Işıl Kasapoğlu. [Erhan Özden](https://www.behance.net/erhanozden) and [Oğuzhan Cengiz](https://www.behance.net/oguzhancengiz) were on the typography side of the project.

## Font Bureau’s collection is being removed from Adobe Fonts.

Founded in 1989 by [David Berlow](https://twitter.com/dberlow) and Roger Black, [Font Bureau](http://fontbureau.typenetwork.com) is [removing](https://fonts.adobe.com/foundries/font-bureau) its entire type collection from the [Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com) library starting June 15. As a result, Adobe CC users will no longer be able to use popular typefaces such as Miller, Titling Gothic, Big Caslon, and Benton Sans after the specified date.

## DM Mono has been added to Google Fonts.

[DM Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono), the new typeface from the award-winning type foundry [Colophon](https://www.colophon-foundry.org) founded in 2009, has been added to Google Fonts’ collection. Developed from [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans) designed by [Jonny Pinhorn](https://www.myfonts.com/person/Jonny_Pinhorn/), the open font licensed (OFL) DM Mono has three weights.

## A new typeface for dark mode from Dalton Maag: Darkroom

[Dalton Maag](https://www.daltonmaag.com/) has announced a new typeface called [Darkmode](https://www.daltonmaag.com/library/darkmode), which aims to overcome the challenges brought by modern interface design by combining high-legibility design with innovative variable font technology. Carefully designed to provide a consistent look when switching between dark and normal modes, Darkmode offers two options for each weight: *DarkmodeOn* and *DarkmodeOff*.

## Variable Color Initials from Typearture

Founded by Arthur Reinders Folmer, [Typearture](https://www.typearture.com) introduced its [variable color font initials](https://www.typearture.com/variable-color-font-initials/), which combine variable and color font technologies.

## New licensing from Apple for macOS Catalina

Apple has [licensed](https://typography.guru/journal/awesome-catalina-fonts/) several typefaces from [Commercial Type](https://commercialtype.com), [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz), and [Mark Simonson Studio](https://www.marksimonson.com) for use in macOS Catalina. This means Mac users can download and use popular typefaces such as *Canela, Graphik, Domaine Display*, and *Founders Grotesk*.

## Monotype acquires URW Type Foundry.

[Monotype](https://www.monotype.com) has [agreed to acquire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200517005033/en/Monotype-Agrees-Acquisition-URW-Type-Foundry-Global) Hamburg-based [URW Type Foundry](https://www.urwtype.com/en/), a subsidiary of Global Graphics PLC with a library of over five hundred type families. Monotype also acquired London-based type foundry [Fontsmith](https://www.fontsmith.com) this past January.
