## Three new typefaces from Google Fonts

*Image: © Google Fonts*

As a result of its collaboration with Google, [Colophon Foundry](https://www.colophon-foundry.org) has released the open font licensed (OFL) typefaces [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans), [DM Serif Text](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Serif+Text), and [DM Serif Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Serif+Display) on Google Fonts. Designed by Jonny Pinhorn, DM Sans is available to users in six weights, while DM Serif Text and DM Serif Display, designed by Frank Grießhammer, are available in two weights.

## What is the difference between a typeface and a font?

*Image: © creativebloq.com*

> “The difference between a font and a typeface is fundamentally that the former is a part of the latter. Helvetica is a typeface composed of a family of sans serif fonts sharing a common design concept. At the same time, it consists of fonts at specific weights, styles, and sizes, each of which also has oblique versions.”

In his [article](http://bit.ly/2LCUouW) published on [Creative Bloq](https://www.facebook.com/Creativebloq/?__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARBywtJIQAzNfRghX9Z2ZJhBE1pxSXzxdDeK8QpkiixaPXZGv2COLr9RBsuKripzex774qrvSl2NunTZROM5CtRKnQtpv2mn74xDLbaG9dyXnskjWV0OxIyYPeRQLZ0Tr-jd8RIShdrCiLB7perfnHdXhOiL0IRUmDb8--IAyY5THrf5o7WqohSTYHB67Oc-7HjMX16J9VRPzetlPTM1teZB7SIEmPH_qfNN7uay3tzIDD6MAKG5oPZRlaRE8LwURpamA1tS9dNvCDdl0Ctd9BBZpeRpLk3m-G7M5tfhzCD_T9qN3tP3mWncE26UqxWT3NWoX1-OEiZ-Apb1JUjifb14ew&__tn__=%2CdK%2AF-R&eid=ARD_qrYlkTGn8udrj7RRez5UcYRwBRJS2n4ywWB1rUu7goFBWBD2iXHSZ0uIi4F7hT6DHVrGCHKzmPDy), Garrick Webster addresses the distinction between *typeface* and *font*, which are frequently confused with one another.

*Image: © [Seyhan Argun](https://www.behance.net/gallery/72357763/ABCD-DE-LA-TYPOGRAPHIE-(GRAPHIC-NOVEL))*

## New typeface from Colophon Foundry: Fann Grotesque

*Image: © [Colophon Foundry](http://colophon-foundry.org)*

[Colophon Foundry](http://colophon-foundry.org) announced [Fann Grotesque](http://bit.ly/2JtjGdx), a new sans serif type family inspired by nineteenth-century British grotesque typefaces. Featuring nine weights and offering an italic option for each weight, Fann Grotesque includes OpenType and small caps features.

## Borna Izadpanah’s award-winning typeface: Lalezar

*Image: © [borna.co.uk](https://borna.co.uk/lalezar.html)*

Designed by London-based Iranian type designer [Borna Izadpanah](https://borna.co.uk) (@BornaIz) with inspiration drawn from the [FilmFarsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filmfarsi) genre that emerged in the Iranian film industry during the 1960s and 1970s, [Lalezar](http://bit.ly/2NO0sni) revives the appealing qualities of this style and transforms them into a modern screen-oriented font with Arabic-Latin language support. The open font licensed Lalezar, which received the [TDC Certificate of Typographic Excellence](https://www.tdc.org/competitions/typeface-design-2017/) in 2017 and Second Prize in the [Granshan Type Design Competition](https://www.granshan.com/competition-2016-–-winners) in 2016, is available on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lalezar?subset=arabic&sort=popularity).

## Two new typefaces from Branding with Type

*Image: © [Branding with Type](https://brandingwithtype.com)*

Spain-based [Branding with Type](https://brandingwithtype.com) has released [BW Beto](https://brandingwithtype.com/typefaces/bw-beto), a new typeface bringing together different features from the three-hundred-year evolution of serif type design. Designed by [http://www.albertoromanos.com](http://www.albertoromanos.com) *Alberto Romanos* and suited for contemporary branding projects, BW Beto and BW Beto Grande each feature five weights matching their italic styles.

## Two new typefaces added to Village: Linear Sans and Radial

*Image: © Village*

Founded by [Milos Mitrovic](https://www.behance.net/blacknail), Norway-based type foundry [Gradient](http://www.wearegradient.net) has joined [Village](https://vllg.com), a *type co-op*, with two new typefaces named [Linear Sans](https://vllg.com/gradient/linear-sans) and [Radial](https://vllg.com/gradient/radial). Supporting the Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic alphabets, Linear Sans and Radial are offered to users in five weights, including a variable font.

## Reading recommendation: “Rebus in the Historical Process and Its Areas of Use”

*Image: © [Yedi Journal.](https://dergipark.org.tr/download/article-file/757317)*

In her article titled [Rebus in the Historical Process and Its Areas of Use](https://dergipark.org.tr/download/article-file/757317) published in [Yedi Journal](https://dergipark.org.tr/yedi), [Ece Çalış Zeğerek](http://w3.sdu.edu.tr/personel/03823/dr-ogr-uyesi-ece-calis-zegerek) addresses the concept of the [rebus](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebus)—also used as a "picture alphabet" or "picture puzzle"—emphasizing that, much like emoji characters, it can be viewed as a communication tool that supports language or is more effective than writing and speech.
