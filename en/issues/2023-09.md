# Typography Newsletter: September 2023

### In this month’s bulletin, there are important developments in typography from around the world and Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~4 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s September 2023 bulletin:

---

## The revitalizing power of indigenous typography

*Image: © [thetyee.ca](https://thetyee.ca/News/2023/08/22/Revitalizing-Power-Indigenous-Typography/)*

While Latin alphabet users rarely have to worry about finding and using typefaces, for users of indigenous languages, this is quite difficult. In his [article](https://thetyee.ca/News/2023/08/22/Revitalizing-Power-Indigenous-Typography/), Christopher Cheung offers an eye-opening read through *Musqueam*, one of Canada’s indigenous languages, demonstrating the importance of representing all languages with diverse typefaces. 🇬🇧

---

## A typeface inspired by Miedinger’s type designs: Münchenstein

*Image: © [typotheque.com](https://www.typotheque.com/blog/muenchenstein-forgotten-typefaces-of-max-miedinger)*

Influenced by the lesser-known typefaces of Swiss type designer [Max Miedinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Miedinger) (1910–1980)—best known as the designer of Helvetica, who designed only three typefaces throughout his fifty-year career—Nikola Djurek designed the type family named [Münchenstein](https://www.typotheque.com/blog/muenchenstein-forgotten-typefaces-of-max-miedinger). 🇬🇧

---

## Interview with Fontworks’ creative director Akira Kobayashi

*Image: [Akira Kobayashi and Adrian Frutiger © PRINT](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/monotype-acquires-fontworks-interview-akira-kobayashi/)*

In our [July bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-temmuz-2023-677cf7be9318), we announced that Fontworks, Japan’s iconic type foundry founded in 1993, was acquired by Monotype. Following the acquisition, Kim Tidwell from Print Magazine conducted an [interview](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/monotype-acquires-fontworks-interview-akira-kobayashi/) with the foundry’s creative director, Akira Kobayashi. 🇬🇧

---

## Google Fonts has been redesigned.

*Image: © [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/)*

[Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) redesigned its website to enhance the user experience and provide greater flexibility for future improvements. Tobias Kunisch, Design Director at Google Fonts, explained the updates in a [detailed article](https://material.io/blog/2023-google-fonts-redesign). 🇬🇧

---

## Hand-painted signs have a brand-new magazine

*Image: © [BLAG (Better Letters Magazine)](https://bl.ag/whats-inside-issue-03-of-blag-better-letters-magazine/)*

In our recent bulletins, we shared Steven Heller’s articles on vernacular typography and sign painting. Heller conducted [an interview](https://www.printmag.com/daily-heller/the-daily-heller-handpainted-signs-has-a-brand-new-blag/) with the magazine’s editor, Sam Roberts, focusing on the [latest issue](https://bl.ag/whats-inside-issue-03-of-blag-better-letters-magazine/) of BLAG (Better Letters Magazine), which is filled with stories and photographs of hand-painted signs and includes an article by one of our editors, [Murat Ertürk](http://muraterturk.com/). 🇬🇧

---

## Carving out a place in an industry dominated by Monotype

*Image: © [thehustle.co](https://thehustle.co/where-do-fonts-come-from/)*

> “The industry is moving toward a monopoly, and this leaves independent designers with fewer places to go.”

An [article](https://thehustle.co/where-do-fonts-come-from/) on the monopolization of the type industry by Monotype—which has frequently made headlines in recent years with acquisition news—through the example of Cindy Thomason, who earned only \$12 despite her typeface *Grandhappy* being used in the blockbuster adaptation of The Great Gatsby. 🇬🇧

## Tasarım Sohbetleri

*Image: © [Tasarım Sohbetleri](https://open.spotify.com/show/5r755zw021DTRNbnMt902G)*

The second guest of the podcast channel [Tasarım Sohbetleri](https://open.spotify.com/show/5r755zw021DTRNbnMt902G)—hosted by [Doğacan Ege Altunsu](http://dogacan.com/), where the impact of design on life is discussed with talented figures from all fields of design—was [Onur Yazıcıgil](https://open.spotify.com/episode/61Jq27ZgmiIeO3NqqGMOIN), founder of [ISType](https://istype.com/).

## Type and typography conference for indigenous languages of Latin America: Multilingüe

*Image: © [Multilingüe](https://www.typedrivesculture.com/multilingue/home)*

Organized with the aim of discussing what tools and resources are used to protect, promote, and revitalize indigenous languages by helping preserve the rich linguistic diversity of the Americas, the [Multilingüe](https://www.typedrivesculture.com/multilingue/home) conference will be held between November 9–11, 2023. The online conference will feature discussions on designing in mother tongues with representatives from the Chatino, Mixe, Ngiba, Nahua, and Zapotec nations, along with participants from Mexico, Ecuador, Argentina, Brazil, and Colombia. 🇬🇧

## Sizing typography with modular scales

*Image: © [andrecasal.com](https://andrecasal.com/articles/fluid-typography-sizing-with-modular-scales)*

André Casal’s [article](https://andrecasal.com/articles/fluid-typography-sizing-with-modular-scales) for developers, showing how type scale is affected by screen sizes through modular and hand-picked scales, and the coding behind it all. 🇬🇧

## A new magazine about type design

*Image: © [typedesignclass.com](https://www.typedesignclass.com/)*

Featuring eleven exclusive interviews with promising and established type designers, introducing the people behind favorite typefaces, and exploring creative processes, thoughts, and perspectives in the current type design landscape, the [magazine](https://www.typedesignclass.com/magazine) became available for pre-order on September 23, 2023. 🇬🇧

---

## Commercial Type’s website has been redesigned.

*Image: © [Commercial Type](https://commercialtype.com/news/everything_new)*

The famous type foundry [announced](https://commercialtype.com/news/everything_new) that it redesigned its website in collaboration with GrayBits and Studio Vance Wellenstein. With the [redesigned website](https://commercialtype.com/), all typefaces in the Commercial Type and Commercial Classics categories were brought together under one roof, and everything from licensing to how OpenType features are showcased was reconsidered. 🇬🇧

---

## Tracing the two-hundred-year evolution of Walbaum-Antiqua

*Image: © [Letter Library](https://letterlibrary.org/view/tour/200-years-walbaum-antiqua/introduction-r17/)*

An [article](https://letterlibrary.org/view/tour/200-years-walbaum-antiqua/introduction-r17/) tracing the two-century evolution of Walbaum-Antiqua, which, despite being designed in the 1800s, did not see widespread use until the 20th century. 🇬🇧

## An online and multilingual encyclopedia of typographic terms: Words of Type

*Image: © [*Words of Type*](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/hellolisahuang/words-of-type)*

Type designer Lisa Lisahuang launched a campaign named *Words of Type* on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/hellolisahuang/words-of-type) to create an online, multilingual encyclopedia of typographic terms. 🇬🇧

## Student Work of the Polytechnic School of Printing (1907–1910)

*Image: © [designreviewed.com](https://designreviewed.com/student-work-of-the-polytechnic-school-of-printing-london-1907-1910/)*

## Thomas Phinney elected as new president of ATypI.

*Image: © [thomasphinney.com](https://www.thomasphinney.com/)*

At the Board of Directors meeting held on September 8, 2023, [Thomas Phinney](https://www.thomasphinney.com/) was elected as the [new president](https://atypi.org/2023/09/22/thomas-phinney-is-new-atypi-president/) of ATypI, and Sumanthri Samarawickrama was re-elected as Vice President. Phinney, CEO of FontLab, has been serving on the board of directors of the international typography association [ATypI](https://atypi.org/) since 2004. 🇬🇧

---

## Uzbekistan: Proposal for a change in its alphabet

*Image: © [Serdar Dağıstan](https://twitter.com/sdserdarsd/status/1706330160188842330/photo/1)*

In [our bulletin published in March 2021](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-mart-2021-2b733a4da85d), we announced that Uzbekistan had decided to transition to the Latin alphabet. [According to information shared by Serdar Dağıstan,](https://twitter.com/sdserdarsd) the addition of the letters *ö, ç, ğ, ş* to the alphabet was discussed at a conference held at Tashkent State University of Uzbek Language and Literature.

## Guessing game: Fontguessr

*Image: © [Elizabeth Lin](https://www.artofvisualdesign.com/fontguessr)*

[Elizabeth Lin](https://twitter.com/lalizlabeth) developed a game called [Fontguessr](https://www.artofvisualdesign.com/fontguessr), based on guessing typefaces from the Google Fonts library by their anatomical characteristics. 🇬🇧
