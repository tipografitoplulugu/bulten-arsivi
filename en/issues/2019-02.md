# Typography Newsletter: February 2019

### A monthly newsletter featuring notable news about typography.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~2 minutes

Hello everyone!

Featured news, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s February 2019 bulletin:

---

## “There is now ‘digital man’ in place of ‘typographic man’!...”

February 3 is the day Johannes Gutenberg, who brought the printing press into the service of the masses in fifteenth-century Europe, died in 1468. On the anniversary of Gutenberg’s death, Tayfun Atay [wrote about](https://t24.com.tr/yazarlar/tayfun-atay-pazar/gutenberg-asil-simdi-olurken,21575) the transformation of the printing revolution he offered humanity into the digital revolution.

## Turkish typeface with the Braille alphabet

[Şafak Özen](https://www.behance.net/safakozen) designed a stencil-combination typeface by combining the standard alphabet with the Braille alphabet used for the visually impaired. We congratulate our typographer/designer friend for this meaningful project that combines typography with social responsibility.

## “The history of typography rests on three foundations: technological, aesthetic, and cognitive.”

*Image: © [aepm.eu](https://www.aepm.eu/)*

An article written by Alan Marshall, Bernadette Moglia, and Alice Savoie regarding typography, its importance, and the issues related to the preservation and use of past typographic works: “[Our typographic heritage](http://bit.ly/2TDGiuH)”. (Eng.)

## New type design foundry: Collletttivo

*Image: [www.collletttivo.it](http://www.collletttivo.it/)*

A new type design foundry founded by a group of Italian design students: [Collletttivo](http://www.collletttivo.it/).

Consisting of six typefaces, Collletttivo’s portfolio is full of intriguing typefaces that are free for personal and commercial use.

## FlehaType’s first typeface: Katka

[FlehaType](https://flehatype.com), a new type foundry founded in Slovenia by Teja Smrekar, has taken its place on MyFonts with its first typeface, [Katka](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/flehatype/katka/), consisting of three weights.

## A typeface designed for national parks: National Park

*Image: © [nationalparktypeface.com](https://nationalparktypeface.com)*

Designed for wayfinding in national parks by Do Studio, founded by Jeremy Shellhorn, associate professor of design at the University of Kansas, under an open font license: “[National Park](https://nationalparktypeface.com)”.

## New handwriting collection from Adobe for CC users

*Image: © Adobe Fonts*

Adobe [announced](https://adobe.ly/2V8Jmzj) a new free handwriting collection for Creative Cloud users.

Produced in 2012 for Acrobat and based on the handwriting of Adobe designers, the typefaces *Ernie, Frank*, and *Tiffany* were made available to CC users for general use.
