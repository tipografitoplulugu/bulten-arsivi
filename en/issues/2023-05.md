## Revitalizing Afghan culture through design and typography

*Image: © [Its Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/omar-mohammad-bamyan-project-graphic-design-250423)*

Based in California, Afghan Designer Omar Mohammad revitalizes Afghan culture through design and typography. In an [interview](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/omar-mohammad-bamyan-project-graphic-design-250423) with Joey Levenson, the designer talks about the Bamyan typeface as the outcome of his project exploring the possibilities of a cultural type design created for a region of Afghanistan. 🇬🇧

## Increased letter spacing improves reading acuity in low-vision readers.

*Image: © [Sofia Beier, Chiron A.T. Oderkerk, Birte Bay, Michael Larsen](https://www.jbe-platform.com/content/journals/10.1075/idj.19033.bei)*

According to the [readability study](https://www.jbe-platform.com/content/journals/10.1075/idj.19033.bei) published by Sofia Beier, Chiron A.T. Oderkerk, Birte Bay, and Michael Larsen in the *Information Design Journal*, increased letter spacing (tracking) and larger letter widths improve reading acuity in low-vision readers. 🇬🇧

## A forgotten archive of medieval books and manuscripts discovered in a church

*Image: © [medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2022/09/medieval-books-manuscripts-discovered-romania/)*

A research team led by Adinel C. Dincă from Babeș-Bolyai University in Romania [discovered](https://www.medievalists.net/2022/09/medieval-books-manuscripts-discovered-romania/) more than two hundred books and manuscripts in St. Margaret’s Church in the city of Mediaș. Among these are 139 printed books dating between 1470 and 1600, two manuscripts from the early 16th century, and approximately sixty more documents dating from the 14th to the 16th centuries. 🇬🇧

## The new website for The Freight Collection is live!

*Image: © [The Freight Collection](https://freightcollection.com/)*

The new website for [The Freight Collection](https://freightcollection.com/), Joshua Darden’s serif type family inspired by 18th-century Dutch typefaces, has been announced. ## The world's oldest known Hebrew Bible sold for a record price.

*Image: © [Euronews](https://tr.euronews.com/2023/05/18/dunyanin-bilinen-en-eski-tevrati-codex-sassoon-acik-artirmada-rekor-fiyata-satildi?utm_medium=Social&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1684410246)*

The world’s oldest known Hebrew Bible, the “Codex Sassoon,” was [sold](https://tr.euronews.com/2023/05/18/dunyanin-bilinen-en-eski-tevrati-codex-sassoon-acik-artirmada-rekor-fiyata-satildi?utm_medium=Social&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1684410246) at auction for a record price. The manuscript bears the name of David Solomon Sassoon (1880–1942), who assembled the world’s most important private collection of ancient Jewish texts.

## Self-erasing font: The Erased Font

*Image: © [The Erased Font](https://erased.freepressunlimited.org/)*

Free Press Unlimited, an international organization supporting press freedom, developed a self-erasing font called [The Erased Font](https://erased.freepressunlimited.org/) for World Press Freedom Day on May 3. Developed based on Google’s [Noto Serif](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Serif) typeface, [The Erased Font](https://erased.freepressunlimited.org/) turns into an awareness campaign through deleted words. 🇬🇧

## Leipzig Museum receives a unique cultural treasure from Dresden.

*Image: © [aepm.eu](https://www.aepm.eu/news/leipzig-museum-fur-druckkunst-receives-unique-cultural-treasure-from-dresden/)*
