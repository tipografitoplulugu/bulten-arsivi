# Typography Newsletter: March 2020

### A monthly Newsletter featuring noteworthy news on typography.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Featured news, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s March 2020 bulletin:

---

## Font viewing application from Just van Rossum: FontGoggles

Developed by [Just van Rossum](https://twitter.com/justvanrossum), [FontGoggles](http://fontgoggles.org) is a font viewing application where you can compare multiple fonts based on various attributes. Supported by Google, FontGoggles has been released as open source, currently available only for the Mac platform.

---

## A new type family from Oğuzhan Cengiz: Salin

[Oğuzhan Cengiz](http://www.hurufatfont.com)’s latest type family [Salin](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/hurufatfont-type-foundry/salin) has gone on sale on MyFonts. A modern sans serif family with geometric and humanist touches, it features twenty fonts across ten weights. Boasting rich OpenType features, Salin also offers users a special weight free of charge that ensures smooth reading at small sizes for long texts.

---

## Google Fonts celebrates its tenth anniversary.

[Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com) [celebrates](https://design.google/news/happy-anniversary-google-fonts/) its tenth anniversary with a new interface and an updated catalog. Supporting variable fonts with this update, Google Fonts offers convenience to users looking for variable fonts.

---

## A new type family from Taner Ardalı: Antikor

[Taner Ardalı](http://tanerardali.com/contact)’s monospaced typeface [Antikor](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/taner-ardali/antikor-family/), consisting of three styles and 11 weights with a total of 55 fonts including true italics, has gone on sale on MyFonts at an 80% discount.

---

## A great resource for designers on variable fonts

> “Welcome to the most radical change in type technology since Gutenberg.”

Developed under the leadership of [Jason Pamental](https://rwt.io), [A Variable Fonts Primer](https://variablefonts.io) has launched, allowing you to discover how variable fonts can benefit UI design, accessibility, long-form reading, and push the boundaries of typographic expression on the web.

---

## “What Brands Should Know About Variable Fonts”

Monotype’s article on how variable fonts can help brands looking to stand out in the industry: [“What Brands Should Know About Variable Fonts”](https://monotype.com/resources/expertise/variable-fonts-for-brands) 🇬🇧

---

## “What Is Molecular Typography and Is It Having a Comeback?”

[Emily Gosling](https://twitter.com/nalascarlett)’s article published on [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org) on molecular typography, which emerged in the 1950s and is based on the idea that letters are actually composed of living organisms: [“What Is Molecular Typography and Is It Having a Comeback?”](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/what-is-molecular-typography-and-is-it-having-a-comeback/) 🇬🇧

---

## A documentary every week from Gary Hustwit during the COVID crisis

[Gary Hustwit](https://twitter.com/gary_hustwit) announced that he will release one of his documentary films for free worldwide every week during the global COVID-19 pandemic. The first documentary released during the week of March 17–24 is [Helvetica](https://www.ohyouprettythings.com/free). 🇹🇷

---

## Type designs produced during the 2018–2020 period at ESAD Type

The works produced during the 2018–2020 period at [ESAD Type](https://twitter.com/esadtype), the Postgraduate Program in Type Design at the [Amiens School of Art & Design](https://twitter.com/esadamiens) in France, have been [published](http://postdiplome.esad-amiens.fr). Among the outputs of this period are the typefaces *Neel* by Élodie Tourbier, *Tiga* by Sirin Gunkloy, *Tirhal* by Samar Zureik, and *Welfare* by Léo Guibert.

---

## Jens Kutílek’s quarantine fonts

Curious about what kind of work type designers might have hidden on their computers when things got really rough during the coronavirus quarantine, [Jens Kutílek](https://www.kutilek.de) decided to [release](https://github.com/jenskutilek/quarantine-fonts) several of his projects in varying stages of progress as open source.

---

## A new type family from Emre Güven: Ghino

Ghino, the new type family from [GuinType Foundry](https://www.instagram.com/guintypefoundry/) founded by designer [Emre Güven](https://www.behance.net/emreguven), has been [released](https://bit.ly/2JgBkAh) on MyFonts. A modern sans serif family with geometric touches, it consists of twenty fonts across ten weights.

---

## “Metal Type in the 21st Century” documentary is free on Vimeo!

[Richard Kegler](https://twitter.com/rkegler)’s 44-minute documentary film, [Making Faces: Metal Type in the 21st Century](https://vimeo.com/ondemand/makingfaces), which focuses on the handmade metal type design process of Canadian graphic artist Jim Rimmer who passed away in 2010, has been made available to watch for free with the code *OMEGA22*. 🇬🇧

---

## A new font from Oğuzhan Cengiz: Anglez

Designed by Turkish calligraphy artist [Mustafa Eren](https://www.instagram.com/mustafaerencalligraphy/), Anglez has been published on [MyFonts](https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/hurufatfont-type-foundry/anglez) by [Oğuzhan Cengiz](http://www.hurufatfont.com).
