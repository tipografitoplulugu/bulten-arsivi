## Times New Roman doesn’t have to be boring!

Michael Xing’s [article](https://medium.com/@michealxing/times-new-roman-doesnt-have-to-be-boring-here-s-why-ea51d80262bb) on why Times New Roman doesn’t have to be boring is well worth reading. 🇬🇧 According to Xing, who notes that overuse has contributed to Times New Roman’s bad reputation and that people have grown tired of it, it is possible to evoke new visual flavors for this outdated typeface. The article presents how Times New Roman is presented in design and how it can be further refreshed with two supporting ideas based on predetermined design principles.

## Commercial Type Vault launched.

Founded in 2004 by Paul Barnes and Christian Schwartz, the New York and London-based type foundry [Commercial Type](https://commercialtype.com) opened the [Commercial Type Vault](https://vault.commercialtype.com/login), a repository of typefaces unreleased for 17 years. The vault offers works in progress, old custom projects, finished families that still need specimens and marketing materials, and barely usable sketches for sale. Since most of the typefaces offered are works in progress, you need to create an account so you can be notified of updates regarding the typefaces you license.

## Typography special issue from Journal of Design History

The [Journal of Design History](https://academic.oup.com/jdh) published a [special issue](https://academic.oup.com/jdh/pages/typographic_histories_vsi) on the theme of typography history consisting of twelve articles. Focusing on typographic history between 1988 and 2020, the articles address different aspects of the subject, from designing letterforms to putting those letterforms into use through typesetting, letterpress, typewriter, collage, lithography, and photocopying. 🇬🇧

## Version 14 of Unicode has been released.

Version 14 of Unicode, the universal character encoding standard for written characters and texts, has been released. The updated standard contains a total of 144,697 characters. You can read the [summary](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode14.0.0/UnicodeStandard-14.0.pdf) of the update here, and the [announcement](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode14.0.0/) here. 🇬🇧

## Monotype acquired Hoefler&Co.

Monotype [announced](https://www.monotype.com/company/news/monotype-announces-acquisition-iconic-type-foundry-hoeflerco) that it has acquired the renowned type foundry [Hoefler&Co](https://www.typography.com), founded in 1989 by Jonathan Hoefler. Thus, the Hoefler&Co collection, consisting of more than 1,100 original type designs, joins the Monotype family. With the acquisition of Hoefler&Co, Monotype’s collection has expanded to include popular typefaces such as Gotham, Knockout, Mercury, Sentinel, Chronicle, Decimal, and Archer. Jonathan Hoefler and Carleen Borsella announced that they will take this opportunity to step away from the company to explore new creative endeavors. Monotype had most recently acquired London-based [Fontsmith](https://www.monotype.com/resources/monotype-acquires-fontsmith) and Hamburg-based [URW Type Foundry](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200517005033/en/Monotype-Agrees-Acquisition-URW-Type-Foundry-Global) in 2020. The acquisition also sparked interesting discussions on [TypeDrawers](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/4186/hoefler-co-sold-to-monotype). 🇬🇧

## Readability in Arabic and Persian type design

A [good article](https://quicktypo.com/readability-in-arabic-persian-scripts/) on readability in Arabic and Persian type design from [Atefeh Mohammadi](https://twitter.com/AtefehMohamma18). 🇬🇧 Emphasizing that when too much energy is spent recognizing words, less energy remains to understand them, Mohammadi stresses that the readability of a typeface is vital and examines the factors influencing readability in Arabic and Persian type design.

## Unified Text Engine for Photoshop Beta from Adobe

Adobe announced the [Unified Text Engine](https://community.adobe.com/t5/photoshop-beta-discussions/announcing-the-availability-of-unified-text-engine-in-photoshop-beta) ([Unified Text Engine](https://community.adobe.com/t5/photoshop-beta-discussions/announcing-the-availability-of-unified-text-engine-in-photoshop-beta)), which provides typographic support for various languages including Arabic, Hebrew, Indic, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean for use in Photoshop Beta. 🇬🇧 In previous versions of Photoshop, a feature was offered to users where they could enable type tool support for Middle Eastern (Arabic), Indic, and Southeast Asian languages. With the Unified Text Engine, all advanced typography features will be automatically available and grouped together in the *Photoshop Type Properties* panel.

## New technology from Dalton Maag: PixelFix

[PixelFix](https://www.daltonmaag.com/support/pixelfix) is a font technology developed by [Dalton Maag](https://www.daltonmaag.com) to help improve the clarity of glyphs on high-resolution screens, capable of delivering smaller file sizes compared to the large file sizes resulting from traditional screen hinting. 🇬🇧

## A new book from Michael Arndt

Award-winning graphic designer Michael Arndt’s new book, [Snails & Monkey Tails ](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/snails-monkey-tails-michael-arndt?variant=39664614834210)will be published in the first month of 2022. ## New typeface from Klim Type Foundry: Epicene

Klim Type Foundry [shared](https://klim.co.nz/blog/epicene-design-information/) the design process of their new typeface named [Epicene](https://klim.co.nz/collections/epicene/). 🇬🇧 Inspired by 18th-century typographers [Johann Michael Fleischman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Micha%C3%ABl_Fleischman) and [Jacques-François Rosart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Fran%C3%A7ois_Rosart), Epicene draws attention with its exaggerated details, solid presence at small sizes, and vibrant appearance at large sizes. Featured in Susan Sontag’s essay titled [Notes on “Camp”](https://web.archive.org/web/20190206150611/http://faculty.georgetown.edu/irvinem/theory/Sontag-NotesOnCamp-1964.html) and meaning lacking gender distinction, having aspects of both or neither, Epicene can be tested in Text and Display versions on the type foundry’s website.

A piece worth reading about people who use typography as a tool to change or preserve things. 🇬🇧
