# Typography Newsletter: August 2024

### This month's bulletin features important developments in typography from around the world and Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~6 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the typography world featured in the August 2024 bulletin of the Turkish Typography Society:

---

## The Kernference will be held in November.

The free online conference [The Kernference](https://www.thekernference.com/), organized by [Goodtype](https://goodtype.us/), will take place between November 13–15, 2024. Video recordings of the sessions will also be available to watch later for free.

---

## Wayzgoose on November 8–10!

Registration has opened for [Wayzgoose 2024](https://woodtype.org/pages/wayzgoose), an event bringing together people from the typography, print, and design worlds, to be held for the sixteenth time this year from November 8–10, 2024, by the Hamilton Wood Type Museum in Two Rivers, Wisconsin, United States.

---

## Pixel font creation tool

A browser-based [pixel font creation tool](https://config.aresluna.org/) created by Marcin Wicary, Design Director at Figma. Also, if you are interested in pixel fonts, be sure not to miss Wicary’s [talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SDI8ubVZi7w) at Config2024.

---

## Problems solved with OpenType

[Roel Nieskens](https://pixelambacht.nl/)’s [talk](https://youtu.be/TreBK-EyACQ) at [Web Conferences](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuZeHD5SGecQomz2pVDHGzg) in Amsterdam on problems solved with OpenType technology in CSS is well worth your time. Even if you are not interested in CSS, it is quite good in demonstrating the potential of OpenType.

---

## Sans Bullshit Sans from Nieskens

Speaking of Roel Nieskens, let us also commemorate [Sans Bullshit Sans](https://www.sansbullshitsans.com/), a font he designed in 2015 using OpenType technology that replaces every buzzword with a censorship bar set in Comic Sans. Nieskens explains [here](https://pixelambacht.nl/2015/sans-bullshit-sans/) how he designed Sans Bullshit Sans.

---

## Color font from Frida Medrano: Kalnia Glaze

[Kalnia Glaze](https://www.fridamedrano.com/kalniaglaze), designed by San Francisco-based Type Designer [Frida Medrano](https://www.fridamedrano.com/), is an example of the new color font technology ([COLRv1](https://nabla.typearture.com/whatisCOLRV1.html)) in variable fonts. Commissioned by [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Kalnia+Glaze), Kalnia Glaze is inspired by the Victorian era and features high contrast and terminals designed with refined taste.

---

## New typeface from Dan Cederholm: Drogönsteel

A modern blackletter interpretation inspired by Heavy Metal logos and 1980s role-playing games, created by Dan Cederholm: [Drogönsteel](https://simplebits.shop/products/dragonsteel). A dice game called “[The Vowels of Hell](https://simplebits.shop/products/vowels-of-hell-game)” was also designed to promote Drogönsteel.

---

## New typeface from David Jonathan Ross: Roslindale

Inspired by [De Vinne](https://archive.org/details/deskbookoftypesp00amer/page/338/mode/2up?view=theater), published in the 1890s by Central Type Foundry, [Roslindale](https://djr.com/roslindale) presents De Vinne’s transformation from a Victorian style into a versatile contemporary type family, based on approaches that regained popularity in the 1970s. The page detailing Roslindale’s story and design process is also quite good.

---

## Three great interviews from Type.today

> “We shouldn't learn the tools; the tools should learn how we work.” Gianpaolo Tucci

Interviews conducted by Type.today with [Gianpaolo Tucci](https://type.today/en/journal/tucci), author of the book “Aesthetic Imperfections” exploring the possibilities of AI typography; [Kris Sowersby](https://type.today/en/journal/kris_sowersby), founder of Klim Type Foundry; and [Lisa Huang](https://type.today/en/journal/lisahuang), creator of the project *Words of Type*.

---

## Font pairing guide from Silverstag Type Foundry

The font pairing guide created by Madrid-based [Silverstag Type Foundry](https://www.silverstag.design/) can be downloaded for free when you subscribe to the type foundry’s [newsletter](https://silverstag.myflodesk.com/free-font-pairing-guide).

---

## New feature from Production Type

Production Type announced a new feature on its website called [Type Playlists](https://productiontype.com/curated-playlists), highlighting curated and editorial collections from its library.

---

## Language support for Comicraft fonts

Comicraft, a type foundry specializing in comic book fonts of all kinds, [announced](https://www.comicbookfonts.com/article-a/275.htm) that it has updated its library of more than three hundred fonts with extensive language support. With this update, all Comicraft fonts now support over two hundred languages.

---

## Kennedy Jr.’s monograph opens for pre-order.

The monograph titled “[Citizen Printer](https://letterformarchive.org/shop/amos-paul-kennedy-jr-citizen-printer/)” by American artist Amos Paul Kennedy Jr., internationally recognized for his text-focused work on social justice and Black power, published by Letterform Archive, is now open for pre-order. In addition, Letterform Archive is organizing an [exhibition](https://letterformarchive.org/news/amos-paul-kennedy-jr-citizen-printer/) curated by Kelly Walters, which opened on June 29, 2024, at its gallery in San Francisco, featuring more than one hundred and fifty typographic works by Kennedy. The exhibition will remain open until January 2025.

---

## Web typography book from Jason Santa Maria

Jason Santa Maria’s book titled “[On Web Typography](https://jasonsantamaria.com/#on-web-typography)”, published in 2014, is available for free download. Opening with a foreword by Ellen Lupton, the book consists of six chapters containing detailed information on web typography, from how we read to type pairing.

---

## New visual identity design for Pomus from Sömestr Studio

Istanbul-based Sömestr Studio designed a [new visual identity for Pomus](https://somestrstudio.com/pomus/), a production company. Prepared in collaboration with [Element Type](https://elementtype.co/) and featuring six distinct states for popular film genres, the logo was designed as a variable font for versatility and effective use in animation features. In addition to the logo, a custom typeface called *Pranklin Pothic* was created, based on the famous Franklin Gothic, to bring typographic capabilities to the website and other design materials. ➚ **Reading Recommendation: [https://medium.com/tipografi/s%C3%B6yle%C5%9Fi-i%CC%87brahim-ka%C3%A7t%C4%B1o%C4%9Flu-181110d5158a](https://medium.com/tipografi/s%C3%B6yle%C5%9Fi-i%CC%87brahim-ka%C3%A7t%C4%B1o%C4%9Flu-181110d5158a)**Interview with İbrahim Kaçtıoğlu, co-founder of Element Type

---

## Winner of the 2024 Gerard Unger Scholarship: Anne-Dauphine Borione

The type family *Dargon*, designed by French Type Designer [Anne-Dauphine Borione](https://www.type-together.com/anne-dauphine-borione) while studying at TypeMedia (KABK), was chosen from among 62 designers worldwide as the recipient of the [2024 Gerard Unger Scholarship](https://www.type-together.com/2024-gerard-unger-scholarship-results). In addition to the Gerard Unger Scholarship, TypeTogether awarded prizes to six promising type designs. The type designs awarded were *Citadel* by Guocheng Xin from China, *Dilinh* by Đông-Trúc Nguyễn from Vietnam, *Dulungan* by Jad Maza from the Philippines, *Lezarde* by Lucas Voilquin from Germany, *Malinali* by Sandra Morales from Mexico, and *Tarisel* by Martin Brendecke from the United States.

---

## Results of Ascenders announced.

The results of Ascenders, the typography-themed portfolio competition organized by the Type Directors Club for designers aged 35 and under, have been [announced](https://tdc.org/competitions/ascenders-2024/). The 2024 winners consist of thirteen emerging designers, including eleven individuals and two duos. The awards ceremony will be held on Wednesday, August 14, at The One Club for Creativity Gallery in New York.

---

## A revival from Ethan Nakache

French Type Designer [Ethan Nakache](https://ethannakache.com/), with the aim of bringing something new to serif type, has [revived](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/ethan-nakache-the-designers-foundry-goodman-typeface-graphic-design-project-290724) the typeface Gesh Ortega Roman 275—produced for phototypesetting by Berthold in 1972—as a variable font under the name [Goodman](https://ethannakache.com/goodman-typeface/), with support from Daniel McQueen, founder of The Designers Foundry.

---

## Superior Type: Searching for the perfect curve

An [article](https://fontstand.com/news/foundry-profiles/superiortype-searching-for-the-perfect-curve/) in which Vojtěch Říha, founder of [Superior Type](https://www.superiortype.com/), explains his sources of inspiration, discusses collaborations with other Czech type designers, and shares the type foundry’s future plans.

---

## Giant newspaper from St. Gallen School of Design students

Typography and Communication Design students at the St. Gallen School of Design (GBS) [step out of the ordinary](https://www.typemates.com/news/giant-newspaper) with a giant newspaper covering an area of 780 m², created using TypeMates’ typeface [Franziska](https://www.typemates.com/fonts/franziska). With Franziska, the oversized version of the newspaper offers a distinct reading experience.

---

## An unusual lettering tool: “Lettering triangle”

Ken Barber [talks about](https://typeandlettering.substack.com/p/an-unusual-angle-on-lettering) an unusual lettering tool he came across in the book “Rogers’ Mechanical Drawing and Design”, first published in 1913. The tool, called a *lettering triangle*, makes it easier to draw uppercase letters with angled anatomy.

---

## The use of generative AI in type design

Filip Paldia [presents](https://www.lttrink.com/blog/use-of-generative-ai-in-type-design-the-state-of-the-art-survey?mc_cid=e6e8e3a344&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) a comprehensive survey of articles published since 2011 on artificial intelligence and type design. Drawing upon recent projects, he explores the possibilities of leveraging generative AI in type design.

---

## Handwriting imitation with AI models

Flux, one of the new AI models, becomes capable of imitating handwriting after a brief training. Researchers are conducting various experiments by training the model on writing styles and languages. The [example shared on X](https://x.com/fofrAI/status/1827474269170725350) includes AI images generated from J. R. R. Tolkien’s handwriting.

---

## A new typography tool from Google: GenType

Google’s AI tool [GenType.](https://labs.google/gentype), which generates letters of the Latin alphabet with a single prompt using Imagen 2.

---

## Monotype and Sufio partnership

Monotype [announced](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/monotype-and-sufio-announce-partnership-monotypes-fonts-api-product?mc_cid=e6e8e3a344&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) a partnership with [Sufio](https://sufio.com/), a global invoicing solution. This partnership provides Sufio customers with access to more than forty thousand typefaces from Monotype’s library for use in invoices, credit notes, and other financial documents.

---

## Custom typeface for Fortnum & Mason

London and San Francisco-based design studio [Otherway](https://otherway.com/) [designed](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/otherway-fortnum-and-mason-typeface-graphic-design-project-120824) a custom typeface for the refreshed visual identity of Fortnum & Mason, the 300-year-old British store selling tea and luxury preserved foods.

---

## The first phototypesetting machine

Designers tend to think that type technology transitioned from movable metal type to digital with Postscript fonts. However, there was a phototypesetting era that lasted from the 1960s to the 1980s. Roger Black offers a [great read](https://typenetwork.com/articles/morisawa-100th-anniversary) on phototypesetting, which is now almost forgotten.

---

## What happens when you set the font size to 32?

A [great tip](https://tonsky.me/blog/font-size/) from Nikita Prokopov on correctly setting font size in digital typesetting.

---

## Jessica Walsh launches her own type foundry.

Known for the New York-based creative studio [&Walsh](https://andwalsh.com/), designer Jessica Walsh has [launched](https://www.creativeboom.com/news/jessica-walsh-launches-her-own-type-foundry-type-of-feeling/) her own type foundry, [Type of Feeling](https://typeoffeeling.com/), dedicated to creating unique typefaces that convey distinct and rarely discussed emotions.
