# Typography Newsletter: August 2023

### In this month's bulletin, there are significant typography developments around the world and in Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~6 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s August 2023 bulletin:

---

## New typeface from Ertekin Erdin

Following [Hasköy](https://haskoy.ertekinn.com/), [Ertekin Erdin](https://ertekin.xyz/) announced his new condensed serif typeface derived from Libre Caslon. Based on the *Libre Caslon Text* family, [Libre Caslon Condensed](https://github.com/ertekinno/libre-caslon-condensed) seems to be an ideal choice for design projects offering a harmonious blend of elegance and legibility.

---

## The Gutenberg Parenthesis: Why is print culture the key to the future?

A [review](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2023/aug/02/the-gutenberg-parenthesis-the-age-of-print-and-its-lessons-for-the-age-of-the-internet-by-jeff-jarvis-review) written by Houman Barekat in *The Guardian* of American journalist Jeff Jarvis’s book [The Gutenberg Parenthesis](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/gutenberg-parenthesis-9781501394829/). 🇬🇧 Through the concept of the *Gutenberg Parenthesis*—used by Danish scholar Lars Ole Sauerberg, meaning that the five hundred-plus years of literary culture history from Gutenberg’s development of the printing press in the mid-fifteenth century to the turn of the millennium should be seen merely as a turning point—the book offers a detailed examination of print history from Gutenberg’s press to word processing software, and provides important lessons for the digital age.

---

## Naming a type design

In her [article](https://www.fastcompany.com/90929143/from-aptos-to-helvetica-heres-how-fonts-get-their-unusual-names) published in Fast Company, following Microsoft’s recent switch to Aptos as its default typeface, Elissaveta Brandon touches upon the topic of giving unusual names to type designs, noting that it is no different than naming a child and requires equal parts strategy and intuition. 🇬🇧

---

## Martial’s epigrams, among the earliest books printed in pocket format

Peter Harrington [shared](https://twitter.com/PHrarebooks/status/1686770809233088512) a video of the first edition of [Marcus Valerius Martial’s epigrams](https://www.peterharrington.co.uk/epigrammata-118784.html), one of the earliest books printed in pocket format by the Aldine Press, founded by Aldus Manutius. The book is a product of Manutius’s groundbreaking concept that he called the *portable library* (libelli portatiles), which began with publishing an edition of Virgil in 1501…

## Eastern European Design Festival (FEED)

Organized to bring together various events such as conferences, workshops, and competitions focusing on visual design in Eastern and Southeastern Europe, the [Eastern European Design Festival](https://www.feedesign.eu/) (FEED), which will be held in Warsaw on September 2–4, 2023, will also feature [Onur Yazıcıgil](http://www.onuryazicigil.com/), founder of [ISType](https://www.istype.com/) from our country.

## Typodiversity 01: Exploring the world of Arabic script

Organized in collaboration with the University of Reading Department of Typography & Graphic Communication and [Typodiversity](http://typodiversity.net/), the typography and type design events will take place on November 3–4, 2023, under the theme *Exploring the world of Arabic script*.

## Why is X so important?

An [article](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/x-letter-symbol-meaning) by historian of mathematics Peter Schumer examining the origins of the letter X, which has been in the spotlight following Twitter’s rebranding. 🇬🇧

## Type selection for responsive interfaces

Jake Giltsoff’s [article](https://maketypework.com/fonts-for-responsive-user-interfaces/) published on Make Type Work regarding typeface selection for responsive interface designs. 🇬🇧 Giltsoff’s fresh initiative [Make Type Work](https://maketypework.com/) looks like it will be a wonderful typography resource.

## Alverata in the rebranding of Bhutan

In the rebranding work of the South Asian nation of Bhutan, TypeTogether’s [Alverata](https://www.type-together.com/alverata-font) typeface, designed by the late Gerard Unger, was used. TypeTogether published a [detailed article](https://www.type-together.com/2022-alverata-bhutan) on the design process of the branding work conducted by [MMBP & Associates](https://www.mmbpltd.com/case-studies/kingdom-of-bhutan/). 🇬🇧

---

## A comprehensive project on handwriting education in primary schools: Primarium

Speaking of [TypeTogether](https://www.type-together.com/), the type foundry has embarked on a groundbreaking educational project called [Primarium](https://primarium.info/) to investigate the state of Latin-based handwriting education in primary schools across more than forty countries and document different handwriting models. Primarium aims to offer a comprehensive perspective that allows for an in-depth contextual analysis of the complex factors affecting how children learn to read and write, from the benefits of learning handwriting to the most effective current handwriting models and teaching methods. 🇬🇧

We care about the stories and design processes of typefaces, and we frequently feature articles on this topic in our bulletin. In [last month’s bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-temmuz-2023-677cf7be9318), we shared Frere-Jones’s article on the design process of the typefaces *Surveyor* and *Whitney*. A [similar article](https://mass-driver.com/article/md-system-a-typeface-for-general-use) on the design of [MD System](https://mass-driver.com/typefaces/md-system), built around a foundational principle, came from Rutherford Craze. 🇬🇧

## All of Matthew Carter’s typefaces are being brought together.

Nearly all of the typefaces designed by Matthew Carter throughout his sixty-year career that exist digitally are being [brought together](https://typenetwork.com/articles/matthew-carter-collection/matthew-carter-fonts) in a single place. Among these are typefaces from Monotype, Morisawa, and Hamilton Wood Type, as well as those at TypeNetwork partner Carter & Cone. ➚ **Reading Recommendation:** “[The Carter Identity](https://typenetwork.com/articles/matthew-carter-collection)” 🇬🇧

A [fine essay](https://www.linyangchen.com/Typography-Fell-Types-font) penned by graduate student Lin Yangchen regarding the adventure of using a seventeenth-century typeface in their thesis. 🇬🇧

## Type specimen platform Letter Library launched.

Founded by Ralf Herrmann, [Letter Library](https://letterlibrary.org/) has launched, aiming to bring together type specimens onto a single platform so they can be researched, examined, and preserved.

---

## Canadian Typography Archives launched.

Another archive news item from Canada… Funded by the Gibson Fund, the website of the [Canadian Typography Archives](https://www.canadiantypography.ca/), which aims to document the history of type and typography in Canada, has launched. It was announced that the archive’s initial focus will span from the first printing press opened in the city of Halifax in 1751 to the beginning of the digital era around 1985, and that in future phases, with the addition of indigenous languages such as French and content, the timeline will be expanded to encompass works up to approximately 2010. 🇬🇧

The 2022 graduation projects of students from the postgraduate type design program [TypeMedia](https://2022.typemedia.org/) have been published.

## New book from the Nebiolo History Project

We had shared developments regarding the [Nebiolo History Project](http://nebiolohistory.org/), conducted by the research team established by Marta Bernstein, James Clough, Alessandro Colizzi, Riccardo De Franceschi, Massimo Gonzato, and Riccardo Olocco to investigate Italy’s most famous type foundry, in [our past bulletins](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-may%C4%B1s-2022-c258249bd466). The [book](https://lazydog.eu/en/product/the-nebiolo-typefoundry-1878-1978-new-critical-assessments/) prepared by the project team about the Nebiolo company, which was Italy’s largest type foundry both nationally and internationally for most of the twentieth century until its closure in 1978, has been published. 🇬🇧

## “New York storefronts were pillars of the community.”

In the [July bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-b%C3%BClteni-temmuz-2023-677cf7be9318), we shared Steven Heller’s article on brush sign painting. ## The signs of Los Angeles

Sign painter [Bryan Yonki](https://bryanyonki.com/), who began documenting his love for Los Angeles signs on his [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/handpaintedinla/) account after moving from Chile to Los Angeles in 2014, has published his book titled “[Hand-Painted in LA](https://www.printmag.com/design-books/hand-painted-in-la/)”. 🇬🇧

## The design process of Grato Marker

An [article](https://www.typemates.com/news/making-of-grato-marker) about the design process of [Grato Marker](https://tejasmrekar.com/work-1/grato-marker), exploring how [Teja Smrekar](https://tejasmrekar.com/) combined handwriting with geometry. 🇬🇧

## A new program from Monotype for type foundries and type designers

Monotype announced its [Foundry Program](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/monotype-launches-new-foundry-program-designed-empower-and-scale-type), aimed at empowering the type community. It offers type foundries and type designers greater visibility, a suite of analytics tools, marketing features, and an improved royalty model. It is reported that more than a thousand type foundries and type designers signed up for the program prior to the launch. 🇬🇧

---

## Fontshop.com, Linotype.com, and Fonts.com are closing.

There is another news item from Monotype. Monotype [announced](https://www.myfonts.com/pages/closure-announcement-faq) that it will close the Fontshop.com, Linotype.com, and Fonts.com pages in the coming months, but customers will have the option to migrate their orders to MyFonts.com. 🇬🇧

## Several issues of Typography Papers journal published as PDFs.

In [our past bulletins](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-aral%C4%B1k-2022-812bc46e6f13), we announced that certain issues of [Typography Papers](http://typography.network/typographypapers/), produced by the University of Reading Department of Typography & Graphic Communication under the leadership of Paul Stiff, were made available online. Issues five, six, seven, and nine of the journal have been uploaded to the journal’s website as PDFs. 🇬🇧

## A stamp kit to explore typography: BlockFace

[According to news from Bigumigu](https://bigumigu.com/haber/tipografik-deneyler-icin-damga-seti-blockface/), [BlockFace](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/martinschneider/blockface-a-stamp-kit-to-explore-typography-and-more?), developed by Will Mower with the *Open Press Project*, enables users to create letterforms and design typographic works by printing with these forms onto various surfaces, from handmade signs to t-shirts.
