## Is the future tense our enemy?

*Image: Nishant Choksi © [hbrturkiye.com](https://hbrturkiye.com/dergi/dil-ar-ge-harcamalarini-nasil-etkiler)*

According to an article published in *Harvard Business Review Türkiye* magazine, the likelihood of plans being realized decreases in languages with a strong emphasis on the future tense. In the [study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0929119920300225), which examined R&D investments across 52 countries between 1996 and 2013, it was [concluded](https://tanatarken.wordpress.com/2020/12/05/gizli-dusmanimiz-gelecek-zaman-eki-ecek-acak/) that in fifteen countries spoken with weak future-tense references, R&D expenditures and the number of acquired patents increased.

## Germany will remove Nazi traces from the phonetic alphabet.

*Image: © [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/turkce/haberler-dunya-55187680)*

[According to a BBC report,](https://www.bbc.com/turkce/haberler-dunya-55187680) Germany will update its phonetic alphabet due to additions made by the Nazis. Jewish names in the German phonetic alphabet—such as “David” for the letter “D”, “Nathan” for “N”, and “Zacharias” (Zechariah) for “Z”—were replaced during the Nazi regime that ruled between 1933 and 1945. Now, experts affiliated with the German Institute for Standardization (DIN) are working on a new phonetic alphabet to be announced to the public in 2022.

## New typography book from Hendrik Weber

*Image: © [niggli](http://www.niggli.ch/en/italic.html)*

Written by type designer [Hendrik Weber](https://twitter.com/Hendrinks) from [Type By](https://www.typeby.com), the book titled [Italic: What gives Typography its emphasis](http://www.niggli.ch/en/italic.html) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2020. Tracing the historical development of italics from handwriting to digital typesetting, the book presents its formal and functional characteristics to readers.

## New typography book from Slanted Publishers

*Image: © [Slanted Publishers](https://www.slanted.de/product/support-independent-type/)*

Edited by Marian Misiak and Lars Harmsen, the book titled [Support Independent Type — the New Culture of Type Specimens](https://www.slanted.de/product/support-independent-type/) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2020. Published by *Slanted Publishers* to support independent type foundries, the book on the impact of type specimens on design and typographic culture presents an extensive archive of physical and digital type specimens.
