# Typography Newsletter: February 2023

### In this month’s bulletin, there are important developments in typography from around the world and Turkey.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~4 minutes

Hello everyone!

Highlights, new typefaces, and developments from the typography world in the Turkish Typography Society’s February 2023 bulletin:

---

## 2023 reports have started to be published.

*Image: © [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/type-trends-2023)*

In Monotype’s [2023 report](https://www.monotype.com/type-trends-2023) on popular trends, along with a pulse check on what is happening in the design and type industry, there are technological developments, their impact on design, and predictions for the future of typography. In [Print](https://www.printmag.com/typography/the-2023-print-typography-report-why-type-empathy-matters/)’s report, on the other hand, the word *empathy* is emphasized, stating that typography promises to spark dialogue and action. The proliferation of perspectives on how we teach typography and how we create it historically, culturally, and orientationally focuses on raising our awareness with more diverse typography choices and establishing more meaningful dialogues with one another. 🇬🇧

---

## How can you identify typefaces that are worth your money?

*Image: © [TypeTogether](https://www.type-together.com/font-quality)*

A remarkable [article](https://www.type-together.com/font-quality) by Veronika Burian and Jose Scaglione from [TypeTogether](https://www.type-together.com/) on how to evaluate the quality of type from a monetary perspective. 🇬🇧

---

## Dedicated interface for variable fonts from Adobe Fonts

*Image: © [SideNote Variable](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/sidenote-variable)*

[Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com/) has revamped its interface for efficiently searching variable fonts. Featuring a notable slant control, the interface offers users more than a hundred variable fonts that help make selecting typefaces quick and easy.

---

## Award-winning variable font for India: Anek

*Image: © [Google Design](https://design.google/library/anek-multiscript/)*

A worthwhile [article](https://design.google/library/anek-multiscript/) about the design process of Anek, a variable font covering nine Indian languages and Latin, created through the collaboration of twelve type designers working across eight cities in India. 🇬🇧

---

## A proofing app for your type designs: Font Proofer

*Image: © [Font Proofer](https://fontproofer.com/)*

Designed by Peter Nowell, [Font Proofer](https://fontproofer.com/) is a new Mac app that helps you generate proofs as often as you need while working on your type designs.

---

## How can typography create a more inclusive future?

*Image: © [It’s Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-how-typography-can-make-a-more-inclusive-future-graphic-design-040123)*

In his [article](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-how-typography-can-make-a-more-inclusive-future-graphic-design-040123), Ray Masaki argues that a more equitable and diverse future for design will depend less on tools and technology and more on the decisions made by type designers. 🇬🇧

---

## The end of Type 1

*Image: © [TypeNetwork](https://typenetwork.com/articles/the-end-of-type-1)*

This month, Adobe will release an application update that removes support for the PostScript font format known as *Type 1*. If some of your work dates back 20 years, potential problems may be lurking. An [article](https://typenetwork.com/articles/the-end-of-type-1) on the topic by [Christopher Slye](https://twitter.com/ChristopherSlye). 🇬🇧

---

## Who can design Arabic typography?

*Image: © [Futuress](https://futuress.org/stories/arabic-typography/)*

In our bulletins, we had previously touched upon the growing interest in Arabic type design in recent years. In his [article](https://futuress.org/stories/arabic-typography/), Lebanese designer Imad Gebrayel sheds light on intriguing topics, exploring questions such as who gets to design Arabic typography, whether Arabic literacy is a prerequisite for Arabic type design, who is behind the wave of Arabic type design, and whether comprehensive research is sufficient to connect with a particular culture. 🇬🇧

## The psychology of deliberately making a font difficult to read

*Image: © [Carol Yepes/Getty Images](https://modus.medium.com/using-typography-to-hack-your-brain-401c4c1ee4bb)*

In her [article](https://modus.medium.com/using-typography-to-hack-your-brain-401c4c1ee4bb), [Sarah Hyndman](https://medium.com/u/374dc01c6ed6) questions why some deliberately make fonts harder to read, even as advancements in printing technology and type design strive to make words more legible. 🇬🇧

## Resources on type design

*Image: © [Type Design Resources](https://typedesignresources.com/)*

Featured in our [February 2022](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-%C5%9Fubat-2022-9d9bcbc58c9c) bulletin, [Justin Penner](https://justinpenner.ca/)’s [page](https://typedesignresources.com/) listing various resources on type design has been updated. ## The typographic splendor of Paris gathered in a book.

*Image: © “[Paris Typographie](https://arenes.fr/livre/paris-typographie/)”*

Published by [Les Arènes](https://twitter.com/les_arenes) Publishing and prepared by Marguerite Chaillou, the 700-page book “[Paris Typographie](https://arenes.fr/livre/paris-typographie/)” showcases the typographic splendor of Paris through posters, signs, banners, neon lights, and graffiti. 🇫🇷 ➚ **Bonus:** [Vernacular typography works from around the world and Turkey](https://aposto.com/s/vernakuler-tipografiye-yonelik-ilgi-artiyor)

## Scale in typography

*Image: © [Spencer Mortensen](https://spencermortensen.com/articles/typographic-scale/)*

[A good article](https://spencermortensen.com/articles/typographic-scale/) by [Spencer Mortensen](https://spencermortensen.com/) on the typographic scale used for centuries to select harmonious font sizes. At the end of the article, a tool is also provided for you to test the formulas. 🇬🇧

---

## The design process of GT Pressura

*Image: © [Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/blog/typeface-stories/gt-pressura-type-story)*

An [article](https://www.grillitype.com/blog/typeface-stories/gt-pressura-type-story) about the design process of the typeface [GT Pressura](https://www.grillitype.com/typeface/gt-pressura), first released in 2012 by [Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/). Conceived when Dominik Huber and Marc Kappeler undertook a book project on conceptual art in Moscow in 2008 and needed a custom typeface to accurately reflect their design vision, the typeface, updated in 2022, has an industrial aesthetic. 🇬🇧

## Why is Japanese calligraphy ink so expensive?

*Image: © [Insider Business](https://youtu.be/GSuFSYY-X9w)*

A [YouTube video](https://youtu.be/GSuFSYY-X9w) about the production process of traditional Japanese calligraphy ink, also known as sumi ink, which takes at least four years to produce and can reach a price of nearly \$2,000. 🇬🇧
