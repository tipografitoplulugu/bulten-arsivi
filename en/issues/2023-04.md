⏰ **Reading Time:** ~3 minutes

Hello everyone!

Featured news, new typefaces, and developments from the world of typography in the Turkish Typography Society’s April 2023 bulletin:

---

## Sungu Çapan passed away at the age of 76.

> “In 1960s Turkey, Yeni Sinema magazine was virtually the Ray Gun of the 1990s that we know so well from the history of world design! And graphic designer Sungu Çapan, of course, was the David Carson of that era!” — Ömer Durmaz

Film writer and critic Sungu Çapan passed away at the age of 76. Better known for his film writing, while also an accomplished graphic designer who graduated from the State Academy of Fine Arts Department of Graphic Arts, Çapan was among the first figures in our country to grasp contemporary typography, and the covers of *Yeni Sinema* magazine, a publication of the Turkish Cinematheque Association, are of great significance to our design history.

## Esad Type 2021–2023 student work

The work of the 2021–2023 class students from the postgraduate typeface design program [Esad Type](http://postdiplome.esad-amiens.fr/) has been published. In addition, [online applications](https://esad-amiens.design/fr/articles/esadtype-2023-25-appel-a-candidatures-call-for-applications) have opened for the 2023–2025 term. The program, which will begin in September 2023, will last a total of eighteen months. Instruction will be conducted predominantly in English and French.

## Monotype and Shopify collaboration

Monotype and Shopify [announced](https://www.monotype.com/company/press-release/monotype-and-shopify-expand-access-typography-digital-environments-help?mc_cid=e8c73dbe31&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) a collaboration that will modernize access to typography for creative and retail professionals worldwide. Through this collaboration, merchants selling on Shopify will now be able to access Monotype Fonts. They will be enabled to prototype design projects using typefaces from Monotype's font library and gain exclusive access to design events. 🇬🇧

## Jikji goes on display in Paris.

Known as the oldest existing work printed with movable type, [Jikji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jikji) has gone on [display](https://tr.euronews.com/2023/04/18/dunyanin-makinayla-basilmis-budist-ogretiyi-anlatan-en-eski-kitabi-pariste-sergileniyor) in Paris after a 50-year hiatus. Printed in 1377 in Korea during the Goryeo Dynasty, 78 years before Gutenberg's 42-Line Bible, the book is also known as the *Anthology of Great Buddhist Priests' Zen Teachings*.

## The mysterious script “Linear Elamite” is being deciphered.

[According to Arkeofili's report](https://arkeofili.com/gizemli-eski-yazi-linear-elamca-sonunda-cozuluyor/), *Linear Elamite*, once used across the Iranian geography, is being deciphered thanks to a team of scientists led by archaeologist François Desset. According to archaeologist Massimo Vidale from the University of Padua—who was not involved in the research—this is one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of recent years... [Another report related to writing](https://arkeofili.com/4-000-yillik-tabletlerde-kayip-kenan-dili-tanimlandi/) from Arkeofili covers the lost Canaanite language identified on two ancient clay tablets discovered in Iraq and covered entirely in cuneiform script.

## Revival project from Onur Yazıcıgil: Pergamon

[Onur Yazıcıgil](http://www.onuryazicigil.com/) has [published](http://www.onuryazicigil.com/miscellaneous) his revival based on stone inscriptions in Greek letters in Pergamon, titled the *Pergamon Project*, as a book. Published by YEM Yayıncılık, the book presents documentation of the transformation of Pergamon’s meticulous epigraphic heritage into a typeface design. ➚ Review [article](https://www.manifold.press/pergamon-yazi-tipi) on the book by Ardan Ergüven.

## The ATypI conference will be held in Paris on May 9–14.

The [program](https://atypi.org/conferences-events/atypi-paris-2023/program/) for the ATypI Typography Conference, to be held in Paris from May 9–14, 2023, featuring Onur Yazıcıgil among speakers from our country, has been announced. The conference will feature more than 100 sessions and 150 speakers.
