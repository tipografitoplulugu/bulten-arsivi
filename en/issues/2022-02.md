## Bauhaus typography is more complicated than you think

*Image: © [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/bauhaus-typography-is-more-complicated-than-you-think/?mc_cid=aa7f68ef5b&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1)*

## German Court rules against Google Fonts

*Image: © [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com)*

The Munich Regional Court [ruled](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/lhm-20-01-2022-3-o-1749320/) that using [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) via a content delivery network (CDN) violates the General Data Protection Regulation due to Google tracking users’ IP addresses without consent. Until the decision becomes final, downloading and using the typefaces from Google Fonts locally is recommended. 🇩🇪

## The 1980s are back.

*Image: © [Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/the-80s-are-back-baby)*

Elizabeth Goodspeed [wrote](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/the-80s-are-back-baby) about the rising popularity of the multi-layered and type-centric advertising aesthetic of the 1980s. 🇬🇧

## Fontspring joins the Dribbble family.

*Image: © [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/stories/2022/02/01/fontspring-joins-dribbble?utm_campaign=2022-02-02&utm_medium=email&utm_source=insider-20220202)*

Hosting over 700 typefaces from the world’s top type foundries, [Fontspring](https://www.fontspring.com/) joins the Dribbble family. By [joining forces with Creative Market in 2020](https://dribbble.com/stories/2020/04/30/creative-market-joins-dribbble), Dribbble had provided greater visibility and opportunities for both designers and digital creators. Following Fontspring joining Dribbble, it was [announced](https://dribbble.com/stories/2022/02/01/fontspring-joins-dribbble) that the teams will continue working as before, with their roadmap and goals established. 🇬🇧

*Image: © TN*

Bas Jacobs of [Underware](https://underware.nl) [spoke](https://underware.typenetwork.com/news/article/thinking-beyond-the-static) with TypeNetwork about how the dynamic possibilities of variable fonts have transformed everything the type foundry designs. 🇬🇧

A [web page](https://2000upm.uk/Homepage) where type designs are sold to raise funds for graphic design students at [Kingston School of Art](https://www.kingston.ac.uk/undergraduate/courses/graphic-design/) to hold their degree show. 🇬🇧

## Canned fish museum from Portugal

*Image: © [conservasdeportugal.com](https://conservasdeportugal.com/category/cans-embalagens/)*

A [canned fish museum](https://conservasdeportugal.com/category/cans-embalagens/) with wonderful typography, prepared by [Can The Can](https://www.canthecan.net) as a result of research conducted since 2012 on the history of the canning industry in Portugal. Aiming to preserve the memory and tell the history of Portugal’s national canning industry, the labels displayed on the page offer a magnificent resource of local typography. 🇵🇹

## The effects of acquiring Hoefler&Co have begun to emerge.

*Image: © [Nuclear Bits](https://nuclearbits.com/bye-hoefler-monotype-hello-commissioner/)*

Nuclear Bits [wrote](https://nuclearbits.com/bye-hoefler-monotype-hello-commissioner/) about the impact on their company of Monotype’s acquisition of Hoefler&Co last year. 🇬🇧 Having used Hoefler&Co’s typefaces on its web page, after receiving an email stating that the annual fee of $99 had doubled to $199, the company decided to switch to the typeface [Commissioner](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Commissioner?query=Commissioner+), designed by Kostas Bartsokas and available for free on Google Fonts.

## Calligrapher Yılmaz Özbek Exhibition

*Image: © “[Calligrapher Yılmaz Özbek Exhibition](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/tr-TR/yilmaz-ozbek-kaligrafi-sergisi-52453)”*

The exhibition “[Calligrapher Yılmaz Özbek](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/tr-TR/yilmaz-ozbek-kaligrafi-sergisi-52453)”, consisting of works by the late Yılmaz Özbek—one of the most prominent figures of Turkish Latin calligraphy and a representative of the Barın school—opened on Saturday, February 26, 2022, at the Fatih Municipality Kadırga Art Galleries. Advised by Erhan Olcay and curated by Fatih Ömeroğlu, the exhibition can be visited until March 20, 2022.

## Seal Database launched!

*Image: © [Seal Database](http://muhur.yek.gov.tr)*

Prepared by the Presidency of the Manuscripts Institution of Türkiye, the [database](http://muhur.yek.gov.tr), which aims to collect all clearly legible seals found in manuscript and printed works across their collections and present them to researchers alongside their images and evaluations, has been launched.

## Stink Studios collaborated with Erkin Karamemet on its rebranding.

*Image: © [Stink Studio](https://www.stinkstudios.com/)*

Drawing on early internet aesthetics for its branding work, creative studio [Stink](https://www.stinkstudios.com/) collaborated with [Erkin Karamemet](https://medium.com/tipografi/s%C3%B6yle%C5%9Fi-erkin-karamemet-dda79629a819) from Swiss type foundry [Dinamo](https://abcdinamo.com) for a custom type design composed of symbols ([Wingdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dingbat)). Described by the studio’s design director Steven Olimpio as “a library of brand illustrations masquerading as a font,” the new font is used across the entire identity as a nod to Stink’s digital roots.

## Roboto Serif announced.

*Image: © [Roboto Serif](https://storage.googleapis.com/material-design/downloads/Roboto_Serif_Specimen.pdf)*

Google announced [Roboto Serif](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Serif), a new member of the Roboto type family—originally introduced in 2011 as Android’s system typeface—designed to make reading more comfortable at any size and in any medium. Offering a comfortable and frictionless reading experience, and also featuring a variable font, the type family was developed by [Greg Gazdowicz](https://commercialtype.com/about/greg_gazdowicz) from Commercial Type. The type specimen can be viewed [here](https://storage.googleapis.com/material-design/downloads/Roboto_Serif_Specimen.pdf). 🇬🇧

## Klingspor Museum opens its typography archive to the public.

*Image: © [Klingspor Museum](https://www.offenbach.de//microsite/klingspor_museum/index.php)*

The [Klingspor Museum](https://www.offenbach.de//microsite/klingspor_museum/index.php) is [opening](https://www.slanted.de/beyond-the-archive/) its digitized typography archive to the public on March 11, 2022. As part of the archive events, the museum will also host various events such as a calligraphy workshop by Ethan Cohen and a visit to the historic Gerstenberg Type Foundry. 🇬🇧

## Font Fashion Week begins!

*Image: © [ILT](https://fontfashion.com)*

[ILT](https://fonts.ilovetypography.com) has announced the first [Font Fashion Week](http://fontfashion.com) event, taking place from April 4–8, 2022. This free online event series on the latest trends in international type design features two tracks titled *Font Runway* and *Fonts in Branding*, alongside articles and interviews from participating type foundries.
