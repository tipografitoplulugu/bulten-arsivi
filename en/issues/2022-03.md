## New letter proposal for gender-neutral Spanish

*Image: © [thebrandinquirer](https://thebrandinquirer.wordpress.com/2022/02/06/%EF%BF%BCmexican-type-designer-creates-new-spanish-vowel-for-gender-neutral-language/)*

We had previously [shared](https://twitter.com/search?q=cinsiyet%20from%3Atipografi&src=typed_query&f=live) developments regarding gender bias in language on our social media accounts, and also [discussed](https://apos.to/s/613f3525abd2860006d2c3bb) the role of gender in typography. This time, an attempt to break gender bias in language came from Mexico. Type designer [Aldo Arillo](https://twitter.com/AldoArillo) and artist Mario García Torres [designed](https://thebrandinquirer.wordpress.com/2022/02/06/%EF%BF%BCmexican-type-designer-creates-new-spanish-vowel-for-gender-neutral-language/) a new vowel to turn Spanish into a gender-neutral language. Defined as genderless, the vowel named *secte* was created from a combination of the letters a, e, and o. 🇬🇧

## Designers pioneering the independent type foundry movement

*Image: [Its Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-a-bold-frontier-type-revolution-graphic-design-040122?mc_cid=c3d89d033a&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1) © Studio Akakir, 2021*

As the type industry grows and type foundries are acquired by large retailers, an increase is also observed in the number of small and independent type foundries. ## Safari starts supporting color fonts.

*Image: © [Webkit](https://webkit.org/blog/12445/new-webkit-features-in-safari-15-4/#typography)*

The first WebKit update of 2022 was [announced](https://webkit.org/blog/12445/new-webkit-features-in-safari-15-4/#typography) with over seventy additions. Safari began supporting color fonts in version 15.4. The font-palette feature added to WebKit allows web developers to select different predefined palettes within a color font. 🇬🇧

## Elements of multi-script typography

*Image: © [Design Regression](https://designregression.com/essay/elements-of-multi-script-typography-chapter-2)*

The [second part of the essay](https://designregression.com/essay/elements-of-multi-script-typography-chapter-2) on the elements of multi-script typography, authored by type designer [David Březina](https://www.mrbrezina.com) from the [Rosetta](http://rosettatype.com) type foundry, has been published. In his essay, Březina draws attention to the distinction between designing type for a language and a type design supporting a language, offering detailed information for multi-script type design ranging from encoding to word shapes. 🇬🇧

## Hermes rebrands as Evri in collaboration with Monotype and Superunion.

*Image: © [Its Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/superunion-monotype-hermes-rebrand-graphic-design-180322)*

[Monotype](https://www.monotype.com) and [Superunion](https://www.superunion.com) utilized the capabilities of [OpenType](https://apos.to/s/61819a6ff6b961000610d2c7) for [Evri’s new visual identity](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/superunion-monotype-hermes-rebrand-graphic-design-180322)—the UK's largest dedicated parcel delivery company, formerly known as *Hermes*, which recently changed its name. With a type design featuring different alternates accompanying every character in the alphabet, 194,481 logos can be generated for the company. This means that every vehicle in the [Evri](https://www.evri.com) fleet can carry its own logo. 🇬🇧

## “Lorem Ipsum” exhibition

*Image: © [Lorem Ipsum](https://chita.com.tr/360/lorem-ipsum/)*

Organized by [Odeabank](https://www.odeabank.com.tr/hakkimizda/o-art/program/lorem-ipsum#)’s art platform O’Art and held at Akaretler Row Houses between February 17 and March 17, 2022, the [Lorem Ipsum](https://chita.com.tr/360/lorem-ipsum/) exhibition has opened for online viewing. Curated by Begüm Güney, the exhibition presents two contrasting approaches—where typography either becomes a medium or serves as an end in itself—interpreting its relationship with contemporary art within the object-image-sign nexus. The exhibition features works by Berkay Tuncay, Gülen Eren, Leyla Emadi, Emin Çelik, Merve Ünsal, Merve Ertufan, Nancy Atakan, Huo Rf, and Eylül Ersöz.

## A new book from İş Kültür: “A History of Writing”

*Image: ©[ Türkiye İş Bankası Kültür Yayınları](https://www.iskultur.com.tr/yazinin-tarihi.aspx)*

New Zealand linguist Steven Roger Fischer’s book “[A History of Writing](https://www.iskultur.com.tr/yazinin-tarihi.aspx)” was published by [Türkiye İş Bankası Kültür Yayınları](https://www.iskultur.com.tr) in translation by Handan Konar. In his book, while shedding light on the key milestones of historical development, Fischer thoroughly examines the most widely used writing systems in the world, from Mesopotamia to Greece and China. 🇹🇷

## Gerrit Noordzij has passed away.

*Image: Gerrit Noordzij in the TypeMedia classroom, 2010. Photo: [Tânia Raposo](https://www.flickr.com/photos/ainat6/albums/72157622231581624/with/4467873975/)*

One of the most influential figures in the world of typography over the last forty years, Dutch type designer, author, and educator [Gerrit Noordzij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerrit_Noordzij) (April 2, 1931–March 17, 2022) [passed away](https://typemedia.org/noordzij/noordzijatkabk.html) at the age of 91. Noordzij, who taught type design at the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague between 1960 and 1990, wrote internationally acclaimed books such as “Letterletter”, “The Stroke of the Pen”, and “The Stroke: Theory of Writing”.
