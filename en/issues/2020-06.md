## A preview tool for fonts in different languages: Universal Specimen

*Image: © Universal Specimen*

Designed by [Rosetta Type Foundry](https://www.rosettatype.com), [Universal Specimen](https://universalspecimen.rosettatype.com) facilitates comparison by offering a preview of the selected font in multiple languages before starting typesetting.

## New font families added to the Adobe Fonts library.

*Image: © [Adobe Blog](https://theblog.adobe.com/spring-into-action-with-fresh-fonts/?red=a)*

Adobe [added](https://theblog.adobe.com/spring-into-action-with-fresh-fonts/?red=a) 122 new font families from seven type foundries, including [MVB Fonts](https://www.mvbfonts.com), [American Type Founders](http://www.atftype.com), and [OH No Type Co](https://ohnotype.co), to the [Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com) library.

## Is the font world's apocalypse approaching?

*Image: © Communication Arts*

> “People who work with fonts like I do worry about a slow-motion apocalypse. Everything in the font world is slowing down.” Thomas Phinney

Thomas Phinney, type designer and owner of [Font Detective LLC](https://thefontdetective.com), explains in [an article he wrote for Communication Arts](https://www.commarts.com/columns/fontocalypse-now) how the world of type has changed over the last decade due to free (libre) fonts, subscription services, and globalization. 🇬🇧

## Typographics Typography Festival will take place in July.

*Image: © [Typographics 2020](https://2020.typographics.com/)*

## ATypI Typography Conference will also be held online.

*Image: © [ATypI](https://www.atypi.org/conferences/all-over-2020)*

The [ATypI (Association Typographique Internationale)](https://www.atypi.org/) typography conference has also joined the conferences moving online. Following the [Typographics Typography Festival](https://2020.typographics.com/coronavirus/), which recently announced it would be held online, [ATypI](https://www.atypi.org/) also announced that this year's [conference](https://www.atypi.org/conferences/all-over-2020) will be held online between October 27–31, 2020, due to the global pandemic. The deadline for paper submissions is June 30! The 2021 conference will be held in Paris, and the 2022 conference will be held in Stockholm.

## A checking tool for the -de/-da suffixes

*Image: © [de/-da Takıntısı](http://dedatakintisi.derlem.com/oyun/anasayfa/)*

Developed at Boğaziçi University Department of Computer Engineering, the application offers a checking tool for the spelling of the -de/-da suffixes. The [-de/-da Spelling Error Finder](http://dedatakintisi.derlem.com/deda/bul/) was prepared by [Hasan Öztürk](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hasan-öztürk-6bb759150/) and [Alperen Değirmenci](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alperen-degirmenci/) under the supervision of [Suzan Üsküdarlı](https://www.linkedin.com/in/suzanuskudarli/) and [Onur Güngör](https://www.linkedin.com/in/onurgungor/). Those who want to support the project can register via [this link](https://lnkd.in/d-2vPaq).

## Type Directors Club is closing down.

*Image: © [TDC](https://www.tdc.org)*

Following Juan Villanueva's resignation from the board of directors, the TDC decided to close down. [In the statement made](https://www.tdc.org), it was stated that the TDC faced severe financial difficulties exacerbated by the global pandemic, and that it needed to be reconstituted in a new and more inclusive form under different leadership in the future. The TDC was founded in 1946.
