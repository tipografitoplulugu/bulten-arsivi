# Typography Newsletter: July 2026

### This month's bulletin features typefaces in sports identities and public communications, type heritage efforts carried out by artificial intelligence and human hands, new tools, and archives.

⏰ **Reading Time:** ~6 minutes

Hello everyone!

The July bulletin opens with the question of which letters institutions choose to represent themselves. Spanning from jerseys to official correspondence, this choice turns typography into a declaration of authority. On one hand, there are fabricated visuals generated by artificial intelligence; on the other, an archival approach patiently unearthing forgotten type heritage.

The newly released type families, meanwhile, simultaneously showcase different design rationales and sales models. A broad spectrum—from modular systems to locally inspired revivals, and from long-term brand strategies to new type foundries born out of a reaction to licensing complexity—is explored this month.

---

## Custom Typeface as an Identity Tool from Jerseys to Official Correspondence

The official typeface of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, FWC26, designed by Pangram Pangram, [ensures](https://pimpmytype.com/fifa-2026-font/comment-page-1/#comment-23934) that the tournament carries a consistent, energetic identity across its brand through a narrow and geometric structure. Oliver Schöndorfer, on the other hand, [criticizes](https://pimpmytype.com/fifa-2026-font/) FIFA for continuing to use the same typeface for country abbreviations, even though they replaced FWC26—which suffered from legibility issues—with Noto Sans for the score and timer. This tension between aesthetic identity and functional legibility shows that custom typeface commissions are not always resolved smoothly.

At the club level, similar decisions are shaped by different motivations. Barcelona unveiled [FC Barcelona Modernista](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/news/4528818/fc-barcelona-presents-new-202627-kit-with-unique-show-at-the-macba-museum?utm_source=chatgpt.com), designed with inspiration from Catalan modernism for their 2026–2027 season kit. Referencing Gaudí's visual language with its vertical proportions and organic lines, the typeface will appear only in Champions League and cup matches, while LaLiga's mandatory typeface will continue to be used in league fixtures. The fruits of the Turkish Football Federation (TFF) lifting its mandatory Arial rule are only just emerging in Turkey. The [custom font](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaXoWQlDD01/) designed by Fatih Hardal for Galatasaray blends the early 2000s outlined lettering heritage with today's minimalist approach, drawing reference from the interlocking structure of the club's 1905 crest and the vertical guide lines of the kit numbers.

The search for institutional identity continues off the pitch as well. New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani [adopted](https://www.fastcompany.com/91562875/mayor-mamdani-has-a-new-official-font) Frere-Jones Type's Empirica for official communications. The ongoing collaboration with the campaign designer adds a handcrafted feel inspired by the city's local lettering tradition. It is rare for a candidate to maintain their typographic diligence from the campaign trail after taking office… Ari Zetkin's analysis documenting the 26-year typographic history of the White House website [shows](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/78453/the-typography-of-whitehouse-gov-2000-2026) that this care is the rule rather than the exception. From Bush's three-column interface to Trump's use of Nord Display, every presidential transition has brought its own typeface decision. The selection of a typeface associated with real estate and masculinity can be read through an effort to establish a measured and dignified image of authority.

## Historical Heritage from Artificial Intelligence to Human Hands

*Image: A superimposed view of a page from the Erbar-Grotesk type specimen and a poster by Ludmilla Kavala. © [Letterform Archive](https://letterformarchive.org/news/seen-together-chlorodont/)*

A [discussion](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/comment/71696/#Comment_71696) on TypeDrawers revealed the limits of artificial intelligence's relationship with historical material. When John Hudson asked ChatGPT to generate letterforms from late Carolingian manuscripts, the system drew only familiar forms without regard to the source. When a specific manuscript was requested, both the dates and the letterforms turned out to be incorrect; moreover, fabricated visuals were presented as if they were authentic source documents. The question Hudson asks is crucial: noting that when a human lies, we understand it alongside sin and the possibility of forgiveness, he asks what our responsibility should be toward an entity incapable of repentance.

Human-led archival practice, by contrast, displays the exact opposite rigor. Letterform Archive's new article series *[Seen Together](https://letterformarchive.org/news/seen-together-chlorodont/)* connects objects in the collection across time. Advertising copy from an Erbar-Grotesk type specimen of the 1930s resurfaces twenty years later in a poster where an art student visualizes a toothache. This unexpected continuity forged by a single consumer product between typography, branding, and design education reminds us of the weight that everyday objects carry in design history.

Obtaining consent and staying faithful to the source are also decisive in revival projects. *Churchward Roundsquare*, used in the posters for Yorgos Lanthimos's film Bugonia, is a typeface discovered by designer Vasilis Marmatakis in the archives of the Te Papa Museum and digitized with permission from Joseph Churchward's daughter. The legacy of Churchward, who refused to use computers and hand-drew 690 typefaces, could only be [brought into the present](https://thespinoff.co.nz/pop-culture/15-11-2025/the-samoan-new-zealand-typographer-behind-the-beloved-bugonia-font) through such a process of permission and research. Slovenian designer Alja Herlah's [Signage Rebirth](https://bl.ag/signage-rebirth-reimagining-slovenias-lettering-heritage/) project shares a similar patience. It reinterprets the original signage lettering of twelve closed or altered Slovenian brands from archival sources, juxtaposing the original design with contemporary interpretations.

Sometimes the value of archival material comes from its imperfections. The vintage digital screens and industrial lettering brought together by Marcin Wichary [show](https://unsung.aresluna.org/good-type-against-all-odds/) that technical limitations can yield an unexpectedly distinctive visual language. In our [March 2026 bulletin](https://open.substack.com/pub/tipografi/p/tipografi-bulteni-mart-2026?r=6ck3m2&selection=8ac5c26e-c192-4dad-bc36-a70a64c56e64&utm_campaign=post-share-selection&utm_medium=web&aspectRatio=instagram&textColor=%23ffffff&bgImage=true), we featured the facade of the Obama Presidential Center, which opened on June 18 in Chicago, as a monumental example of this meticulous approach. Pentagram [inscribed](https://www.printmag.com/architecture/typography-has-its-monumental-moment-at-the-obama-presidential-center/) 103 words selected from a speech Obama delivered in 2015 into the building's facade using 1.3-meter-tall Gotham letters. Up close, the physical presence of the letters stands out, while from a distance, the entire composition integrates with the city's skyline.

## Different Design and Marketing Pursuits in New Type Families

*Image: Opet Italic © [Element Type](https://elementtype.co/opet-italic/)*

The common thread among new type families this month is that the rationale behind design decisions is made visible as well. Designed for Strasbourg's UNESCO World Book Capital project and released for free, Azimut transforms the classical weight structure into three independent yet harmonious styles. In this system [reviewed by Dan Reynolds](https://azimut.strasbourg.eu/english.html#DR), uppercase letters are designed with the same width across all three styles; users can create decorative arrangements by switching between styles without needing knowledge of OpenType or kerning. Referencing Gerrit Noordzij's definition of typography as writing with prefabricated letters, Reynolds suggests that Azimut embodies this concept for today's reader.

Sometimes the source of inspiration is not a corporate commission, but the very surfaces of the city. [Olivier Gourvat](https://oliviergourvat.com/) [designed](https://www.printmag.com/type-tuesday/type-tuesday-calenza-proves-the-best-type-museums-are-city-streets/?mc_cid=4343328ef6&mc_eid=48a95a3cf1#utm_source=The+League+of+Moveable+Type) [Calenza](https://www.motyfo.com/calenza/) by studying Valencia's ceramic street plaques in use since 1902. The narrow letterforms produced by sign painters by squeezing long street names form the starting point for the type family's three members: normal, condensed, and compressed. Quirky letterforms rarely encountered in the city were also included in the family.

We value the processes behind typeface designs and make a point of featuring them in our bulletins. Announced by Mass-Driver after a five-year development process, [MD UI](https://mass-driver.com/typefaces/md-ui/) [presents](https://mass-driver.com/article/md-ui-a-typeface-for-interfacing) a striking example of this approach by bringing together 48 static styles across three optical sizes into a single variable font. It is also noteworthy that the type foundry chose to refresh its own visual identity with the new typeface rather than announcing it through a separate promotional campaign. A similar meticulousness is [evident](https://elementtype.co/opet-italic/) in Element Type's collaboration with Opet. Taking the 2004 logo as a starting point, the type foundry redesigned the letters from scratch and expanded the typeface's character set to support over a hundred languages.

Beyond the design process, how type is sold is also a topic of this month's bulletin. Having published typefaces designed for brands on Google Fonts for a long time, Koto established its own type foundry, [CCType](https://cctype.com/), and [made its debut](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/koto-cctype-typography-project-080726) with [CC Timeline](https://cctype.com/typefaces/cc-timeline). Combining British movable type with Swiss-German modernist phototypesetting influences, the family adopts a one-time purchase model instead of traditional licensing. In his [article](https://revistes.uab.cat/grafica/article/view/ep-ertuk) examining the marketing strategies of type foundries, one of our editors, [Murat Ertürk](https://www.muraterturk.com/), had pointed out the industry's tendency to move away from conventional sales models.
