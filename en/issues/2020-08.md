## Introduction to variable fonts

In their [guide article](https://web.dev/variable-fonts/) on variable fonts, [Mustafa Kurtuldu](https://twitter.com/Mustafa_x) and [Thomas Steiner](https://twitter.com/tomayac) from Google’s London office explained the working principles of variable fonts, how type designers create them, and how they work in CSS. 🇬🇧

## Hendrik D. L. Vervliet passed away at age 96.

Belgian book and printing historian [Hendrik D. L. Vervliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._D._L._Vervliet) passed away at the age of 96. Known for his work on 16th-century printing and [Robert Granjon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Granjon), Vervliet authored numerous works, including [The Palaeotypography of the French Renaissance: Selected Papers on Sixteenth-century Typefaces](https://www.amazon.com.tr/Palaeotypography-French-Renaissance-Sixteenth-century-Typefaces/dp/9004169822/ref=sr_1_4?__mk_tr_TR=ÅMÅŽÕÑ&dchild=1&keywords=Hendrik+Vervliet&qid=1596712023&sr=8-4) and [Sixteenth-Century Printing Types of the Low Countries](https://www.amazon.com.tr/Sixteenth-Century-Printing-Types-Low-Countries/dp/9061948592/ref=sr_1_2?__mk_tr_TR=ÅMÅŽÕÑ&dchild=1&keywords=Hendrik+Vervliet&qid=1596712023&sr=8-2).

## How many female type designers do you know?

Prepared by [Yulia Popova](http://yulia-popova.com/) and aiming to shed light on the work of female type designers, the book [How many female type designers do you know?](https://www.onomatopee.net/product/how-many-female-type-designers-do-you-know/) has taken its place among the typography books published in 2020.

The first section of the book features data on research regarding the gender issue in the field of type design, along with biographies of female type designers working in the 19th and 20th centuries, while the second section includes interviews with 14 female type designers.

## A good guide to pairing fonts

In an article published on the web page [Learn UI Design](https://learnui.design/blog/), [Erik Kennedy](http://erikdkennedy.com) prepared a [six-point guide](https://learnui.design/blog/guide-pairing-fonts.html) to help you pair fonts. 🇬🇧

## TDC is restructuring.

Last June, we reported that the Type Directors Club had decided to shut down. TDC has [published an update](https://www.tdc.org/building-community/taking-stock/) regarding the future of the organization and how it will be restructured. 🇬🇧

## Custom font for Hertha Sports Club

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I6Q2owmLpSg&feature=youtu.be)*

Inspired by Berlin’s street art culture, [Martin Wenzel](https://twitter.com/supertype_de) [designed](https://youtu.be/I6Q2owmLpSg) a custom font for Hertha, one of the city's clubs. The Hertha media team has started using the custom-designed font across all channels, including video subtitles.

## New typeface for Toyota: Toyota Type

*Image/Video: [Video Source](https://vimeo.com/442092369)*

Working together with [Saatchi & Saatchi](http://saatchi.com/en-us/), [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/products/studio) produced [a video](https://www.monotype.com/resources/case-studies/making-toyota-type) on how they created [Toyota Type](https://www.toyota.com/brandguidelines/typography/) as part of Toyota's new brand identity. 🇬🇧

## 10 experts critiqued the Biden-Harris logo.

Ten experts, ranging from Obama's design team to Hillary for America’s design director, [critiqued](https://www.fastcompany.com/90539400/experts-weigh-in-on-the-biden-harris-logo-it-could-be-scribbled-on-a-napkin-and-id-be-happy) the new Biden-Harris logo. The campaign, designed by the advertising agency Mekanism and built upon Biden's original branding and logo, was written about for Fast Company by [Lilly Smith](https://twitter.com/lilly_smith48). 🇬🇧

Two typefaces from Hoefler&Co, Decimal and Mercury, were selected for Biden’s campaign. As TTT, we covered this news in the [July Bulletin](https://medium.com/tipografi/tipografi-haberleri-temmuz-2020-f398d60949d7).

## ATypI’s first female president Carolina Laudon

[Carolina Laudon](https://laudon.se/about) from Sweden became the first female president of [ATypI](https://www.atypi.org). Laudon, who won Sweden’s most prestigious typographic design award, the Berling Prize, and has been a board member since 2018, previously served as vice president.

## An article by Ethan Wang on the future of digital typesetting

In his [article published on Medium](https://medium.com/microsoft-design/leading-trim-the-future-of-digital-typesetting-d082d84b202), Ethan Wang focuses on how an emerging CSS standard can solve longstanding problems and raise the bar for web applications. 🇬🇧

---

## A noteworthy article by Silvia Sfligiotti

> “A few large words set to the regular weight of any grotesque font, centered or aligned in the upper-left corner of a page...” Silvia Sfligiotti

In an [article written on Medium](https://medium.com/@silviasfligiotti/this-is-auto-tune-typography-3953e74cc2ac), Silvia Sfligiotti points to an important issue concerning text that is frequently seen in our visual environment, typeset in capital letters and generally with default settings, which she describes as *auto-tune*. 🇬🇧

## A must-read article by Kris Sowersby

Kris Sowersby from [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz) penned an [article](https://klim.co.nz/blog/signifier-design-information/) shedding light on the design process through the type foundry’s new typeface, [Signifier](https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/signifier/). 🇬🇧

## Monotype and House Industries announce collaboration.

Fonts by [House Industries](https://houseind.com) will be [distributed](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200827005280/en/Monotype-House-Industries-Announce-Collaboration-Fonts-Creative) by [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com). Thus, the House Industries library will be made available to users in the coming months through Monotype’s Mosaic application as well as e-commerce channels like MyFonts.com.

---

## Custom typeface for Goldman Sachs: Goldman Sans

International investment bank [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com) has also joined the trend of commissioning custom typefaces, which is becoming increasingly common among corporations. Planned to be rolled out gradually across its website, apps, and even YouTube videos according to marketing needs, [Goldman Sans](https://design.gs.com/d/story/goldman-sans/) was designed by the British type foundry [Dalton Maag](https://www.daltonmaag.com).
