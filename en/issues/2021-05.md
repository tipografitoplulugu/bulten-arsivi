## Microsoft Office’s default typeface is changing.

*Image: © [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2021/04/28/beyond-calibri-finding-microsofts-next-default-font/)*

Microsoft Office is [changing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2021/04/28/beyond-calibri-finding-microsofts-next-default-font/) Calibri, its default typeface of 15 years, and is asking for your help to pick a new one. The five candidates Microsoft has selected to replace Calibri include *Tenorite* by Erin McLaughlin and Wei Huang, Steve Matteson’s contemporary sans-serif typeface *Bierstadt*, John Hudson and Paul Hanslow’s humanist sans-serif *Skeena*, *Seaford* designed by Tobias Frere-Jones and his team, and finally *Grandview* by Aaron Bell. The selected typeface will begin being used in 2022.

## Type and signage design competition from Ankara Metropolitan Municipality

*Image: © [Sokaktaki Ankara](https://sokaktakiankara.ankara.bel.tr)*

Ankara Metropolitan Municipality is organizing a nationwide [type and signage design competition](https://sokaktakiankara.ankara.bel.tr) to be used for neighborhood, avenue, and street signs, building door numbers, and architectural identification plaques across the central and surrounding districts of Ankara province. ## Custom typeface for Pinterest from Grilli Type

*Image: © [Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/commissions/pinterest)*

[Grilli Type](https://www.grillitype.com/) [designed](https://www.grillitype.com/commissions/pinterest) a custom typeface for Pinterest. Commissioned by British design studio [MadeThought](https://www.madethought.com) as part of the brand’s refreshed visual identity, *Pinterest Sans* features a distinctive and recognizable appearance that allows images on the platform to shine. 🇬🇧

[Type Journal](https://typejournal.ru/en/) is an online magazine dedicated to type design, visual culture, and typography in the Russian-speaking world. Bringing together everything related to typography, from legends such as Vadim Lazursky, Solomon Telingater, and Vladimir Favorsky to contemporary designers, the magazine publishes articles and interviews on the history and rapid evolution of typography, as well as news on technology, innovations, and inspiration.

## Can you design a typeface with your very own eyes?

*Image: © [typeroom](https://www.typeroom.eu/can-you-design-a-typeface-with-your-very-own-eyes-julia-schafer-explains)*

In the [Eyes as a Tool](https://www.typeroom.eu/can-you-design-a-typeface-with-your-very-own-eyes-julia-schafer-explains) project at the intersection of biology and type design, carried out by Swiss graphic designer [Julia Schäfer](https://juliaschaefer.ch/) in collaboration with [Haskins Lab](https://haskinslabs.org)—a research institute investigating speech, language, reading, and their biological foundations—she explores whether eye-tracking technology can be used as a tool in scientific environments to generate a typeface simulated from human attention. 🇬🇧

## Design Regression has launched.

*Image: © [designregression.com](https://designregression.com)*

The online journal [Design Regression](https://designregression.com), which publishes design-related writing for reading and reading research, is now live. A collaborative project by a group affiliated with the University of Reading, including David Březina, Mary Dyson, Andrea Churchill Wong, and Matthew Lickiss, the journal is funded by [Rosetta Type Foundry](https://www.rosettatype.com). Stating that it understands reading as a way to decode the world around us, the journal aims to publish articles on the reading process, typography, writing, type design, multilingualism, information design, the social context of a designer, and visual literacy. 🇬🇧

## A new version of MainType has been announced.

*Image: © [MainType 11](https://www.high-logic.com/font-manager/maintype)*

The latest version of [MainType](https://www.high-logic.com/font-manager/maintype), a font management software developed for the Windows operating system, has been announced. Released by [High-Logic](https://www.high-logic.com)—founded in 1997 by Erwin Denissen—and supporting variable fonts and OpenType features, [MainType 11](https://www.high-logic.com/font-manager/maintype) brings Windows users features that make it easy to find, preview, organize, install, and print fonts.
